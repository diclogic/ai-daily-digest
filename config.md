## Discovery Pass (run BEFORE the per-leader searches)
- Run 2-3 open-ended searches first, e.g. "AI news today", "new AI model
  launched", "AI startup unveils", and scan the front pages of TechCrunch
  AI, The Verge AI, and Hacker News for the last 24h
- Reserve at least one "Model & Product Launches" slot for the most
  significant item from a lab or company NOT already covered elsewhere in
  the digest, even on days when OpenAI/Anthropic/Google ship something big
- Launches announced only via blog post, X, or press (no arXiv paper) count
  as launches — do not rely on arXiv alone to surface new models
- If a tracked leader has nothing new, write one line and move on; do not
  spend search budget confirming silence

## Trending Open-Source Projects
- Surface viral/fast-rising AI and agent-related GitHub repos
- Sources: github.com/trending (daily + weekly), GitHub search for recently
  created repos with rapid star growth (e.g. topic:ai-agents, topic:llm,
  topic:agents sorted by stars), and repos generating buzz on HN/X
- Prioritize star velocity (stars gained per day) over absolute star count —
  a 2-week-old repo at 3k stars beats an old repo drifting upward
- 3-5 repos per digest, skip repos already featured in a previous digest
- For each: name + link, one-line description, why it's trending

## Format Preferences
- Concise summaries (1-3 sentences per item)
- Include source links where available
- Quality over quantity
