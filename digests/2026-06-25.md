## Notable Papers

### "NatureBench: Can Coding Agents Match the Published SOTA of Nature-Family Papers?"
Submitted June 23, 2026. A cross-discipline benchmark of 90 tasks from peer-reviewed Nature-family publications, built on NatureGym (containerized per-task environments). Tests whether AI coding agents can achieve scientific discovery, not just reproduction. Under strict web-search-disabled conditions, the best agent configuration surpasses SOTA on only 17.8% of tasks — a sobering result that highlights the gap between current agents and genuine scientific capability. ([arXiv 2606.24530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24530))

### "Quantized Reasoning Models Think They Need to Think Longer, but They Do Not"
Sanae Lotfi et al. Accepted to ICML 2026. Demonstrates that quantized (compressed) reasoning models waste compute by generating unnecessarily long chain-of-thought — they "think" they need more steps but produce equivalent answers with truncated reasoning. Directly actionable for reducing inference costs in deployed reasoning systems.
