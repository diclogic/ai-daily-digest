## Notable Papers & Research

### Selective Activation Sparsity Follow-ups (ICML 2026)

Workshop papers at ICML 2026 continue exploring the selective activation sparsity method presented last week, which teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters per task. Early replications show the technique holds across modalities — vision-language models trained this way now match models 2–3x their size on VQA benchmarks while using 40% fewer FLOPs at inference.

([ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026))

### Intelligence Is Not the Bottleneck (arXiv)

A recent arXiv paper validates an LLM-based first-pass manuscript scoring system against peer-review outcomes, finding that model scores predict accept/reject decisions at rates comparable to individual reviewers. The result raises questions about the scalability of AI-assisted peer review and whether the bottleneck in scientific publishing is evaluation capacity rather than evaluator intelligence.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.15887))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6-Cyber via Daybreak Red

OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber on August 10, a cybersecurity-specific model built on GPT-5.6 Sol and available only through the new Daybreak Red access tier. The model responds to 95% of sensitive security queries (exploit-chain development, authentication bypass, privilege escalation) vs. 1.5% for base Sol — the widest "alignment gap" OpenAI has deliberately opened in a shipping model. Before launch, GPT-5.6-Cyber discovered two zero-day vulnerabilities in Chrome's V8 engine (patched as CVE-2026-15903), five flaws in a major mobile OS, three critical bugs in a popular database, and 400+ kernel privilege-escalation vulnerabilities. Access requires identity verification, legal attestation, and monitoring through Daybreak Red; launch partners reportedly include Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare.

([OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/10/openai-gpt-astra-restrictions-safety-hacking-defenders) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/openai-daybreak-cybersecurity-tiers-gpt-56-cyber-081126) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323884/20260811/gpt-56-cyber-uncovered-chrome-zero-days-standard-ai-wont-touch-alignment-gap-proven.htm))

### Qwen3.8-Max Open Weights — Still Missing

Alibaba promised open weights for Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T params, 95B active, 1M-token context) and Qwen3.8-27B during the week of August 10. That window has now passed with neither appearing on Hugging Face or ModelScope and no license named. The API-only model remains available at $2/$6 per million tokens. This would be the first Max-class model Alibaba has ever open-sourced; the delay is fueling speculation about export-control or licensing complications.

([DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/qwen3-8-max) · [Neomanex](https://neomanex.com/news/qwen38-max-open-weights-countdown-aug-2026) · [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-qwen-38-max24t-and-27b-new))

## Other Notable AI News

### Jeff Dean Exits Google After 27 Years to Found Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals left Google to co-found Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation that uses AI to automate scientific research — proposing experiments, running them, evaluating results, and iterating thousands of times over. Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures co-lead the round (size undisclosed); Alphabet is investing and providing compute for at least a year. Dean is CEO. This is the most significant Google Brain/DeepMind talent departure since the unit's founding. Alphabet stock fell ~5% on the news.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/google-grapples-with-exit-of-ai-pioneer-and-most-google-person) · [Radical Ventures](https://radical.vc/our-investment-in-discovery-loop/))

### EU AI Transparency Rules Take Effect

New EU AI Act transparency obligations took effect August 2, requiring disclosure when users interact with AI or are exposed to AI-generated content. Separately, the July 2026 EU action plan on Cybersecurity and AI sets out coordinated measures for Member States to address risks from advanced models.

([European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-transparent-ai-2026-08-02_en))

### White House Finalizes AI Oversight Framework

The administration finalized its AI oversight framework, granting government early access to the most advanced models before public release. This follows months of negotiations with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta. The framework also committed $5B+ across 15+ federal agencies for AI-driven science (the "Genesis Mission").

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/altman-white-house-wiles-ai-framework.html) · [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/54941/2026-08-07-ai-washington-report-august-2026-edition))
