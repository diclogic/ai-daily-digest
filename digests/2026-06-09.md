## Notable Papers & Research

- **"Teaching the Way, Not the Answer: Privileged Tutoring Distillation for Multimodal Policy Optimization"** — Shizhe Xiang et al. (June 8). Introduces a distillation method where a privileged "tutor" model teaches a student model reasoning strategies rather than copying answers, achieving state-of-the-art on multimodal policy benchmarks. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent))

- **"PaperFlow: Profiling, Recommending, and Adapting Across Daily Paper Streams"** — Fuqiang Wang et al. (June 8). An agentic system that profiles a researcher's interests from their publication history, recommends relevant new arXiv papers daily, and adapts recommendations based on feedback — a practical tool for staying current. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent))

- **"AXIOM: A Trust-First Neuro-Symbolic Execution Architecture for Verifiable Mathematical Reasoning"** (June 2026). Combines neural language models with symbolic verification to produce mathematical proofs that can be formally checked, addressing the trustworthiness gap in LLM-generated reasoning. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

- **AMI Labs LeJEPA Preprints** — Two papers from Yann LeCun's research circle (late May) provide the first rigorous public evidence on whether his "world models" thesis is on track. One proves the LeJEPA architecture can recover true hidden structure of an environment under specific conditions; the companion benchmark identifies robustness failures that must be solved. ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317928/20260606/yann-lecun-world-models-bet-ami-labs-stakes-103-billion-against-large-language-models.htm))

### Colorado AI Act Enforcement Postponed to 2027

The June 30, 2026 enforcement deadline for Colorado's AI Act has been pushed to January 1, 2027 after Governor Polis signed SB 26-189 in May. The revised law replaces the original comprehensive risk-management framework with a narrower notice-and-transparency model, removing risk management programs and impact assessments. A federal judge also stayed enforcement in April. ([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/colorado-ai-act-amended-and-effective-date-delayed))

### Microsoft Phi-4 Reasoning Models

Microsoft recently unveiled Phi-4-reasoning, Phi-4-reasoning-plus, and Phi-4-mini-reasoning — small language models trained for inference-time scaling on complex tasks. Despite their small size, they outperform OpenAI o1-mini and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B on most benchmarks, and beat the full DeepSeek-R1 (671B params) on AIME 2025. Also launched: MAI-Code-1-Flash for code generation. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html))
