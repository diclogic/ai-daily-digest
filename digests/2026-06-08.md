Reduces gradient variance by 77.4% on average and improves pass@256 performance by up to 5.2% on math reasoning benchmarks — a practical advance for training LLMs with reward-based optimization. ## Other Notable AI News

### Trump Signs AI Executive Order — Voluntary Frontier Model Review

On June 2, President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," establishing a voluntary framework where AI developers can submit frontier models to the government for up to 30 days before releasing them to partners. NSA, CISA, and NIST have 60 days to design the benchmarking process. Explicitly does not create a mandatory licensing or permitting requirement. Sam Altman met with Congressional leaders on June 3 to discuss the order. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/trump-executive-order-ai.html) | [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/trumps-new-ai-executive-order-drastically-shifts-the-administrations-stance-on-the-tech/))

### Sanders Proposes 50% Public Stake in AI Companies

Senator Bernie Sanders announced the American AI Sovereign Wealth Fund Act, proposing a one-time 50% stock tax on frontier AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI), payable in shares deposited into a federal fund that would give the public voting rights, board representation, and eventual dividend distributions. The bill has drawn sharp criticism from industry and libertarian outlets. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/03/bernie-sanders-ai-ownership-sovereign-wealth-fund-electrification/) | [Sanders.senate.gov](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/op-eds/the-public-should-own-half-of-the-big-a-i-companies/))
