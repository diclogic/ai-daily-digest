## Notable Papers & Research

- **"Probe Before You Edit: Probing-Guided Molecular Optimization for LLM Agents in Structure-Based Drug Design"** — Zaifei Yang et al. (May 30). Exposes a failure mode where LLM agents edit molecules blindly; proposes PROBE, a framework inspired by medicinal chemists that tests pocket-ligand responses before committing to an optimization direction, achieving joint improvement in both binding affinity and druggability. ([arXiv:2606.00555](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00555))

- **"Emergent Collaborative Deliberation in Multi-Model AI Systems: A BFT-Derived Protocol for Epistemic Synthesis"** (June 2026). Introduces a Byzantine fault tolerance-inspired protocol for multi-agent AI deliberation, showing that structured disagreement among LLMs produces more reliable epistemic outcomes than single-model or naive ensemble approaches. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

- **"EnergyMamba: An Uncertainty-Aware Graph-Enhanced Selective State Space Model for Energy Consumption Prediction"** — Dahai Yu et al. (May 30). Combines graph neural networks with Mamba state-space models and conformal prediction for energy forecasting that captures spatial grid dependencies. Achieves ~5% accuracy and ~6% uncertainty-quantification improvements over 15 baselines. Accepted at KDD '26. ([arXiv:2606.00506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00506))

- **"G-DRAGON: Geospatial Reasoning and Dynamic Planning for Retrieval-Augmented Outdoor Navigation"** (June 2026). A retrieval-augmented framework combining long-range geospatial planning with fine-grained "last-mile" exploration for outdoor robot navigation — relevant as OpenAI and others push into physical AI. ([arXiv](https://arxivtldr.org/weekly))

## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Fable 5 — Mythos Goes Public (June 9)

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, the public version of its Mythos architecture. This is Anthropic's most capable model ever made generally available: state-of-the-art on nearly all tested benchmarks across software engineering, knowledge work, vision, and scientific research. Stripe reported it compressed months of engineering into days, performing a codebase-wide migration of a 50-million-line Ruby codebase in one day. Key specs: 1M-token context window, up to 128k output tokens per request, priced at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. Available on Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Free for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise subscribers through June 22. Safety guardrails block responses in cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, and distillation domains, falling back to Claude Opus 4.8 for those queries. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-mythos-claude-fable-5.html) | [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318082/20260609/anthropic-launches-claude-fable-5-most-powerful-public-model-gated-safeguards.htm))

### Andrej Karpathy — Jevons Paradox for Software

Karpathy, now three weeks into his Anthropic pre-training role, broke his public silence with an observation shared via Simon Willison's blog: as working software becomes increasingly easy to produce, Jevons paradox kicks in — his own demand for software is growing, not shrinking. "You can now ask for anything — explainers, visualizers, dashboards, bespoke single-use apps." The implication: AI coding tools won't reduce demand for engineering, they'll explode it. ### US-Japan $1B "Genesis Mission" AI Partnership
