# AI Daily Digest — July 10, 2026

**Top story:** OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family goes fully public alongside ChatGPT Work, a new agentic workspace that connects to Slack, Gmail, and CRMs to complete multi-step business tasks — marking OpenAI's most aggressive push into enterprise automation yet.

## Model & Product Launches

### GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna — Fully Public + ChatGPT Work

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 model family is now available to all developers and enterprises worldwide, ending the 12-day government-gated preview. Alongside the model launch, OpenAI introduced **ChatGPT Work**, an agentic workspace that goes beyond single-prompt chat. Users describe an objective — "prepare a quarterly business report," "research competitors," "build a financial spreadsheet" — and ChatGPT Work creates a plan, connects to workplace tools (Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, CRMs, internal knowledge bases), and executes multi-step workflows with minimal guidance. On desktop, it can interact with approved local files and applications; a built-in browser enables web-based workflows. Rolling out today to Pro, Enterprise, and Edu subscribers; Plus and Business users follow in coming days. Sol pricing: $5/$30 per million input/output tokens; Terra at $2.50/$15; Luna at $1/$6.

### Cognition SWE-1.7 — RL-on-RL Coding Model

Cognition launched SWE-1.7 on July 8, its most capable coding model, trained via reinforcement learning on top of Kimi K2.7 Code (itself already heavily RL-trained). The "RL-on-RL" approach produced a fourfold jump from its predecessor: SWE-1.6 scored 9.4% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main; SWE-1.7 hits 42.3%. It also scores 81.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 77.8% on SWE-Bench Multilingual, at 1,000 tokens/sec via Cerebras. Available exclusively through Devin (Web, Desktop, CLI) — no standalone API. 256K context window.

([Cognition](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-1-7) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320021/20260709/cognition-swe-17-rl-top-rl-yields-near-frontier-code-low-cost.htm) · [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/07/09/cognition-swe-17-adds-near-frontier-coding-scores-to-devin-xcxwbn/))

### Claude Fable 5 — Access Window Closing July 12

### Sam Altman — "54% More Token Efficient"

In a CNBC interview on July 9, Altman made his most detailed public case for GPT-5.6: "5.6 Sol is not only the best model in the world for most people — it's also much more efficient than other models out in the world." He claimed GPT-5.6 is "54 percent more token efficient on agentic coding tasks" compared to rivals. On the government review process: "I thought it was a very productive process. We had a very good time working with Secretary Lutnick, Secretary Bessent, Director Karen Cross." He noted the government's technical capability was impressive and that officials "really care about getting these models out quickly, but with a high level of safety." On OpenAI's potential IPO: "I don't know." On the reported 5% government stake proposal: "a lot of inaccuracies."

### Yann LeCun — RAISE Summit: "A Toddler Beats ChatGPT"

At RAISE Summit in Paris, LeCun expanded on his world-models thesis: "All the text on the internet adds up to roughly 10^14 bytes. A person would need about 400,000 years to read it. A four-year-old child, through vision alone, has already taken in about the same amount." His new venture AMI Labs ($1.03B raised, $3.5B valuation) is building JEPA-based world models that learn abstract representations of scenes rather than generating every pixel — targeting industrial applications like factory and engine control. He also pitched **Tapestry**, a plan for an open foundation model that multiple countries could train together without sharing data.

## Other Notable AI News

### GPT-5.6 Release Tests the White House Voluntary Framework

The 12-day government review of GPT-5.6 before public release functioned as the first real-world test of the Trump administration's voluntary AI pre-release framework. OpenAI said it made "many changes" during the process. The August 1 deadline for NSA and CISA to deliver classified frontier model benchmarks and formalize the review framework still stands. The central sticking point remains the threshold at which a model triggers review — labs want it to apply only to truly frontier systems, while government officials push for a lower bar.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319979/20260709/gpt-56-goes-public-after-12-day-white-house-gate-tests-voluntary-ai-framework.htm) · [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/54941/2026-07-08-ai-washington-report-july-2026-edition) · [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/900af3cf/executive-order-establishes-voluntary-early-access-framework-to-frontier-ai-models))

### Z.ai GLM-5.2 Fuels US-China AI Debate

Zhipu AI's GLM-5.2 continues to draw Western interest as an open-weight model with coding and agent capabilities close to leading U.S. models at roughly one-sixth the cost. It now ranks above Anthropic's models on OpenRouter and sits fifth on the Artificial Analysis intelligence leaderboard. On long coding-agent trajectories, Z.ai claims it trails Opus 4.8 by just 1% while edging past GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.7. The 1M-token context window model has no regional limits — released days after the U.S. export-control action on Fable 5 and Mythos 5.

### HHS Deploys ChatGPT for Medicaid Fraud Detection

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services is using ChatGPT and other AI tools to scan at least five years of audit history across all 50 states under the **AERO** (Audit Enforcement and Risk Oversight) program. Initial analysis found that some states have failed to remedy internal control issues for 3-5+ years, and hundreds of grantees haven't submitted required audits. Enforcement consequences range from temporary payment holds to permanent debarment. The program is led by Assistant Secretary Gustav Chiarello.

([Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hhs-ai-fraud-state-audit-aero/820989/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319108/20260625/hhs-deploys-chatgpt-medicaid-audits-no-error-rate-no-appeals-no-deadline.htm))
