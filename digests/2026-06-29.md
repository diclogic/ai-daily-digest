## Model & Product Launches

### Zhipu AI GLM-5.2 — Open-Weight Model Matches Mythos on Security Benchmarks

Zhipu AI, one of China's leading AI labs, released GLM-5.2, which independent benchmarks show matches Claude Mythos on cybersecurity tasks. Semgrep benchmarked GLM-5.2 on IDOR (Insecure Direct Object Reference) detection, finding it scored 39% F1 — beating Claude Code's 32% — while Graphistry's independent CyBT-CTF evaluation confirmed it matches Opus 4.8 on cybersecurity investigation tasks. Unlike Mythos, GLM-5.2 is open-weight under an MIT license: anyone can download, fine-tune, or strip safety controls from it. The timing is pointed: the US government export-controlled Mythos and Fable on national security grounds, but an open-weight Chinese model now offers comparable capability with zero restrictions.

([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/chinese-ai-lab-says-it-can-match-anthropics-all-poweful-claude-mythos-at-sniffing-security-bugs/) · [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/06/28/1922225/chinas-ai-matches-anthropic-in-cybersecurity-causing-worry-over-us-restrictions) · [explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/zhipu-ai-matches-claude-mythos-security-bugs-chinese-ai-2026))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump AI Executive Order — July 2 Deadline in 3 Days

The June 2 Executive Order's first 30-day deadline arrives Wednesday. Federal agencies must: prioritize AI-enabled cyber defense, release binding operational directives for civilian IT, and form an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" with industry. The 60-day deadline (August 1) requires a voluntary framework for pre-release federal access to frontier models — the mechanism now governing both GPT-5.6 and Mythos releases.

([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/assessing-trumps-executive-order-on-ai-oversight))

### Qualcomm in Talks to Acquire Tenstorrent for $8–10B

Qualcomm is in advanced talks to acquire Jim Keller's Tenstorrent, the RISC-V AI chip startup, for $8–10 billion — roughly 3x its previous $3.2B valuation. Tenstorrent's Blackhole chip (GA April 28) packs 768 RISC-V cores per accelerator. The deal would give Qualcomm a credible data center AI play and deep RISC-V expertise to challenge Nvidia's dominance. Neither company has commented; no guarantee of completion.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319017/20260624/qualcomm-bets-14-billion-cracking-nvidias-ai-monopoly-risc-v-open-compiler.htm) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/06/16/qualcomm-said-to-be-circling-ai-chip-biz-tenstorrent-in-10b-risc-v-power-play/5256084) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/qualcomm-targets-tenstorrent-in-reported-10b-risc-v-deal))

### Colorado AI Act Delayed to January 2027

Governor Polis signed SB 189 on May 14, replacing the original Colorado AI Act (which was set to take effect June 30 — tomorrow) with a substantially narrower law focused on disclosure and transparency rather than the original risk-based framework. The new version drops the duty of care for algorithmic discrimination, deployer impact assessments, and AG reporting obligations. New effective date: January 1, 2027.

([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/colorado-ai-act-amended-and-effective-date-delayed) · [Littler](https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/colorado-amends-its-artificial-intelligence-law-substantially-reducing) · [Carpe Datum](https://www.carpedatumlaw.com/2026/05/colorados-ai-reset-two-weeks-a-white-house-callout-and-a-pivot-away-from-the-eu-model/))
