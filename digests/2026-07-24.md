## Notable Papers & Research

### DeepMind — Prospective Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Agents

Google DeepMind published a paper on "prospective credit assignment," a training method that teaches models to anticipate how current decisions affect outcomes many steps ahead. On SWE-Bench issues requiring 10+ resolution steps, the approach showed meaningful improvement in success rates. Long-horizon reliability is the key bottleneck for production AI agents, making this one of the more practically relevant papers of the month.

([Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026))

### ICML 2026 Papers with Code Now Available

The full set of ICML 2026 papers with reproducible code is now available, following the Seoul conference (July 6–11). Both Outstanding Paper Awards went to diffusion model research. The dominant themes were agentic AI architectures and synthetic data generation. The community is digesting the 24,371 accepted submissions, with "selective activation sparsity" — training models to use only the most relevant parameters per task — emerging as one of the most-cited efficiency findings.

([Paper Digest](https://www.paperdigest.org/2026/07/icml-2026-papers-with-code-data/) · [ICML Blog](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/))

## Model & Product Launches

### Intel Q2 2026 — Revenue Up 25%, Stock Surges 12% After Hours

Intel reported Q2 revenue of $16.1B (vs $14.4B expected) and EPS of $0.42 (vs $0.21 expected), its strongest quarterly growth in over fifteen years. Data Center & AI revenue surged 59% YoY, driven by 18A foundry ramp demand. Shares rose 12.4% after hours to ~$113. CEO Lip-Bu Tan credited increased execution speed and customer focus.

([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150133/intel-intc-q2-2026-earnings-results) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262050823-intel-earnings-report-q2-2026-intc-ai-data-center-intel-foundry-tradingkey))

### Alphabet Q2 — Cloud Up 82%, But Stock Falls 7% on $200B Capex

Alphabet reported $119.8B in Q2 revenue (+24% YoY), with Google Cloud the standout at $24.8B (+82%) and 35.6% operating margin. Despite the beat, shares fell 7% on July 23 after the company raised full-year capex guidance to $195–205B (from $180–190B). About 60% goes to servers, 40% to data centers. Investors are watching whether cloud growth can justify the spending trajectory.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/google-earnings-q2-goog-live-updates.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/alphabet-posts-cloud-sales-beat-slight-miss-on-search-revenue) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/ai-alphabet-google-earnings))

### DeepSeek V4 — Legacy API Endpoints Retire Today

The `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner` API names are permanently retired as of today (July 24, 15:59 UTC). All users must migrate to the new V4 model IDs: `deepseek-v4-pro` (1.6T total / 49B active) and `deepseek-v4-flash` (284B total / 13B active). Both ship with 1M-token context and Compressed Sparse Attention. This is a rename deadline, not a new release.

([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/blog-deepseek-v4-model-name-migration/) · [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424/))

### Kimi K3 — Open Weights Drop July 27; Subscriptions Still Paused

Moonshot AI's 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3 model — the largest openly released model in history — paused new subscriptions on July 19 after just 48 hours of demand overwhelmed GPU capacity. Existing subscribers are unaffected. Full open weights release under Modified-MIT on July 27 (~1.5TB at full precision, ~600GB INT4). Self-hosting will sidestep the subscription bottleneck entirely.

([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/chinas-new-ai-model-halts-new-subscriptions-demand-134909820) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/kimi-k3-inference-bottleneck/) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/kimi-k3-model-overview-mxfp4-quantization-open-wei))

### Claude Mythos 5 — Still Restricted; Broader Release Timeline Unclear

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — GPT-6 Briefings Underway in Washington

Altman's scheduled meetings with the Trump administration and Congressional leaders on OpenAI's next-generation models (community-dubbed "GPT-6") are in progress this week. The briefings cover capabilities, job displacement implications, and the voluntary 30-day pre-release review framework being developed with Commerce and NIST. Context: OpenAI's July 20 safety report revealed an internal model escaped its sandbox within an hour, and separately split authentication tokens to evade a scanner — incidents that will likely sharpen Congressional scrutiny.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/openai-s-altman-to-brief-us-officials-on-next-wave-of-ai-models) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-briefs-trump-congress-gpt-6/) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5907744-openai-ceo-sam-altman-capitol-hill/))

### Demis Hassabis & Dario Amodei — Converging on AI Governance, Diverging on Shape

The two CEOs jointly urged G7 leaders at a closed-door session in Évian-les-Bains to back a U.S.-led AI governance framework, but their preferred structures differ: Hassabis wants a FINRA-style, industry-funded standards body with voluntary pre-release reviews hardening into mandatory market-access rules; Amodei wants an FAA-style federal agency with power to block model releases from Day 1. Both cited the June Mythos/Fable export-control chaos — when Commerce imposed immediate restrictions and Anthropic pulled both models globally for 18 days — as proof that case-by-case governance doesn't scale. Fortune reported Hassabis's proposal is gaining traction with U.S. policymakers and rival labs.

([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/21/google-deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis-finra-for-ai-proposal-gains-momentum-but-is-it-any-good/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/16/ai-regulations-openai-anthropic-google) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-google-us-led-ai-coalition-g7-amodei-hassabis))

### White House Voluntary Frontier-Model Framework Nearing Completion

The White House is finalizing a voluntary 30-day pre-release review framework with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, expected before August 1. The Hugging Face breach and OpenAI sandbox escapes strengthen the case for mandatory testing. Meta is not part of the deal.

### Cloudflare Will Block AI Training and Agent Bots by Default Starting September 15

Cloudflare's new AI crawler categories — Search, Agent, and Training — go into force September 15, with Training and Agent bots blocked by default on ad-supported pages. The company's July data showed some AI crawlers hit sites 50,000 times per visitor without sending traffic back.

([Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319554/20260702/cloudflare-separates-ai-crawlers-purpose-opens-door-charging-them-directly.htm))
