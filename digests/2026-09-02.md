## Notable Papers & Research

### Normalized Low-Rank Adaptation (NoRA)
*Jiale Kang, Ziyin Yue, Zheng Zhan, Yangyi Huang, Weiyang Liu — arXiv:2608.31036, August 31, 2026*

Introduces NoRA, which normalizes LoRA's down-projection matrices during training. Consistently accelerates convergence, improves stability, and mitigates catastrophic forgetting across pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning — with zero additional parameters or inference cost.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31036))

### Scaling Large Reasoning Models beyond Human Supervision: A Path toward Superintelligence
*Zhiqin Yang, Jingwen Fu et al. — arXiv:2608.31075, August 31, 2026*

Surveys how large reasoning models can keep improving as human supervision recedes from the learning loop. Catalogs the failure modes — reward hacking, feedback drift, curriculum collapse, environment errors — and proposes evaluation dimensions around policy capability, feedback fidelity, and experience quality. A roadmap paper for the post-RLHF era.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31075))

### Does On-Policy Distillation Really Distill? From Noisy Teacher to Self-Improvement
*Yi Ding, Ruqi Zhang*

Analyzes on-policy distillation for LLMs and introduces On-Policy Self-Adaptation (OPSA), a supervision-free method that improves reasoning without requiring a clean teacher signal — relevant as labs push toward self-improving systems.

([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending))

## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 — Live Now
*Anthropic, September 1, 2026*

Anthropic's biggest model drop since Opus 5. Fable 5.1 (generally available) and Mythos 5.1 (invite-only via Project Glasswing) share the same architecture but run under different safeguard tiers. Key changes:

- **Performance**: Biggest gains in agentic coding, long-running workflows, front-end/visual code generation, and finance/analysis tasks. Scores 52.6% on TerminalBench Science.
- **Pricing**: $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. Cache reads drop 75% to $0.25/MTok, cutting typical workloads ~25% and highly agentic workloads up to ~45%.
- **Context**: 1M token window, 128K max output.
- **Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)**: New security architecture for enterprise deployments, details still emerging.
- **Mythos 5.1**: Gains in cybersecurity and biology. Available only to vetted cyberdefenders and life scientists through Anthropic's trusted access programs.

([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable) · [Marktechpost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/01/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-5-1-and-claude-mythos-5-1-52-6-on-terminal-bench-science-and-75-cheaper-cache-reads/) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-claude-fable-51-mythos-51-launch/))

## Other Notable AI News

### Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic for Copyright Infringement
*Filed August 29, 2026 — Northern District of California*

The publishers allege Anthropic used ~20,000 copyrighted songs — including works by The Beatles, Taylor Swift, and Michael Jackson — without licensing to train Claude. They claim the AI reproduces lyrics and generates derivative compositions that compete commercially with human songwriters. Seeking up to $150,000 per willful infringement (potentially billions total) plus an injunction against maintaining unlicensed music in training data. Anthropic invokes fair use. The case lands days before Anthropic's IPO prospectus is expected to go public.

([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/31/sony-warner-music-sue-anthropic-saying-it-pirated-songs-to-train-its-ai) · [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/sony-music-publishing-warner-chappell-anthropic-lawsuit-1236847442/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2246997/sony-warner-sue-anthropic-for-blatant-violation-of-copyright-law/))
