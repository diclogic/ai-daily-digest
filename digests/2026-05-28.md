## Notable Papers & Research

**LLMs as Noisy Channels: A Shannon Perspective on Model Capacity and Scaling Laws**
*ByteDance Seed, University of Virginia, UC Berkeley (May 21, 2026)*
Introduces the "Shannon Scaling Law," a theoretical framework that models LLM training as information transmission over a noisy channel. By mapping model parameters to channel bandwidth and training tokens to signal power, the framework reveals a fundamental Shannon capacity for LLMs: scaling model size or data without preserving sufficient signal-to-noise ratio inevitably amplifies noise, inducing U-shaped performance degradation. Consistently outperforms classical scaling laws across Pythia and OLMo2 experiments.
[arXiv 2605.23901](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23901)

**MiniMax-M2: Compact MoE Models Built for Agentic Workflows**
*MiniMax (May 2026)*
MiniMax open-sourced MiniMax-M2, a Mixture-of-Experts model with 230B total parameters and ~10B active parameters, purpose-built for coding and agentic tasks. Plans and executes complex, long-horizon toolchains across shell, browser, retrieval, and code runners. The follow-up M2.7 variant demonstrated early signs of self-evolution — updating its own memory and building complex skills in its harness during RL experiments.
[GitHub](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-M2) · [MiniMax announcement](https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m2)

## Model & Product Launches

**Claude Managed Agents: Self-Hosted Sandboxes and MCP Tunnels (May 27)**
Anthropic shipped two features for Claude Managed Agents. **Self-hosted sandboxes** (public beta) let companies run agent tool execution on their own infrastructure while the orchestration loop stays on Anthropic's side — with managed providers Cloudflare, Daytona, Modal, and Vercel available for those who don't want to self-host. **MCP tunnels** (research preview) connect agents to MCP servers on private networks via end-to-end encrypted outbound connections, with no inbound firewall rules or public endpoints required.

## Key Opinions & Statements

**Dario Amodei & Sam Altman — Both Walk Back AI Job Predictions (May 26)**
In a striking convergence, both the Anthropic and OpenAI CEOs publicly retreated from their previous warnings about AI-driven job losses within days of each other. Altman told a Sydney conference he's "delighted to be wrong" about the jobs apocalypse and that "people care about interacting with actual people." Amodei, who spent much of 2025 warning that AI could eliminate half of entry-level white-collar work, now invokes the Jevons Paradox — the 19th-century observation that efficiency gains expand demand rather than contract it. Fortune notes the timing is conspicuous: both companies are pursuing valuations above $850B, and apocalyptic job-loss narratives aren't exactly IPO-friendly.
[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/) · [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/sam-altman-wrong-ai-could-lead-to-a-jobs-apocalypse) · [Fortune (Amodei)](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/dario-amodei-jevons-paradox-will-ai-wipe-out-white-collar-jobs/)

**Yann LeCun — "Everyone Is Going to Be a Boss" (May 4–5)**
The AMI Labs founder continued his campaign against AI doom narratives, predicting a future where "everyone is going to be a boss" — managing AI agents instead of people. LeCun argued that historically it takes new technologies 15 years to achieve productivity gains, and that AI is "just another set of tools that makes us more efficient," with nothing "qualitatively different" about this technological revolution.
[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/ai-job-apocalypse-warnings-destructive-yann-lecun/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/04/ai-godfather-survival-guide-hype-doom)

## Other Notable AI News

**China Expands Travel Restrictions to Private-Sector AI Talent (May 26)**
Bloomberg reported that China has begun imposing overseas travel restrictions on AI researchers, executives, and startup founders at private companies including DeepSeek and Alibaba, requiring approval from "relevant authorities" before traveling abroad. Previously limited to state-affiliated individuals (university researchers, SOE executives), extending these protocols to the private sector marks a major policy shift. Individuals are added to restricted lists based on the "strategic value" of their research. The restrictions were first applied quietly to some DeepSeek executives in December 2025 before being broadened in recent months.
[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-26/china-expands-travel-curbs-to-top-ai-talent-at-private-firms) · [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/05/27/tech/china-travel-curbs-ai-firms/)

**Anthropic Opens Seoul Office (May 26)**
Anthropic appointed KiYoung Choi as Representative Director of Korea ahead of a Seoul office opening, continuing its international expansion alongside its recent KPMG alliance (276,000 employees across 138 countries) and Gates Foundation partnership ($200M, four years).

**Canada Rules OpenAI Violated Privacy Laws (May 6–7)**
Four Canadian privacy commissioners found that OpenAI violated national privacy law by using publicly accessible internet data to train ChatGPT without valid consent, including potentially sensitive information like health conditions, political views, and information about children. The investigation remains "conditionally resolved" federally and unresolved in some provinces, with OpenAI required to implement enhanced privacy protections.
[CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/privacy-investigation-chatgpt-open-ai-9.7188538) · [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/federal-and-provincial-privacy-watchdogs-say-openai-violated-canadian-privacy-laws/)
