## Notable Papers & Research

**ConvexTok: Optimal Tokenization via Integer Programming Relaxation**
*ETH Zurich & Kensho Technologies (May 21, 2026)*
Proposes a tokenization algorithm that formulates the problem as an Integer Program relaxed into a Linear Program, achieving compression rates within 1% of the provable theoretical optimum. Outperforms BPE at larger vocabulary sizes.
[arXiv cs.AI listings](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)

**TurboQuant (Google, ICLR 2026)**
A new algorithm that significantly reduces memory overhead from KV caches — one of the biggest bottlenecks in large-model inference — enabling more efficient deployment of frontier models.

**LLM-Emu: Profile-Driven LLM Inference Emulator**
*University of Cambridge*
An online emulator for LLM inference that uses profile-driven latency sampling to simulate LLM behavior with less than 5% absolute error in wall-clock time — useful for capacity planning and systems research.

**AI vs. Human Creativity Study**
A large-scale study comparing over 100,000 people against frontier AI systems finds that generative AI now beats the average human on certain creativity tests, though top human performers still excel.

## Key Opinions & Statements

**Pope Leo XIV — *Magnifica Humanitas* Encyclical (May 25)**
The first papal encyclical on artificial intelligence was released yesterday, co-presented by Anthropic co-founder **Chris Olah** at the Vatican — an unprecedented event where a pope personally presented an encyclical. The document argues for "safeguarding the human person in the time of artificial intelligence," warning that AI has "even greater consequences than the industrial revolution" while avoiding both techno-utopianism and blanket rejection. Olah acknowledged that "frontier AI labs operate inside incentives and constraints that can sometimes conflict with doing the right thing."
[Anthropic: Chris Olah's remarks](https://www.anthropic.com/news/chris-olah-pope-leo-encyclical) · [CNN coverage](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/25/europe/pope-leo-ai-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas-intl)

**Demis Hassabis — "Foothills of the Singularity" (Google I/O, May 20)**
The Google DeepMind CEO declared "We're at the foothills of the singularity" during the I/O keynote, estimating AGI arrival by 2030 "plus or minus a year." He emphasized AI as "the most important tool for scientists" and highlighted WeatherNext's advance warning of Hurricane Melissa's landfall in Jamaica. Hassabis compared AI's impact to "10 times the industrial revolution, maybe 10 times faster."
[Semafor interview](https://www.semafor.com/article/05/20/2026/google-exec-demis-hassabis-predicts-were-at-the-foothills-of-the-singularity) · [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91544235/demis-hassabis-google-io-2026)
