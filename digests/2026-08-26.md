## Notable Papers & Research

### How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently

Penghui Qi, Xiangxin Zhou, and Wee Sun Lee present Best-Practice Critic Optimization (BPCO), a recipe for stable critic training in reinforcement learning for LLMs. Across models from 1.5B to 30B-A3B MoE, BPCO matches or exceeds group-based baselines (like GRPO) while sampling only one response per prompt — a meaningful efficiency gain for RLHF pipelines.

([arXiv 2608.23566](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566))

### CrystalMem: Elastic Memory for Self-Evolving LLM Agents via Knowledge Crystallization

Beining Wu and Jun Huang propose a memory architecture that lets LLM agents compress, reorganize, and selectively retrieve accumulated knowledge — addressing the growing problem of context overflow in long-running agent sessions.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### Can LLM Agents Price Competitively?

A new study examines whether LLM-based agents can learn competitive pricing strategies, finding they converge toward collusive outcomes even without explicit coordination — raising antitrust questions as AI agents increasingly handle business decisions.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI: o3 Retired from ChatGPT, Assistants API Shuts Down

Two major OpenAI deprecations take effect today:
- **o3 retirement**: After a 90-day sunset, o3 is removed from ChatGPT (API access continues through December). Existing conversations will auto-migrate to GPT-5 models.
- **Assistants API shutdown**: All `/v1/assistants`, `/v1/threads`, and `/v1/threads/runs` endpoints return hard errors starting today — no grace period, no degraded mode. OpenAI's replacement is the Responses API + Conversations API. Crucially, there is no automated thread migration tool; developers must manually export conversation history.

([OpenAI Model Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/325345/20260824/openai-assistants-api-shuts-down-tuesday-no-automated-migration-threads-risk.htm))

### Nvidia: Vera CPU Architecture Revealed at Hot Chips

Nvidia disclosed the full internal architecture of its Vera CPU at Hot Chips 2026 (August 24):
- 88 custom Olympus Arm cores across six chiplets on a single interposer
- Spatial multi-threading: core resources split into two pipelines with shared data/cache
- Eight 128-bit LPDDR5X controllers, 1.2 TB/s SOCAMM2 memory bandwidth
- Claims ~1.8x faster task completion on agentic workloads vs. Grace Blackwell
- Directly benchmarked against AMD's Venice CPU line

Vera is the CPU component of the Vera Rubin full-stack AI factory platform (Vera CPU + Rubin GPU + BlueField-4 + Spectrum-6).

([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/hot-chips-2026-nvidia-breaks-down-88-core-vera-cpu-spatial-multithreading-benchmarked-1-2-tb-s-socamm2-memory-agentic-workloads-detailed-and-more) · [ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/nvidia-vera-cpu-at-hot-chips-2026/) · [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/hot-chips-conference/))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### OpenAI Disrupts Russian Influence Operation Using ChatGPT

OpenAI disclosed on August 25 that it disrupted a Russian covert-influence cluster that used ChatGPT (accessed via VPNs) to generate English-language propaganda across Substack, Telegram, X, Facebook, and LinkedIn. The operation built a fake Israeli think tank called the "International Burke Institute," falsely listing prominent academics including Francis Fukuyama and Noam Chomsky as experts. Of 36 articles linked to the site (September 2025–May 2026), 34 were plagiarized. The operation included a fabricated "sovereignty index" designed to cast Russia favorably. Audiences were small, but the operation's elaborate construction sets it apart from cruder influence campaigns.

([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-influence-campaign-russia/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/openai-banned-russian-chatgpt-accounts-influence-campaign-082526) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/politics/26/08/61407726/openai-uncovers-a-covert-russian-influence-machine-using-chatgpt-to-build-fake-expertise-and-spread-pro-moscow-narratives-across-social-media))

### Sam Altman: AI Control Concentrated in "Too Few Hands"

In an August 23 interview with podcaster David Senra, Altman warned against AI power concentration: "The right approach is for people to deeply control the future." He argued AI's direction should be steered by social choices, not company agendas, saying "democratization of AI is the only fair and safe path forward." This continues his broader August deceleration posture — acknowledging he overestimated disruption speed and that "the economy just has so much inertia."

([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/23/sam-altman-voices-fears-that-control-of-ai-could-be-centered-in-too-few-hands/) · [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/sam-altman-backs-democratic-ai-160058276.html))

### Dario Amodei: AI Backlash Is "Fundamentally a Crisis of Trust"

Amodei posted on X (August 16–17) that public AI backlash is driven by decades of institutional distrust, not by safety warnings. "Ordinary people don't trust companies, governments, or the tech industry and always suspect that we are cooking up some new way to screw them over." ## Other Notable AI News

### EU AI Act Transparency Obligations Now Enforced

Since August 2, the European Commission's AI Office has been enforcing the AI Act's Article 50 transparency requirements. AI systems must now identify themselves to humans they interact with; deepfakes and AI-generated text on public-interest matters must carry machine-readable labels. Non-compliance triggers fines up to EUR 15M or 3% of global annual turnover. Anthropic's global watermarking rollout (invisible marks in all Claude text output) is the most visible industry response so far.

([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august) · [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/))

### Developer AI Dependency Hits 80%

A new Coddy Developer Survey finds 80% of developers describe their AI coding tool usage as "dependence" rather than an advantage. Respondents cite the loss of natural stopping points (reviews, mental walls) as a driver of daily fatigue, with LeadDev's 2026 leadership survey finding 45% of engineers now work more hours per week than last year.

### Global Startup Funding Hit $510B in H1 2026

Crunchbase reports global venture funding reached a record $510B in H1 2026, surpassing all of 2025's $440B. OpenAI and Anthropic alone accounted for $217B — 43% of all startup funding. AI infrastructure, healthcare automation, robotics, and defense remain the top categories.
