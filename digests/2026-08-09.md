## Notable Papers & Research

### GRAM: Making Open-Weight Models Safer by Removing Dangerous Knowledge

Joint research from AE Studio and Anthropic, cited by Dario Amodei on July 27, introduces GRAM — a modular training technique that compartmentalizes dangerous knowledge into removable modules before open-weight release. The released model behaves as if it never learned that information, and the approach resists fine-tuning attacks that try to train the knowledge back in. A potential breakthrough for the open-vs-closed safety debate.

([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ae-studio-research-cited-by-anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-as-a-promising-method-for-making-open-weights-ai-models-safer-302845303.html) · [Anthropic Position Statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models))

### Video Models as Native 4D Renderers

New arXiv paper proposes world-grounded conditioning from animated mesh to turn video diffusion models into 4D scene renderers, bridging the gap between 2D video generation and 3D-consistent scene synthesis.

([arXiv cs.CV](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CV/current))

### Gene Ontology-Guided Spatial Gene Expression Prediction

Accepted at ACM MM 2026, this paper uses hierarchical spatial prediction from histopathology images guided by gene ontology structure — a cross-disciplinary application of vision models to computational biology.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### Meta Launches Muse Code + Muse Spark 1.2

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Code (beta) on August 5 — a terminal-based coding agent that competes directly with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex CLI. Powered by the new Muse Spark 1.2 model (co-trained with the agent itself), it runs parallel subagents in isolated git worktrees and installs with a single shell command on macOS/Linux. Pricing: $1.25/$4.25 per 1M tokens, or $0.10/$0.20 if you opt into training data sharing. Muse Spark 1.2 has a 1M context window and focuses on coding, debugging, and codebase navigation.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/meta-launches-muse-code-an-ai-agent-for-large-code-bases/) · [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-code-and-muse-spark-1-2) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/05/meta-superintelligence-labs-releases-muse-code/))

### Alibaba Releases Qwen3.8-Max — 2.4T Parameters, Open Weights Coming

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max on August 3, its most capable model yet: 2.4T total parameters (95B active via Sparse MoE), 1M context window, multimodal. Ranks 2nd on Vision Arena, 5th on Text Arena. API pricing at $2/$6 per 1M tokens. Open weights for Qwen3.8-Max and Qwen3.8-27B are scheduled for release on Hugging Face during the week of August 10 — the first time Alibaba has open-sourced a Max-class model.

([Alibaba Cloud](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/press-room/alibaba-unveils-qwen3-8-max?_p_lc=1) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/alibaba-drops-another-china-ai-model-with-breakthrough-performance) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/03/alibaba-qwen-releases-qwen3-8-max/))

### DeepSeek V4 Flash — 99% Cheaper Than Claude Opus 4.8

DeepSeek released V4-Flash-0731 in public beta: 284B total parameters (13B active), 1M context window, MIT-licensed. API pricing at $0.14/$0.28 per 1M tokens (cache hits: $0.0028). Approaches Claude Opus 4.8 performance on coding benchmarks while costing roughly 99% less. V4-Pro official release expected "soon."

([HuggingFace Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/deepseek-v4-flash-official-release) · [BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash) · [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Demis Hassabis — Steps Down as DeepMind CEO, Becomes Alphabet Chief Scientist

Announced August 5: Hassabis moves from CEO of Google DeepMind to Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, shifting from day-to-day management to AGI strategy and scientific applications. Koray Kavukcuoglu (CTO) takes over as SVP of Google DeepMind, overseeing Gemini development and the Gemini app. Hassabis retains leadership of Isomorphic Labs (AI drug discovery). The move formalizes what insiders describe as a long-planned transition.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/google-deepmind-boss-hassabis-moves-to-chair-role-in-shakeup) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/05/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-ai) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/05/demis-hassabis-steps-down-google-deepmind-ai-shakeup/))

### Jeff Dean — Leaves Google After 27 Years to Found Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le left Google on August 5 to co-found Discovery Loop, a startup aiming to automate the experimental loops of scientific research. Google will be a founding investor and Cloud partner. The departure of four of Google's most influential AI figures — including the architects of MapReduce, TensorFlow, and Gemini — is the most significant talent exodus in the company's history. Alphabet shares fell ~4-5% ($160-200B in market cap).

([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/jeff-dean-leaves-google-to-automate-the-scientific-method-with-discovery-loop/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/jeff-dean-google-chief-scientist-discovery-loop-startup-080526) · [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/))

### Sam Altman — OpenAI Fires Back at Apple; Astra Previewed to White House

OpenAI filed a Motion to Dismiss Apple's trade-secret lawsuit on August 6, calling it "rotten to the core" and arguing OpenAI "has no use, need or desire for Apple's trade secrets." Apple alleges former employees brought confidential information about unreleased products to OpenAI. Hearing set for October 1. Separately, Altman previewed OpenAI's upcoming Astra model to the White House ahead of the government's classified AI framework deadline, with no public release date announced.

([OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/apple-is-getting-this-wrong/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/04/openai-fires-back-at-apple-publishing-private-emails-to-counter-trade-secret-claims/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/altman-white-house-wiles-ai-framework.html))

### OpenAI-Apple Trade Secrets Lawsuit Escalates

Apple's lawsuit accuses OpenAI of coordinated theft of confidential information about unreleased products, alleging a former employee exploited a security bug and that job candidates were encouraged to bring proprietary Apple hardware to interviews. OpenAI's August 6 Motion to Dismiss argues it isn't building anything that would benefit from Apple's secrets. October 1 hearing on Apple's preliminary injunction request.

([Apple Insider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/06/openai-fires-back-says-apple-is-suing-because-it-cant-compete) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/04/apple-says-more-ex-employees-may-have-taken-confidential-data-to-openai/))

### AI Model Pricing War Accelerates

The market is in a full pricing race: DeepSeek V4 Flash at $0.14/1M input, Meta's training-data-sharing tier at $0.10/1M input, Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max at $2/1M. Performance differences among top-tier models continue to narrow, shifting buyer decisions toward cost, latency, and ecosystem fit. Nine model releases across five calendar days from four vendors in the first week of August alone.

([AIToolsRecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/AINewsAugust2026.aspx) · [TechDG](https://techdg.in/stay-ahead-of-the-curve-with-our-august-2026-ai-news-report-explore-the-latest-ai-model-updates-robotics-and-how-to-adapt-your-business-strategy-today/))

### U.S. Frontier Model Review Gates Now Active

The White House completed its classified framework for vetting frontier AI models. "Covered frontier models" are defined as closed-source systems with state-of-the-art capabilities and national security risks; open models are explicitly excluded. Major releases (GPT-5.6, Claude Fable 5) now require government review. Benchmarks and thresholds remain classified.

([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/08/05/2026/the-white-house-maintains-a-little-obscurity-for-ai-security) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/04/baffling-white-house-wont-publicly-release-ai-model-evaluation-framework-it-reviewed-today-with-openai-anthropic-microsoft-and-others/))
