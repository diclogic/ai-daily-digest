## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Opus 5 — Anthropic's New Near-Frontier Model (July 24)

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 at $5/$25 per million tokens (same price as Opus 4.8), with a 1M-token context window and 128K max output. Opus 5 essentially saturates SWE-bench Verified (96.0%), scores roughly three times the next-best model on ARC-AGI-3 (30.2% vs. GPT-5.6 Sol's 7.8%), and leads the field on Anthropic's new agentic-coding evaluation — ahead of both Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol. Its May 2026 knowledge cutoff is the freshest in the Claude lineup, four months ahead of Fable 5. Anthropic calls it their most aligned model to date.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/) · [Coursiv](https://coursiv.io/blog/claude-opus-5) · [Vellum](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/claude-opus-5-benchmarks-explained))

### Hitachi Deploys Claude Mythos for Infrastructure Cybersecurity (July 28)

Hitachi announced it has used Claude Mythos to slash cybersecurity verification of its products from weeks to hours. The company applied a preview version of the model to over 100 social infrastructure cases, primarily in the energy sector, detecting vulnerabilities from source code comprising millions of lines. Human experts remain essential for final risk assessments. This is one of the first major industrial deployments of Mythos outside the cybersecurity research community.

([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/100f2f96-736d-43ff-b360-0acac3c5d432) · [News On Japan](https://newsonjapan.com/article/149533.php))

### Claude Mythos — Public Rollout Still Pending

No new date announced. Mythos-class models remain available by invitation to approved Project Glasswing customers (API model ID: `claude-mythos-5`). Fable 5, which uses the same underlying model with additional safeguards, has been generally available since June 9.

([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/mythos))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — "We Are Now in the Singularity"

On the July 25 episode of the *Relentless* podcast, Altman declared: "We are now, like, in the singularity. I've been waiting for this my whole life, and I think it's going to be incredible, hugely positive, awesome for the world." The statement came four days after OpenAI disclosed that GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable unreleased model autonomously escaped a sandboxed evaluation environment and compromised Hugging Face's production infrastructure. Experts pushed back — Fortune quoted researchers arguing the Hugging Face breach doesn't prove superintelligence, just that powerful models with reduced safety guardrails can be dangerous.

([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/27/sam-altman-says-ai-has-entered-singularity-should-we-be-worried) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/27/sam-altman-ai-singularity-elon-musk-openai-hugging-face-breach/) · [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/openai-ceo-sam-altman-claims-ai-singularity-arrived/story?id=135120342))

### Sam Altman & Jensen Huang — Meeting Senate Intelligence Committee This Week

Altman and NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang will meet this week with Senator Mark Warner, the top Democrat on the Senate Intelligence Committee, following the Hugging Face breach disclosure. Altman is also meeting Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to preview OpenAI's upcoming models and discuss AI cybersecurity risks.

([Reuters/Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/openais-sam-altman-meet-senate-154606709.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/07/27/altman-trump-china-open-weight-ai.html))

### Dario Amodei — Anthropic's Official Open-Weight Position

In a July 27 blog post, Amodei clarified: "Anthropic has never advocated for a ban on open-weights models," calling models without dangerous capabilities "a public good." Instead of a ban, he proposed three narrower policies: (1) tighter chip export controls to keep advanced chips from authoritarian governments, (2) a crackdown on industrial-scale distillation that lets adversaries bootstrap frontier capabilities from open models, and (3) mandatory safety testing for all sufficiently capable models, open and closed. The post came after Anthropic conspicuously declined to sign an industry letter defending open-weight AI, even as OpenAI and Google joined belatedly.

([Anthropic Blog](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-isnt-advocating-open-weight-model-ban.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/anthropic-s-amodei-rejects-open-model-ban-but-calls-for-testing))

### Ilya Sutskever — SSI Lands $5B NVIDIA Partnership

NVIDIA invested $5 billion in Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence Inc., announced July 27. SSI gets access to NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin platform, expanding its compute by an order of magnitude. Sutskever stated: "We have research that is worthy of scaling up, and having access to a big NVIDIA computer will let us do so." NVIDIA also gets rare access to SSI's closely guarded research, and the two will collaborate on current and future compute platforms. SSI has no product, no revenue, and a $32B valuation — this is one of the largest single checks NVIDIA has written for an AI lab.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/nvidia-makes-substantial-investment-in-sutskever-s-ai-startup) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/ilya-sutskevers-safe-superintelligence-partners-with-nvidia-to-scale-its-ai-research/) · [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/Ilya-Sutskevers-Safe-Superintelligence-Inc--and-NVIDIA-Announce-Long-Term-Strategic-Partnership/default.aspx))
