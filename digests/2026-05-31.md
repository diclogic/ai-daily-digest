## Notable Papers & Research

**Stanford Hiring Algorithm Audit**
Stanford researchers released findings from a large-scale study of AI hiring algorithms, uncovering concerning patterns in how automated systems reject candidates — raising fresh questions about algorithmic bias in employment decisions at scale.

**LLM-Emu — Online Emulator for LLM Inference**
University of Cambridge researchers introduced LLM-Emu, an online emulator that replaces GPU forward execution with profile-driven latency sampling for LLM inference benchmarking, achieving less than 5% absolute error in wall-clock time predictions without requiring actual GPU hardware.

**Anthropic Project Glasswing Update**
Anthropic published updated results from Project Glasswing, its initiative using Claude Mythos Preview to secure critical open-source software. Scanning over 1,000 OSS projects, Mythos identified 23,019 potential issues — 6,202 rated high or critical severity — with over 90% validated as true positives by external security firms. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/glasswing-initial-update) | [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/26/anthropic-project-glasswing-update/) | [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-mythos-detected-23000-potential-vulnerabilities-across-1000-oss-projects/))

## Key Opinions & Statements

**Demis Hassabis — "Species-Level Transition" (Stanford, May 29)**
The Google DeepMind CEO warned at Stanford's AI@GSB series that AI represents a "species-level transition" with "little margin for error" over the next decade. He said humanity is in the "foothills of the singularity" and AI is advancing roughly 10x faster than the Industrial Revolution. Hassabis narrowed his AGI timeline to 2029-2030, supported "smart, targeted" AI regulation, and said he hopes AI will cure disease and slow aging — but that empathy, mentorship, and inspiration should remain "uniquely human work." ([Stanford Daily](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/05/29/deepmind-ceo-warns-about-ai/) | [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/05/20/2026/google-exec-demis-hassabis-predicts-were-at-the-foothills-of-the-singularity))

**Sam Altman & Dario Amodei — Walking Back Jobs Apocalypse**
Both CEOs have reversed earlier warnings about AI-driven mass unemployment as their companies approach IPOs. Altman told Commonwealth Bank of Australia CEO Matt Comyn he was "pretty wrong" about AI's economic impact, retreating from June 2025 warnings that entry-level roles were at serious risk. The timing — coinciding with both companies' IPO preparations — has drawn scrutiny. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/) | [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/05/26/sam-altman-ai-job-losses-openAI-/) | [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/05/26/no-ai-jobs-apocalypse-so-far-says-openais-sam-altman))

**Yann LeCun — AI Doom Narratives Are "Extremely Destructive"**
The Turing Award winner and AMI Labs founder continued his campaign against AI hype, calling CEO-driven job loss narratives "extremely destructive." He urged students not to abandon college plans over AI fears, arguing this technological revolution is fundamentally no different from prior ones. LeCun also predicted the robotics industry will need a "fundamental paradigm shift" away from LLM-based architectures by early 2027, calling current Vision-Language-Action models "brittle." ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/ai-job-apocalypse-warnings-destructive-yann-lecun/) | [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/04/ai-godfather-survival-guide-hype-doom) | [Humanoids Daily](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/yann-lecun-predicts-paradigm-shift-in-robotics-by-2027-dismisses-llms-as-intrinsically-unsafe))

**Andrej Karpathy — Settling In at Anthropic**
Two weeks after his high-profile move from OpenAI to Anthropic (announced May 19), Karpathy is leading a new team focused on using Claude itself to accelerate pretraining research. His statement that "the next few years at the frontier of LLMs will be especially formative" signals his belief that the current architecture still has significant headroom — a notable counterpoint to LeCun's LLM skepticism. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/19/anthropic-openai-karpathy-andrej-claude) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/) | [X](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312))

## Other Notable AI News

**HHS Deploys ChatGPT for Healthcare Fraud Detection**
The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services launched "AERO" (Audit Enforcement and Risk Oversight), using ChatGPT and other LLMs to analyze five years of audit records from all 50 states to detect fraud, waste, and abuse in federal health spending. HHS framed the shift as moving from "pay and chase" to "detect and deploy." Hundreds of grantees were found to have delinquent audits, some over two years late. ([US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/us/articles/2026-05-21/us-hhs-launches-ai-initiative-to-detect-fraud-and-waste-in-health-programs) | [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hhs-ai-fraud-state-audit-aero/820989/))
