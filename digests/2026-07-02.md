## Notable Papers

- **"Self-Compacting Language Model Agents"** — Introduces a method for LLM agents to autonomously compress their own context during long-horizon tasks, reducing token costs without sacrificing task completion. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23525))

- **"Benchmarking Agentic Review Systems"** — Systematically evaluates AI-powered code and paper review agents across consistency, coverage, and hallucination rate, providing a standard benchmark for the rapidly growing review-agent space. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19749))

- **"ResearcherBench: Evaluating Deep AI Research Systems on the Frontiers of Scientific Inquiry"** — A new benchmark designed to evaluate AI systems on open-ended scientific research tasks rather than narrow question-answering, measuring whether models can formulate hypotheses and design experiments. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16280))

## Model & Product Launches

### Anthropic: Claude Sonnet 5 (July 1)
Anthropic's newest general-purpose model is now the default for all Free and Pro Claude users. Sonnet 5 ships with improved reasoning, coding, and tool use, performing close to flagship Opus 4.8 on many tasks. Introductory pricing runs at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens through August 31, then moves to $3/$15. Commercial versions include real-time safety classifiers equivalent to the Opus 4.8 framework. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5))

### Anthropic: Fable 5 & Mythos 5 Restored (July 1)
After a 19-day suspension triggered by U.S. export controls, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick lifted restrictions on June 30. Fable 5 returned to global availability on July 1 across Claude.ai and Claude Code. The episode began June 12 when Amazon researchers found a jailbreak that let Fable 5 produce exploit code; Anthropic responded by training a new safety classifier that blocks the technique in >99% of cases. Mythos 5 — Anthropic's vulnerability-focused model sitting above the Opus tier — was partially restored to 100+ vetted U.S. organizations. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/01/anthropic-fable-mythos-ai-models-restored-trump-administration-export-controls/))

### Anthropic: Claude Science (June 30)
A new AI workbench for scientific research, positioned as "Claude Code for science." It integrates 60+ scientific databases and tools via a coordinating agent that delegates to domain-specialized sub-agents (genomics, proteomics, cheminformatics, etc.). Runs on Claude Opus 4.8, available to all paid subscribers. Anthropic also announced a research support program: up to 50 projects, $30K in compute credits each, applications open through July 15. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench), [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/30/1139987/claude-science-is-anthropics-newest-flagship-product/))

### OpenAI: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna (Limited Preview)
OpenAI previewed its GPT-5.6 family — Sol (flagship, $5/$30), Terra (balanced, $2.50/$15), and Luna (fast, $1/$6) — to ~20 vetted organizations under a government-coordinated rollout. Sol is launching on Cerebras at up to 750 tokens/sec. It sets new state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and is competitive with Anthropic's Mythos Preview on ExploitBench at 1/3 the output tokens, though it does not cross OpenAI's "Cyber Critical" threshold. General availability expected in "coming weeks." ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov))

### Google: Gemini 3.1 Flash Image Models (June 30)
Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash Image ("Nano Banana 2") and Flash-Lite Image. Flash-Lite generates images in 4 seconds at $0.034/1,000 images, the cheapest option in Google's creative model line. Both are available through Google AI Studio and the Gemini API; existing preview endpoints deprecate July 17. ([Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/3-1-flash-image))

### Claude Mythos Watch
Mythos 5 is the latest update to the Mythos Preview model first released in April via Project Glasswing. It remains restricted to vetted partners but access expanded from ~50 to 100+ U.S. organizations as of July 1. Fable 5 is architecturally the same underlying model as Mythos 5 but with robust safeguards for cybersecurity and biology domains. No broader public availability timeline has been announced. ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/05/29/anthropics-guarded-mythos-model-is-headed-for-wider-release/))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO)
At the Claude Science launch event on June 30, Amodei tempered expectations on near-term biological breakthroughs: he emphasized AI will be "a general purpose technology that helps us to make sense of that complexity" but said he doesn't expect the "compressed 21st century" effect he's previously predicted to materialize in the next couple of years. Separately, Anthropic's $350M worker displacement commitment (announced June 11) remains in the spotlight — a $200M research fund plus a $150M fellowship program (Claude Corps, $85K/year, applications close July 17). The company proposed a 3% AI revenue tax to fund UBI. ([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-release-claude-science-ceo-dario-amodei/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/anthropic-dario-amodei-ai-jobs-tax-displacement-fund/))

### Sam Altman (OpenAI CEO)
Altman has been engaging with economic policymakers, speaking with Fed Chair Jerome Powell and appearing at Federal Reserve conferences about AI's macroeconomic impact. OpenAI faces a pivotal year — enterprise market share has fallen from ~50% in 2023 to 27%, and enterprise customers increasingly complain about costs running ahead of 2025-era budget projections. ([SF Examiner](https://www.sfexaminer.com/news/technology/why-openai-faces-massively-critical-year-ahead-in-2026/article_6112d59f-28b5-4770-bde1-b333deb5f01a.html))

### Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind CEO)
In a June interview with Semafor, Hassabis dismissed the narrative that Google is losing top AI talent despite a string of high-profile departures, insisting DeepMind continues to attract leading researchers. He maintains his AGI timeline of "2030, plus or minus a year," with 2029 now seen as a possibility. ([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/23/2026/deepmind-chief-demis-hassabis-says-googles-still-winning-ai-talent))

### Yann LeCun (AMI Labs)
Now fully independent from Meta, LeCun continues to build AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs), which raised $1.03B at a $3.5B valuation in March. The venture is focused on "world models" — AI systems that learn physical-world structure and dynamics rather than predicting text — targeting manufacturing, aerospace, and robotics applications. LeCun has reiterated at the AI Impact Summit that AI's near-term role is as an "amplifier for human intelligence," not a replacement. ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/01/22/1131661/yann-lecuns-new-venture-ami-labs/), [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/03/10/former-meta-ai-chief-yann-lecun-just-raised-1-billion-to-prove-the-entire-ai-industry-is-wrong/))

### Ilya Sutskever (SSI CEO)
Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence Inc. continues its singular approach — $6B raised at a $32B valuation with ~20 researchers, no product, no published papers. He has framed 2026 as the start of a "new research era" where algorithmic innovation, not additional compute, drives progress. SSI remains the highest-valued AI lab with no commercially available product. ([StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-ilya-sutskever-ssi-financial-breakdown-2026-06-06))

## Trending Open-Source Projects

### [facebook/astryx](https://github.com/facebook/astryx)
Open-source design system built inside Meta over 8 years, powering 13,000+ internal apps. Ships 150+ accessible React components, brand-level theming, dark mode, and a CLI — designed so both humans and AI agents build with the same tooling. Went public recently and surging.
**Why trending:** First major design system explicitly built for agent-ready UIs; fills a gap as more products ship AI-powered interfaces.
*2,831 stars | +708 today*

### [ogulcancelik/herdr](https://github.com/ogulcancelik/herdr)
A terminal-native agent multiplexer written in Rust — think tmux rebuilt for AI agents. One local binary, no GUI, no Electron, no account, no telemetry. Lets you run and manage multiple coding agents in parallel terminal sessions.
**Why trending:** Solves a real pain point as developers juggle Claude Code, Codex, and other terminal agents simultaneously. v0.6.3 dropped June 30.
*9,733 stars | +609 today*

### [allenai/olmocr](https://github.com/allenai/olmocr)
Toolkit from the Allen Institute for AI for linearizing PDFs into clean text suitable for LLM training datasets. Handles complex layouts, tables, and figures that trip up simpler parsers.
**Why trending:** Data quality is the bottleneck for the current generation of models; OLMoCR offers a production-grade open-source solution for document-to-training-data pipelines.
*18,359 stars | +334 today*
