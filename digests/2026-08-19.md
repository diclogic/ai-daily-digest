## Notable Papers & Research

### SparDA — Sparse Decoupled Attention for Efficient Long-Context Inference

NVIDIA researchers introduced SparDA, an add-on architecture that introduces a fourth per-layer projection (the "Forecast") alongside Query, Key, and Value, enabling lookahead selection of which KV blocks the next layer will need. On MiniCPM4.1-8B at 128K context, SparDA achieves 1.81x speedup over dense attention and improves long-reasoning accuracy by 6.5 points. The technique is architecture-agnostic and can be layered on top of existing sparse attention methods, making it relevant for frontier models like DeepSeek-V4 and GLM-5.1.

([arXiv 2606.04511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04511) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.04511))

### Intern-S2-Mobius — Continued Discussion

Shanghai AI Lab's Mobius-v0 architecture (published August 17) continues generating discussion. The approach separates knowledge storage from reasoning computation, achieving comparable performance with 62.6% less training data and up to 4x inference speedup. If validated at larger scales, it challenges the assumption that bigger contexts require proportionally more compute.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13505) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/internlm/Intern-S2-Mobius))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI ChatGPT for Teens — Launched August 18

OpenAI launched a teen-tailored version of ChatGPT for users aged 13–17, the first generation to grow up with AI assistants. Key features include: content restrictions blocking suicide, self-harm, and romantic/sexual conversations; age-prediction to auto-route minors into the teen mode; a Study Mode that gives guiding questions instead of direct answers; parental controls with quiet hours and high-risk safety notifications; and more frequent break reminders during extended use. ChatGPT will also be barred from using terms of endearment or suggesting it has feelings or consciousness when interacting with teens.

([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-teens-ai-safety/39739860-9af4-11f1-9cc4-2dc9b46e2d5c_story.html) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/chatgpt-teen-safety-measures-include-age-verification-openai-says-rcna231637) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-for-teens))

### DeepSeek V4-Pro GA — August 13

DeepSeek's V4-Pro went generally available with the V4-Pro-0813 checkpoint, adding adjustable reasoning effort levels (low/high/max) to its API. The release includes native support for the OpenAI Responses API, Codex integration, and off-peak pricing. Guidance: start at "high" for agent workflows and route only the hard tail to "max" — reliable agent skills and disciplined loop engineering matter more than brute-force reasoning on every turn.

([CoderSera](https://codersera.com/blog/deepseek-v4-complete-guide-2026/) · [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-pro-0813-terminal-bench-cline-august-2026))

## Other Notable AI News

### Etched Doubles Valuation to $21B in Under a Month

AI chip startup Etched raised $700M in a round led by Jane Street, with Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, and Tiger Global also participating. The San Jose company was valued at $5B in December, $10.3B in July, and now $21B — a 4x increase in eight months. Etched builds specialized inference systems designed to make AI models faster and cheaper to run, directly challenging NVIDIA's dominance.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/inference-chip-startup-etched-raises-another-700m-at-21b-valuation/))

### Apple Accidentally Leaks Camera AirPods with AI Vision

A video found in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate shows camera-equipped AirPods using Visual Intelligence — Apple's on-device AI that lets Siri answer questions about the wearer's surroundings. ### AI Store Manager Luna Fires First Human Employee

Andon Labs' AI store manager Luna, built on Claude Opus 4.8, recommended firing a human employee at San Francisco's Andon Market after 17 of 23 shift no-shows — the first known dismissal recommendation by an LLM manager. The process was more nuanced than headlines suggest: Luna initially recommended a formal warning, only escalating to termination after learning that multiple offline warnings had already been given. Humans reviewed and carried out the decision. Co-founder Lukas Petersson noted a human manager would have fired the employee much sooner.

([Time](https://time.com/article/2026/08/14/claude-fired-worker-ai-job-disruption/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/andon-market-luna-ai-store-manager-fires-employee) · [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/a-real-life-terminator-ai-store-manager-fires-human-employee/91391677))
