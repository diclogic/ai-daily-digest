## Notable Papers & Research

### Discriminative World Models for Web Agents
*Kelvin Li, Dhruv Pendharkar, Anish Pahilajani, Chuyi Shang, Leon Oks, Leonid Karlinsky, Rogerio Feris, Trevor Darrell, Roei Herzig — arXiv:2609.02885, September 2026*

Proposes discriminative world models that let web agents predict whether a candidate action will reach a goal state before executing it — replacing the usual act-then-observe loop with a cheaper lookahead that cuts wasted interactions.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885))

### SafeEvolve: Harness-Policy Co-Evolution from Agent Experience for Safety Alignment
*Qinghua Mao, Wanying Qu, Dadi Guo et al. — arXiv:2609.02786, September 2026*

Introduces a co-evolution framework where an agent's safety harness and its decision policy improve together from runtime experience, rather than being tuned separately. Relevant as labs ship agentic systems with broader tool access.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02786))

### BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning
*Authors not specified — Hugging Face Trending, late August 2026*

A 150M-parameter reasoning model that uses recurrent latent reasoning to achieve a new cost-accuracy frontier on ARC-AGI-1 — noteworthy for showing that small models with the right architecture can punch well above their weight on reasoning benchmarks.

([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending))

### Gemini 3.8 Flash — Live Now
*Google, September 2, 2026*

Google's third Flash model in six weeks. Tuned for long-horizon coding, autonomous agents, and complex enterprise workflows, with a 1M-token context window and 64K output. Key numbers:

- **Performance**: Beats 3.7 Flash on every published benchmark; beats Claude Opus 5 on three. Scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (+3 over predecessor).

- **Availability**: Live in the Gemini app, AI Mode, Google Sheets, AI Studio, and the Gemini API.

### OpenAI Astra — Preview Access Imminent
*OpenAI, September 1, 2026*

OpenAI's next frontier model (successor to GPT-5.6 Sol) is the first to reach the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold under the Preparedness Framework — meaning it can find unknown flaws and build exploits across hardened systems without step-by-step human guidance. ## Other Notable AI News

### NYC Bans Student-Facing AI Through 8th Grade
*September 3, 2026*

New York City's Department of Education — the nation's largest school district — is releasing an AI policy barring student-facing generative AI (including chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude) in pre-K through 8th grade. Companion chatbots are banned K–12. Screen time caps: 30 min/day grades 3–5, 45 min grades 6–8. High schools will pilot five vetted AI programs (max 45 min/week, teacher-supervised) and offer twice-yearly AI literacy classes. Teachers may still use approved AI for lesson planning, translation, and communications, but not for grading, behavior monitoring, or crisis counseling.

([CNN](https://us.cnn.com/2026/09/02/tech/new-york-city-classroom-ai-ban) · [NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/news/2026/09/02/new-york-city-bans-ai-use-in-schools-through-eighth-grade) · [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/09/02/nyc-schools-to-set-ai-policy-ban-screen-time-limits/))

### JetStream Ships Clearance — Zero Trust for AI Agents
*September 2026*

JetStream debuted Clearance, a reasoning engine that evaluates and authorizes every AI agent action before it executes — working at the MCP tool-call level. Policy binds to agent identity and reaches down to individual tool parameters (e.g., allow a server's read tool, block its delete tool). Detection tells you what happened; Clearance decides whether it runs at all. GA this fall.

([Security Boulevard](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/08/jetstream-launches-ai-kill-switch-for-individual-agents/) · [AI Agent Store](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week))

### Sony/Warner v. Anthropic — Lawsuit Pending
*Filed August 29, Northern District of California*

No new developments since filing.
