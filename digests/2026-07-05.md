## Notable Papers

- **"Do We Need Adam? Surprisingly Strong and Sparse Reinforcement Learning with SGD in LLMs"** — Selected as an Oral at ICML 2026, this paper shows that plain SGD matches or outperforms AdamW during RL fine-tuning of LLMs while updating fewer than 0.02% of parameters — over 1,000x fewer than AdamW. Immediate practical payoff: eliminating momentum buffers cuts GPU memory during RL training. ([arXiv:2602.07729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07729))

- **"Kairos: A Native World Model Stack for Physical AI"** — Introduces a full-stack world model framework that learns from diverse embodied experiences, maintains persistent state through hybrid temporal attention, and runs on both server and consumer hardware. Bridges the gap between video generation models and actionable robotic planning. ([arXiv:2606.16533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16533))

### Pentagon–Anthropic Emails Released (July 3–4)
Court documents published July 3–4 revealed the full email exchange between Dario Amodei and Pentagon undersecretary Emil Michael that led to the Defense Department blacklisting Anthropic. The core dispute: Amodei insisted on guardrails barring Claude's use for fully autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance. Michael demanded "all lawful uses" — which, as Amodei pointed out, legally permits domestic surveillance. Michael gave "one more chance to align on core principles," Amodei held firm, and Defense Secretary Hegseth designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk. The emails are the clearest public record of where a frontier AI lab drew an ethical line with the U.S. military. ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/read-the-tense-emails-between-the-pentagon-former-uber-exec-and-anthropic-dario-amodei-2000780849), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319713/20260704/pentagon-blacklisted-anthropic-over-autonomous-weapons-limits-emails-reveal-very-close-talks.htm), [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-pentagon-emails-amodei-michael-guardrails))

## Other Notable AI News

- **UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance opens tomorrow (July 6–7):** The inaugural session in Geneva brings all UN member states together to discuss AI governance frameworks. Runs alongside WSIS Forum 2026 (July 6–10) and ITU's AI for Good Summit (July 7–10) — an unprecedented convergence of global AI governance events. The UN Scientific Panel's preliminary report found the US controls ~75% of frontier AI compute and China ~15%, with 40+ governance frameworks worldwide that remain "fragmented, inconsistent, and rarely tested." ([UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-dialogue-ai-governance-geneva-6-7-july), [UN](https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/en))

- **ICML 2026 opens Monday in Seoul (July 6–11):** Record 23,918 submissions, 6,352 accepted (26.6%), 168 orals. "Agentic AI" appeared in 60+ of 247 workshop proposals. Pascale Fung delivers the opening keynote — a deliberate signal that technical and governance conversations have converged. ([ICML](https://icml.cc/), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319684/20260704/icml-2026-opens-monday-seoul-agentic-ai-tops-record-year-peer-review-strains.htm))

- **Tesla caps employee AI spending at $200/week starting July 6:** After months of gamifying token consumption with leaderboards, Tesla is slamming the brakes — engineers were burning "thousands of dollars in tokens weekly." The cap excludes xAI's Grok products. Uber already burned its entire 2026 AI budget by April; Meta, Amazon, and Walmart have imposed similar limits. Enterprise AI cost management is becoming a systemic challenge. ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/02/tesla-caps-employee-ai-spending-200-week/), [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/tesla-caps-employee-ai-spend-200-per-week-adoption-push))

- **US June jobs report: 57,000 added, weakest since 2024:** AI is cited among structural causes. Goldman Sachs estimates AI automation has increased the unemployment rate by ~0.16 percentage points in easily-substituted occupations, while decreasing it by 0.06 points in "augmented" roles. PwC's 2026 AI Jobs Barometer shows a "two-track" labor market: professionalized roles see 42% faster salary growth, while entry-level codifiable jobs face the squeeze. ([BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-will-reshape-more-jobs-than-it-replaces), [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/ai/ai-jobs-barometer.html))

## Trending Open-Source Projects

### [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)
Collection of Claude Code skills from the creator of Total TypeScript. Fixes common agent failure modes: the /grill-with-docs skill makes agents ask detailed questions before coding, producing a CONTEXT.md that improves every subsequent session. Workflow enforcement over one-shot execution.
**Why trending:** The coding-agent skills ecosystem is exploding, and Pocock's reputation + practical focus made this the canonical collection. First hit #1 trending in April; still gaining ~970 stars/day.
*156,737 stars | +973 today*

### [usestrix/strix](https://github.com/usestrix/strix)
Open-source AI penetration testing tool that finds and fixes application vulnerabilities autonomously. Runs security scans using LLM-powered reasoning about attack surfaces.
**Why trending:** Security teams want AI-native pentesting that goes beyond rule-based scanners. Strix's velocity (1,900+ stars/day) suggests it's hitting a nerve as AI-powered offensive security becomes mainstream.
*36,257 stars | +1,904 today*

### [alibaba/page-agent](https://github.com/alibaba/page-agent)
JavaScript in-page GUI agent that controls web interfaces through natural language via the live DOM. No headless browser, no screenshots, no multi-modal model required — works with any text-capable LLM. MIT-licensed.
**Why trending:** Browser automation without brittle selectors is a universal pain point. Alibaba open-sourcing a production-grade solution with broad LLM compatibility made it instantly viral.
*23,246 stars | +742 today*

### [agentskills/agentskills](https://github.com/agentskills/agentskills)
Specification and documentation for agent skills — a standardized format for packaging reusable capabilities across different coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, etc.).
**Why trending:** As the coding-agent ecosystem fragments, interoperability matters. This project is positioning itself as the common skills standard, riding the same wave as mattpocock/skills.
*22,388 stars | +351 today*
