## Notable Papers & Research

### Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness
*Published August 24, 2026*

An open-source harness that uses recursive subagents, persistent computation, and agent-to-agent coordination to extend language models' long-horizon capabilities across coding and reasoning tasks. Demonstrates that orchestration-level improvements can yield substantial gains without changing the underlying model.

### VoiceMem: Streaming Dual-Brain Memory for Real-Time Interaction
*Published August 26, 2026*

Introduces a dual-brain streaming memory architecture for speech language models that improves retrieval accuracy, emotional personalization, and real-time efficiency — a step toward voice assistants that remember and adapt across conversations without prohibitive latency.

### F-WANDA: Fisher-Reweighted Post-Training Pruning for Sustainable LLM Deployment
*Himanshu Mishra (University of British Columbia)*

Proposes a Fisher-information-reweighted pruning method that preserves critical parameters while aggressively compressing large language models post-training, targeting the growing cost and energy footprint of deploying frontier models at scale.

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI Astra (GPT-6) — Leaks Intensify, September Release Expected

Leaked outputs from Astra surfaced under internal codenames "mozaik-alpha-fdm" and "ultima-alpha," showing voxel world generation, photorealistic product renders, and zero-shot website designs at Max reasoning effort. Prediction markets put Astra at 60% chance of release by September 15, 78% by month-end. OpenAI has not confirmed a release date, and the model remains in a safety review after hitting the "critical cybersecurity threshold" in August. Astra is widely understood to be GPT-6.

### Dario Amodei: AI Companies Haven't Delivered, IPO Looms

In a mid-August X post, Amodei wrote that "the most accurate criticism of AI companies including Anthropic is that they have not yet delivered on their promises" and that only genuine breakthroughs will win public trust. He separately voiced support for pre-deployment testing of frontier and near-frontier open-weights models, and a self-regulatory body for AI. Anthropic investors are targeting an October IPO at a $2 trillion valuation — which would be the largest IPO in history, surpassing SpaceX's $1.77T listing in June. ## Other Notable AI News

### John Ternus Becomes Apple CEO (September 1)

John Ternus officially assumed the CEO role today, ending Tim Cook's 15-year tenure (Cook becomes Executive Chairman). Ternus inherits an ambitious fall product slate for the September 9 event: iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max with the 2nm A20 Pro chip, Apple's first foldable iPhone, a smart home display with facial recognition, a tabletop robot, AirPods with cameras, smart glasses, and an overhaul of Siri powered by an on-device LLM. The transition is the biggest leadership change in consumer tech since Satya Nadella succeeded Steve Ballmer.

([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/apple-s-new-ceo-john-ternus-takes-reins-from-tim-cook-focusing-on-ai) · [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/new-apple-ceo-john-ternus-takes-over-on-sept-1-ahead-of-iphone-18-launch-amid-ai-race-what-to-know-1.500657538))

### a16z Closes $1.1B Machine Age Fund, Raises Growth Fund to $8.5B

Andreessen Horowitz closed its first AI hardware-dedicated fund on August 28 — the $1.1B "Machine Age Fund" targeting chips, memory, networking, storage, data centers, and robotics. Three days later (August 31), a16z raised its fifth growth fund to $8.5B, bringing total new capital to $9.6B as global AI investment approaches $1 trillion for 2026.

([TechCrunch — Machine Age](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/) · [TechCrunch — Growth](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/a16z-brings-growth-fund-to-8-5b-days-after-launching-new-1-1b-fund/))

### Discovery Loop: Jeff Dean's Post-Google Startup Takes Shape

Jeff Dean's Discovery Loop — co-founded with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals after all four left Google in August — is reportedly in talks to raise $1B at a ~$10B valuation. The public benefit corporation aims to automate the scientific method by running thousands of experimental loops in parallel, starting with ML research before expanding to chip design, biology, and materials science. Co-led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures, with Google as founding investor and cloud partner.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/jeff-dean-google-chief-scientist-discovery-loop-startup-080526) · [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/))
