## Model & Product Launches

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Three Days Out (July 17 Target)

Google's next flagship remains on track for a July 17 GA launch, though Google has not officially confirmed the date, the reported 2M-token context window, or pricing. The model is a ground-up architectural rebuild after engineers found structural failures in recursive tool-calling and SVG generation in the original 2.5 Pro base. Reported features include a Deep Think Reasoning Layer for multi-step logic and math. Every circulating spec comes from unnamed sources — no model card or API listing exists yet.

([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model))

### Claude Fable 5 — Extended Again to July 19

Anthropic extended included Fable 5 access on paid plans for the second time in a week — now through July 19 — an apparent competitive response to OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol launch. After July 19, all Fable 5 usage moves to prepaid credits at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. Meanwhile, an unreleased model called "Claude Honeycomb EAP" briefly appeared in Cursor, fueling speculation that Opus 5 may ship by month-end. Anthropic has neither confirmed nor denied the leak.

([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/13/ai-model-wars-anthropic-extends-fable-access-again-after-openais-sol-release/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/fable-5-honeycomb-opus/))

### DeepSeek V4 — Mid-July Launch Approaching

DeepSeek confirmed V4 is coming mid-July with a standard 1M-token context window across its entire lineup, stronger agent execution, math reasoning, and code generation. For the first time, the company will introduce peak/off-peak API pricing. DeepSeek also raised 51 billion yuan (~$7B) — the largest single AI fundraise in China's history — and is developing its own inference chip to reduce reliance on Nvidia and Huawei hardware.

([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/06/30/deepseek-to-launch-v4-in-mid-july-with-new-peak-time-api-pricing/) · [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/deepseek-record-funding-domestic-compute-agi-jul2026) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/chinese-ai-startup-deepseek-developing-own-ai-chip-reuters-says))

Claude Mythos 5 remains restricted to ~150 vetted organizations via Project Glasswing. No timeline for broader public access.

## Notable Papers & Research

### "We Must Act Now" — Economic Impact Statement

Over 200 economists and AI researchers, including 16 Nobel laureates (Michael Spence, Daron Acemoglu, Simon Johnson, and others), published a joint statement on July 13 warning that AI may grow "far more capable over the coming decade" and could bring "large-scale job displacement" alongside major gains in living standards. Organized by Erik Brynjolfsson, Ajay Agrawal, Anton Korinek, and Tom Cunningham. The full statement and signatories are at wemustactnow.ai.

([Stanford Digital Economy Lab](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/wemustactnow/) · [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-13/over-200-experts-call-for-urgent-action-to-tackle-ais-economic-impact) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/07/13/nobel-economists-tech-leaders-warn-how-ai-could-threaten-jobs/))

### SceneSmith — Collaborative AI Agents for 3D Environments

MIT researchers announced SceneSmith on July 13, a system using collaborative AI agents to create realistic 3D environments (kitchens, hotels, living rooms) where robots can simulate everyday tasks. Represents a step toward scalable sim-to-real training for robotics.

([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/topic/artificial-intelligence2))

### AI Research Shifts in H1 2026

An analysis of 170,927 AI papers posted to arXiv in the first half of 2026 found a clear shift from model-centric innovation toward system-level deployment, governance, and real-world integration. Dominant themes: AI agents, evaluation frameworks, responsible scaling, and human-AI collaboration.

([Medium / AI Papers Academy](https://medium.com/@aipapers/170-927-ai-papers-reveal-the-biggest-research-shifts-of-the-first-half-of-2026-f26f3b792824))

## Other Notable AI News

### China Bans AI Companions for Minors — Effective Tomorrow

China's Interim Measures for the Management of AI Human-Like Interaction Services take effect July 15. The regulation bans virtual companion and virtual family-member services for minors, requires guardian consent for users under 14, and prohibits inducing emotional dependence. ByteDance's Doubao is taking its agent function offline on July 15; Alibaba's Qwen already shut down user-created agents on July 10.

([AI Safety China](https://aisafetychina.substack.com/p/china-bans-ai-partners-for-minors) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/china-ai-rules-force-bytedance-131534302.html) · [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=65e35f8f-dcec-4576-b81f-e9d141d76445))

### Meta Iris Chip Enters Production in September

Meta's custom AI chip "Iris," designed with Broadcom and fabricated by TSMC, will begin production in September after passing testing in just six weeks. The chip handles both training and inference and is part of Meta's campaign to cut dependence on Nvidia. Meta plans 7 GW of compute in 2026, doubling to 14 GW in 2027. Separately, Meta Compute — which sells AI compute to third parties — launched the same week.

([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/meta-start-production-iris-ai-122141801.html) · [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-09/exclusive-meta-to-put-ai-chip-into-production-in-september-as-it-looks-to-double-computing-capacity-memo-shows))

### Cloudflare Splits AI Bot Controls Into Three Categories

Cloudflare now lets site owners separately control Search, Agent, and Training crawlers. Starting September 15, new domains will block Agent and Training crawlers by default on ad-supported pages while keeping Search allowed. A new BotBase database tracks all known bots. Available to all customers, including Free tier.

([Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319554/20260702/cloudflare-separates-ai-crawlers-purpose-opens-door-charging-them-directly.htm))

### Apple vs. OpenAI — Legal War Escalates

Apple's July 10 trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI continues to reverberate. TechCrunch published a roundup of the "wildest allegations" on July 13, including claims that OpenAI's Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan used Apple's confidential project code names during recruiting and asked candidates to bring Apple hardware components to interviews. This is now running in parallel with the xAI appeal (see above), putting OpenAI on two legal fronts simultaneously.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/the-wildest-allegations-in-apples-trade-secrets-lawsuit-against-openai/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/how-apple-s-lawsuit-threatens-to-disrupt-openai-s-bid-to-rival-the-iphone))
