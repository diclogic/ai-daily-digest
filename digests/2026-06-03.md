## Notable Papers

- **Multi-Agent Query Routing** — Routes queries to panels of specialized agents with a judge aggregator, achieving 91.2% on MMLU vs. GPT-4o's 88.7% at 10× lower cost ($0.003/query). Highlights the growing viability of mixture-of-agents approaches over single monolithic models. ([DEV Community roundup](https://dev.to/rileycraig14/20-most-impactful-ai-research-papers-right-now-curated-via-neural-search-5ab3))

- **Autonomous Agent Error Recovery** (Anthropic Research, arXiv 2026.05103) — Injects stack traces back into agent context to iterate until tests pass, achieving a 94% fix rate on Python and 87% on TypeScript in fewer than 3 iterations. Practical framework for production agent reliability. ([DEV Community roundup](https://dev.to/rileycraig14/20-most-impactful-ai-research-papers-right-now-curated-via-neural-search-5ab3))

- **Semantic Intent Memory for Agents** — Replaces keyword-based RAG retrieval with intent-based semantic embeddings for agent long-term memory, significantly improving recall for multi-session agent workflows. ([DEV Community roundup](https://dev.to/rileycraig14/20-most-impactful-ai-research-papers-right-now-curated-via-neural-search-5ab3))

## Model & Product Launches

### Anthropic: Claude Mythos Expansion (Project Glasswing)

Anthropic expanded Project Glasswing to **150 new organizations** across 15+ countries, granting them access to Claude Mythos Preview. The model has an autonomous exploit success rate of 72.4% (vs. near-zero for previous models) and has identified 23,000+ potential vulnerabilities, with ~6,000 expected to be confirmed as severe. Initial partners already used Mythos to find zero-day vulnerabilities in every major OS and browser, including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug. The EU's cybersecurity agency ENISA is among the new partners. **Anthropic says Mythos-class models will be available to all customers "in a matter of weeks."**

Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-expanding-mythos-access-to-150-new-organizations/), [Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/ai-anthropic-claude-mythos-project-glasswing-expand/821714/), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-expands-glasswing-as-it-promises-public-claude-mythos-class-model-releases/)

### Microsoft Build 2026: Seven New MAI Models + Project Polaris

Microsoft announced **Project Polaris**, its in-house mixture-of-experts coding model that will replace GPT-4 Turbo as the default in GitHub Copilot starting August 2026 — a landmark move reducing Microsoft's reliance on OpenAI. Other launches include:
- **MAI-Code-1-Flash** — rolling out to all Copilot plans now
- **MAI-Thinking-1** — Microsoft's first in-house reasoning model, trained without OpenAI data
- **MAI-Image-2.5** — handles both text-to-image and image-to-image workloads
- **Multi-Agent VS Code Extension** — lets Copilot spawn parallel subagents for complex tasks
- **Azure Agent Mesh** — federated multi-agent execution across clouds and devices

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317596/20260602/github-copilot-replaces-gpt-4-project-polaris-ships-multi-agent-vs-code-build.htm), [ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/microsoft-build-2026-recap-windows-agent-platform-project-polaris-copilot-workspace/)

### Google: Gemini 3.5 Flash Now Default

Google rolled out **Gemini 3.5 Flash** as the default model in AI Mode in Search globally and in the Gemini app, with API access at $1.50/$9.00 per million tokens. Google also announced "the biggest upgrade to its Search box in over 25 years."

Source: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/)

## Other Notable AI News

### Anthropic Files for IPO

Anthropic confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 1. Coming less than a week after raising **$65 billion in Series H** at a **$965 billion valuation**, the filing sets up what could be the first frontier AI lab to go public — potentially before OpenAI. A listing is unlikely before autumn.

Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/01/nx-s1-5843199/anthropic-ipo-filing-ai-large), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/)

### Alphabet Raises $80B for AI Infrastructure

Alphabet announced its first equity raise since 2005 — an **$80 billion offering** to fund AI compute infrastructure. The deal includes a **$10 billion private placement from Berkshire Hathaway** (Greg Abel's first major tech bet), $30B in underwritten offerings, and a $40B at-the-market program. Alphabet projects **$180–190B in capex for 2026**, with 2027 "significantly" higher.

Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/02/2026/berkshire-to-invest-10-in-alphabet-in-companys-first-raise-since-2005), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-plans-to-raise-80-billion-to-pay-for-ai-buildout/)

### Trump Signs AI Cybersecurity Executive Order

President Trump signed an executive order establishing an **AI cybersecurity clearinghouse** and asking AI companies to voluntarily submit their most powerful models for government testing up to 30 days before public release. The order explicitly bars mandatory licensing requirements. Reports indicate Anthropic's Mythos debut forced the administration to reconsider the government's role in evaluating AI risks.

Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/trump-ai-executive-order-rcna348072), [Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/trump-ai-security-executive-order/821755/)

### Florida Sues OpenAI and Sam Altman

Florida AG James Uthmeier filed an 83-page civil complaint naming Altman personally, alleging OpenAI knowingly released ChatGPT despite internal safety warnings, marketed it as safe for children without adequate safeguards, and collected minors' data without parental consent. This is the first state lawsuit of its kind against an AI company.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/florida-sues-openai-sam-altman-in-first-of-its-kind-lawsuit-over-violent-incidents/), [NPR Illinois](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-06-01/florida-sues-openai-and-sam-altman-over-alleged-safety-lapses)
