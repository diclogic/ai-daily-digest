## Notable Papers & Research

### Learning Compositional Meta-Routing for Agentic Workflows: An Executable Benchmark
*Natan Vidra, Alina Kapanova, Arun Kanhai, Spurthi Setty (submitted to AAAI 2027)*

Introduces a benchmark and method for evaluating how LLM agents should route subtasks across different models and tools in multi-step workflows — directly addressing the orchestration overhead that's become a bottleneck as agent architectures scale.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### Progressive²: Teacher-Student Progressive Co-Evolving Knowledge Distillation for Substantial Model Compression
*Tiancong Cheng, Ying Zhang, Zhiwen Yu, Yifang Yin, Bin Guo*

Proposes a co-evolving distillation method where teacher and student models improve together during compression, achieving substantial size reductions while preserving capability — relevant as inference cost becomes the dominant constraint for production LLM deployments.

([arXiv cs.LG](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current))

### Adversarial Training Without Input Gradients via Low-Rank Householder Expansions

Demonstrates adversarial robustness training that bypasses costly input-gradient computation, using low-rank Householder expansions to achieve comparable robustness at a fraction of the training cost — potentially making adversarial hardening practical for large models.

([arXiv cs.LG](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI Retires DALL·E GPT from ChatGPT (Today)

Today, August 30, OpenAI permanently retires the official DALL·E GPT inside ChatGPT, steering all users to ChatGPT Images instead. The DALL·E API for developers was already retired earlier in 2026. User-created GPTs with image generation are unaffected, but anyone with images stored via the DALL·E GPT should download them before they're gone.

([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpt/you-have-until-august-30-to-save-your-chatgpt-dall-e-images-heres-how-to-avoid-losing-them-forever) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/DALL-E-leaves-ChatGPT-on-August-30-download-your-images-first.1360522.0.html) · [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jelinda-montes/dall-e-gpt-shuts-down-august-30-download-your-images-before-the-official-tool-disappears-tomorrow/91396233))

### Meta Hatch AI Agent Platform — Launch Imminent

Meta is reportedly weeks away from launching Hatch, a consumer AI agent platform integrated into Instagram and WhatsApp. Unlike chatbot assistants, Hatch performs tasks — booking restaurants, shopping, managing calendars — across third-party services including DoorDash, Etsy, and Outlook. Meta is considering a tiered pricing model up to $199.99/month for premium usage. The platform is powered by a new model codenamed "Watermelon" due in October.

([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-plans-launch-hatch-ai-agent-platform-coming-weeks) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/metas-consumer-focused-ai-agent-could-be-weeks-from-launch/) · [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/meta-hatch-ai-agent-instagram-whatsapp))

## Other Notable AI News

### NVIDIA Posts Record $96.2B Quarter, Then Pauses Cloud Financing

NVIDIA's fiscal Q2 (ended July 26) delivered $96.2B in revenue (+106% YoY) and profit up 126%. But just days later, the company paused its controversial cloud-financing program — launched in July — that offered credit to AI cloud companies in exchange for revenue share. Employees raised concerns the arrangement could draw antitrust scrutiny. The program was live for fewer than two months.

([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-pauses-revenue-sharing-deals-223140237.html) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/nvidia-pauses-revenue-sharing-deals-ai-cloud-antitrust-082826) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/61486472/nvidia-reportedly-pauses-revenue-sharing-deals-with-ai-cloud-companies-amid-antitrust-concerns))

Following up on the August 28 ruling: Judge Rita Lin's 59-page order found the Pentagon "retaliated" against Anthropic for refusing to remove safety restrictions for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons, calling the supply-chain risk designation "illegal and baseless." A second lawsuit in D.C. over a separate designation remains pending.

([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/anthropic-pentagon-blacklist-claude-judge-rcna594825) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/08/28/federal-judge-blocks-pentagons-illegal-designation-of-anthropic-as-a-supply-chain-risk/))

### Google's A2A Protocol Joins Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

Google's Agent-to-Agent protocol formally joined the AAIF under the Linux Foundation on August 20, alongside Anthropic's MCP. Over 250 members now, including AWS, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Cloudflare, and Bloomberg. Agent interoperability standards are converging.

### Pew: 52% of Americans Now More Concerned Than Excited About AI

Pew Research (survey June 22–28, 2026): 52% of Americans are more concerned than excited about AI — up from 37% in 2021. For the first time, a majority of adults under 30 share this concern. 71% believe AI will reduce jobs within 20 years.

([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/08/18/young-adults-in-the-us-are-increasingly-wary-of-ai-concerned-it-will-take-jobs/))
