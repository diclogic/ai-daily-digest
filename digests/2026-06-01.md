## Notable Papers & Research

- **"Mean Mode Screaming" in Ultra-Deep Diffusion Transformers** — Researchers isolated a previously unnamed training instability in 1000+ layer Diffusion Transformers and proposed "Mean-Variance Split Residuals" to stabilize training at extreme depth. Important for next-gen image/video generation architectures.

- **COMPASS: Cognitive MCTS-Guided Process Alignment for Safe Search Agents** — Wenkai Shen et al. (accepted at ICML 2026). Introduces a Monte Carlo Tree Search-based alignment method for keeping AI search agents safe during autonomous web browsing.

- **SLAT: Segment-Level Adaptive Trimming for Efficient CoT Reasoning** — Jian Yao et al. (accepted at ICML 2026). Reduces the computational cost of chain-of-thought reasoning by adaptively trimming unnecessary reasoning segments, achieving comparable accuracy at significantly lower token costs.

- **LLM-Emu (University of Cambridge)** — An online emulator for LLM inference that realistically simulates LLM behavior with less than 5% absolute error, useful for benchmarking and cost estimation without running full models.

- **Planner-Centric Reinforcement Learning for Deep Research with Structure-Aware Reward** — (Accepted at ICML 2026). Applies RL to multi-step research agents with structured reward signals, improving deep research task completion.

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.8, featuring improved agentic coding (64.3% → 69.2% on SWE-bench Pro), stronger computer use (84% on Online-Mind2Web), and what the company describes as "sharper judgement and more honesty about its progress." ### GitHub Copilot Token-Based Billing — Effective Today (June 1)
GitHub Copilot switches from flat-rate to token-based billing today. Base subscription prices remain the same ($10/mo Pro, $39/mo Pro+), but now represent credit allowances rather than unlimited access. Developers report a single agentic coding session can consume $30–$40 in credits — 3–4x a Pro subscriber's entire monthly allotment. Code completions remain unlimited; the metering applies to chat, agentic workflows, and code review. The community discussion thread has 900+ downvotes.
— [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/what-a-joke-github-copilots-new-token-based-billing-spurs-consternation-among-devs/)
