## Notable Papers & Research

### Understanding Evolution Strategies for LLM Reasoning: Broader Reasoning Coverage than GRPO

Demonstrates that evolution strategies produce broader reasoning coverage in LLMs than Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), the method behind DeepSeek-R1's success. The paper shows ES-based training explores a wider solution space, reducing mode collapse in chain-of-thought reasoning.

([arXiv cs.LG](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent))

### When Does LLM Orchestration Pay Off? A Controlled Evaluation of Accuracy, Cost, and Task Difficulty

A systematic evaluation comparing single-model inference against multi-LLM orchestration pipelines (routing, cascading, ensembling), finding that orchestration only reliably improves accuracy on medium-difficulty tasks — easy tasks don't need it and hard tasks defeat it regardless.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### First Demonstration of Multi-Agent LLM System for Million-Scale Optical Link Management in Global Production AIDCs

Demonstrates a multi-agent LLM system managing over one million optical links across production AI data centers in real time — the first paper to show LLM-based agents operating at this scale in live infrastructure rather than simulation.

([arXiv cs.MA](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### a16z Launches $1.1B "Machine Age" Fund for AI Hardware

Andreessen Horowitz raised $1.1 billion for its first fund dedicated to physical AI infrastructure — chips, memory, networking, storage, data centers, robotics, and edge AI hardware. ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/28/andreessen-horowitz-raises-1-1b-ai-infrastructure-fund/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/andreessen-horowitz-raises-1-1-billion-for-ai-infrastructure-fund))

### Z.ai GLM-5.3-Flash: Open-Weight Multimodal MoE Under MIT License

Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash on August 26 — a 320B-parameter mixture-of-experts model activating only 18B parameters per token, with native text/image/video support and a 1M-token context window. MIT-licensed open weights. It ran anonymously on OpenRouter as "Ox Alpha" for twelve days before the reveal. Priced at $0.15/$0.50 per 1M input/output tokens (50% launch discount through September 9).

([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-releases-glm-5-3-flash-a-320b-a18b-natively-multimodal-moe-with-a-1m-token-context/amp/) · [explainx.ai](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/glm-5-3-flash-ox-alpha-official-launch-august-2026))

### AWS Mechanical Turk Shutting Down September 30

Amazon will shut down Mechanical Turk on September 30, ending the 21-year-old crowdsourced labor marketplace that Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence." At its peak, MTurk served over 500,000 workers; it had been in decline as Scale AI, Mercor, and Prolific captured the AI training data market.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/amazon-service-that-jeff-bezos-called-artificial-ai-is-shutting-down.html) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/amazon-mechanical-turk-shutting-down-082626))

## Other Notable AI News

### Federal Judge Rules Pentagon's Blacklisting of Anthropic Unconstitutional

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin ruled on August 28 that the Pentagon violated the First Amendment and Fifth Amendment by blacklisting Anthropic, finding the government "retaliated" against the company after it refused to remove safety restrictions on Claude for mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons. "The empty invocation of national security is not a blank check to punish and retaliate against government critics," Lin wrote in a 59-page opinion. The ruling vacated the designation and barred enforcement. A second lawsuit over a separate supply-chain risk designation remains pending in D.C.

([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/anthropic-pentagon-blacklist-claude-judge-rcna594825) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/08/28/federal-judge-blocks-pentagons-illegal-designation-of-anthropic-as-a-supply-chain-risk/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/28/judge-blocks-pentagon-anthropic-blacklist))

### Meta Fixes Smart Glasses Recording Privacy Loophole

Meta is pushing a software update to Ray-Ban smart glasses that will kill the camera if the privacy LED is covered during recording. Previously, users could start recording and then cover the indicator light to film covertly. This is the second privacy fix in under two months. Meta VP of AR Alex Himel announced the update on Threads.

([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/28/meta-ray-ban-smart-glasses-privacy-led-loophole-update/) · [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/meta-smart-glasses-recording-led-fix-3704164/))

### Greenko/AM Intelligence Orders ~9,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin Systems

India's Greenko Group subsidiary AM Intelligence placed a binding order for approximately 9,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 rack-scale systems for Q1 2027 delivery — roughly $8 billion in capex to bring 200MW online near-term, scaling toward 1GW of compute-as-a-service across India, the US, Finland, and Malaysia.

### Google's A2A Protocol Joins Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol formally joined the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation on August 20, putting it under the same neutral governance as Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP). The AAIF now counts over 250 members including AWS, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI.
