## Model & Product Launches

### GPT-5.6 Now Publicly Available

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family went live to the general public on July 9 after weeks of restricted government-vetted preview access. The family ships three variants: **Sol** (flagship — complex reasoning, coding, agentic work at $5/$30 per million tokens), **Terra** (GPT-5.5-level quality at half the cost), and **Luna** (high-volume, low-cost). Altman says Sol is 54% more token-efficient for coding tasks than previous models. The release followed what Altman described as a "collaborative back and forth" with Commerce Secretary Lutnick and Treasury Secretary Bessent.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/openai-launches-its-new-family-of-models-with-gpt-5-6/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/openai-expanding-gpt-5point6-ai-model-release-ending-government-limits.html) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/))

### Grok 4.5 — xAI's Opus-Class Competitor

SpaceXAI released Grok 4.5 on July 8, a 1.5-trillion-parameter MoE model trained on real Cursor session data. Priced at $2/$6 per million tokens — over 60% cheaper than Opus 4.8 or GPT-5.5. Elon Musk positioned it as "roughly comparable to Opus 4.7, but much faster," noting it uses ~14K output tokens per task vs. ~67K for Opus 4.8. Available in Grok Build, Cursor (all plans), and the SpaceXAI console. EU availability targeted for mid-July. A further step-change release is signaled for next month.

([SpaceXAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-grok-new-model))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — July 17 GA Still on Track

Google's next flagship remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview. Key specs: 2M-token context window (double any current frontier model), Deep Think extended reasoning mode (locked behind the $250/mo Ultra subscription). The model is six weeks late after Google scrapped the 2.5 Pro base for a ground-up architectural rebuild targeting gaps in mathematical reasoning and SVG generation. Lands into a hypercompetitive window — five days after GPT-5.6 and nine days after Grok 4.5.

([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model))

### Claude Fable 5 — Included Access Window Closed

The extended included-access window for Fable 5 on paid plans closed July 12 at 11:59 PM PT. Access now requires prepaid usage credits at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. Anthropic has said it aims to restore Fable 5 as a standard part of subscriptions once capacity allows.

### Mythos 5 — Status Unchanged

Claude Mythos 5 remains restricted to vetted U.S. partner organizations via Project Glasswing (~150 organizations across 15+ countries). Pricing starts at $10/$50 per million tokens. Mythos Preview autonomously discovered and exploited a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE vulnerability — one of the demonstrations that led to restricted distribution. No timeline for broader public access.

([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/mythos) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/))

## Notable Papers & Research

### ICML 2026 Concluded — Record Scale

ICML 2026 wrapped up in Seoul on July 11 after six days. The conference accepted 6,352 papers from 23,918 submissions (536 spotlights, 168 orals). Both Outstanding Paper Awards went to diffusion model research — the first time in recent memory that diffusion dominated both slots. The Test of Time Award went to DeepMind's A3C paper on asynchronous deep RL.

### AlphaEvolve Expanding Impact

Google DeepMind's Gemini-powered coding agent AlphaEvolve has expanded across genomics, quantum physics, mathematics, and AI infrastructure. Reported achievements include a 30% reduction in DNA sequencing error detection and 10x lower error rates in quantum circuits.

([Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research))

### Claude Science Launch

Anthropic launched Claude Science in late June / early July — a dedicated product for biologists and pharmaceutical companies targeting drug discovery, protein structure analysis, genomics, and computational biology. CEO Dario Amodei compared its potential impact to what Claude Code did for programming, though he tempered expectations: "I don't think that today we can make progress at a rate of 10 years per year."

([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-release-claude-science-ceo-dario-amodei/) · [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-ai-biotech-impact/))

## Other Notable AI News

### Apple Sues OpenAI for Trade Secret Theft

Apple filed suit in Northern California federal court on July 10, alleging that OpenAI orchestrated a coordinated campaign to steal trade secrets via hiring 400+ former Apple employees. The complaint targets OpenAI's Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan (24-year Apple veteran, former VP of iPhone/Apple Watch product design), alleging he used Apple's confidential project code names during recruiting, coached departing employees on evading security procedures, and asked candidates to bring Apple hardware components to interviews. Apple also alleges engineer Chang Liu failed to return an Apple laptop containing confidential documents after leaving for OpenAI. A stunning reversal for two companies that partnered on Apple Intelligence in 2024.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai-over-alleged-trade-secret-theft/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-10/apple-sues-openai-for-trade-secret-theft-in-blockbuster-case))

### UN Global AI Governance Dialogue

The UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance took place in Geneva on July 6-7, focused on international approaches to managing AI. Discussions centered on child safety — millions of children globally are using AI for learning and personal advice while safeguards fail to keep pace. No binding commitments emerged.

([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167862))

### OpenAI Jalapeño Inference Chip

OpenAI and Broadcom's custom Jalapeño inference chip (unveiled June 24) continues to draw attention. Built from initial design to tape-out in nine months — possibly the fastest ASIC cycle recorded in its class. Early lab results show substantially better performance-per-watt than current state-of-the-art. Initial deployment targeted for late 2026, with full ramp in 2027-2028.

([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/openai-unveils-its-first-custom-chip-built-by-broadcom/))
