# AI Daily Digest — June 28, 2026

**Top story:** The US government partially lifted its export control on Anthropic, clearing Claude Mythos 5 for redeployment to 100+ US organizations defending critical infrastructure — but the letter is pointedly silent on Fable 5, leaving Anthropic's most popular model offline for a 16th day. Meanwhile, GPT-5.6 Sol remains locked to ~20 government-approved partners with broader access now expected mid-July, and the Trump AI executive order's first 30-day deadline (July 2) is days away.

## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Mythos 5 — Partially Cleared for US Critical Infrastructure

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick wrote to Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown on June 26 citing "significant progress" in daily talks and determining "that appropriate safeguards are in place to permit certain trusted partners to access the Claude Mythos 5 Model." Key details:

- **Scope:** 100+ US companies and government agencies that operate and defend critical infrastructure — primarily cybersecurity firms, defense contractors, and federal agencies.
- **Fable 5 still offline:** Lutnick's letter is "silent on Fable 5." Sources close to the talks say negotiations are moving toward releasing Fable as well, but no timeline has been given. Fable 5 — the model most developers and subscribers are waiting for — remains suspended since June 12.
- **Background:** The export control was triggered after Amazon CEO Andy Jassy flagged a purported jailbreak to Treasury Secretary Bessent. Anthropic's updated privacy policy requiring government ID + biometrics via Persona takes effect July 8.
- **Prediction markets:** Polymarket odds for full Fable 5 restoration by July 1 have dropped to ~57%; by December 31 they remain at 96%.

The model's 2-million-token context window will be the largest in any production frontier model. No specific July date has been given.

### OpenAI Jalapeño — First Custom Inference Chip Unveiled

OpenAI and Broadcom revealed Jalapeño on June 24: a reticle-sized ASIC designed from scratch for LLM inference, co-developed in just nine months. Engineering samples are already running GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark workloads at production target frequency and power, delivering "substantially better" performance per watt than current GPUs. Initial deployment targeted for late 2026, ramping through 2027, full scale in H1 2028.

### Andrej Karpathy — Software 3.0 Framework Published

Karpathy published his Sequoia Ascent 2026 talk as a blog post, formalizing the "Software 3.0" framework: where Software 2.0 involved learned weights from datasets, Software 3.0 treats the LLM context window as the program itself — prompts, tools, memory, and examples compose the "code." He identifies December 2025 as the tipping point where agentic coding went from "helpful but messy" to consistently producing correct code. Key insight: Software 3.0 automates what humans can verify — if correctness can be checked by a test suite, game score, or formal proof, an LLM can produce it.

([karpathy.bearblog.dev](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/) · [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-andrej-karpathy-software-thesis-evolution-2026-06-17) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/60091727/andrej-karpathy-says-ai-is-no-longer-a-chatbot-its-becoming-your-teammate))

## Notable Papers

### "AOrchestra: Automating Sub-Agent Creation for Agentic Orchestration"
Accepted at **ICML 2026** (Seoul, July 6–11). Addresses the problem of automatically creating and coordinating sub-agents in multi-agent systems — directly relevant as GPT-5.6 Sol ships with "Ultra" mode sub-agent orchestration and agent frameworks proliferate.

### "AutoWebWorld: Synthesizing Infinite Verifiable Web Environments via Finite State Machines"
Accepted at **ICML 2026**. Generates synthetic web environments for training and evaluating web agents — a bottleneck for agentic AI development where real-world web interactions are expensive and non-reproducible.

### "Adaptive Reasoning in LLMs: From Post-Training to Test-Time Learning"
Selected as an **ICML 2026 tutorial**. Surveys the rapidly evolving landscape of inference-time reasoning, from chain-of-thought to test-time compute scaling — the core technique behind "thinking" modes in GPT-5.6, Claude, and Gemini.

### "ARCA: Adapter-Residual Credit Assignment When Token Signals Degenerate"
Accepted at **DEMO 2026 (ICML Workshop)**. Addresses credit assignment in adapter-based fine-tuning when token-level signals break down — practical for efficient model customization.

## Other Notable AI News

### Anthropic Accuses Alibaba of Largest Known Distillation Attack

Anthropic alleged that operators affiliated with Alibaba and its Qwen AI lab ran 28.8 million exchanges through ~25,000 fraudulent accounts between April 22 and June 5, specifically targeting Claude's software engineering and agentic reasoning capabilities to train Qwen models. Anthropic took the evidence to the White House. The attack didn't extract model weights or training data — it used Claude like a normal user at industrial scale. The timing is notable: Anthropic brought this to the government shortly before the export control directive hit.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html) · [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-claims-that-chinas-alibaba-illicitly-distilled-its-models-from-april-to-june-2026-says-effort-involved-25-000-fake-accounts-and-28-8-million-exchanges-on-claude) · [InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4189342/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-using-25000-fake-accounts-to-scrape-claude-ai.html))

### Trump AI Executive Order — July 2 Deadline Approaching

The June 2 Executive Order on "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" has its first 30-day deadline on July 2. Federal agencies must: prioritize AI-enabled cyber defense of information systems, release binding operational directives for civilian IT, and form an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" with industry. The 60-day deadline (August 1) requires a voluntary framework for pre-release federal access to frontier models — the mechanism that now governs both GPT-5.6 and Mythos releases.

([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Morrison Foerster](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260605-trump-issues-executive-order-seeking-to-promote-collaboration) · [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/assessing-trumps-executive-order-on-ai-oversight))
