## Notable Papers & Research

### Continual Capability Evolution for LLMs and Agentic AI

Zhiyan Hou, Dan Zhang, and Tao Feng propose reframing continual learning as "continual capability evolution," introducing a tri-axial framework that categorizes methods by *when*, *where*, and *how* learning occurs. Useful lens for teams building long-lived agent systems that must absorb new skills without catastrophic forgetting.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### MASS: Multiplayer World Models with Authoritative Shared State

Researchers from Alaya Lab and Peking University present an architecture separating world dynamics from view rendering through a learned, typed state layer. Eliminates cross-view inconsistencies in multi-agent simulation — relevant to both game environments and multi-agent training setups.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### Memory Reward Inflation in Self-Improving LLM Agents

New work examines how reward signals inflate when self-improving agents rely on their own memory, causing training instabilities. Submitted to NeurIPS 2026 — addresses a practical failure mode in autonomous agent loops.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI Pauses Astra Over Critical Cyber Capabilities

OpenAI disclosed on August 9 that it has paused all internal Astra activities that don't meet strengthened security controls. Internal evaluations found sharp gains in agentic coding and cybersecurity, leading OpenAI to conclude it cannot rule out "Critical" cyber capabilities — meaning the model could potentially identify zero-day exploits or execute novel cyberattacks against hardened targets autonomously. Astra is now under isolated testing, restricted network access, encrypted weights, sandboxed execution, and real-time chain-of-thought monitoring. Altman said the company plans to make Astra "generally available" but added: "Given its cyber capabilities, we need a little bit longer to do this safely."

([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/09/openai-pauses-astra-after-it-nears-first-ever-critical-cyber-risk/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/07/openai-astra-model-delay-cybersecurity-risks) · [OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/))

### Seedance 2.5 API Goes Public

ByteDance opened the Seedance 2.5 developer API on August 7 after the model launched on July 31. Generates audio and video together in a single pass, with clips up to 30 seconds and support for up to 30 image, 10 video, and 10 audio references. Available on EvoLink for text-to-video, image-to-video, and reference-to-video workflows.

([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/bytedances-seedance-2-5-generates-30-second-video-clips-with-built-in-audio/))

## Other Notable AI News

### GOLD EAGLE Cybersecurity Clearinghouse Goes Live

The White House's GOLD EAGLE initiative — an AI-driven public-private clearinghouse for vulnerability discovery, validation, and remediation across critical infrastructure — is now operational. Directed under Executive Order 14409 (signed June 2, 2026), it is implemented by Treasury, DHS/CISA, and the Department of War in collaboration with industry partners.

([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/white-house-launches-gold-eagle-initiative-for-unprecedented-cybersecurity-vulnerability-coordination/) · [K&L Gates](https://www.klgates.com/thought-leadership/GOLD-EAGLE-Takes-Flight-White-House-Launches-AI-Enabled-Cybersecurity-Clearinghouse-7-29-2026))

### AI Captures 70%+ of Global VC in Q2

AI took more than 70% of global venture funding in Q2 2026, with OpenAI and Anthropic together absorbing $217B — 43% of all venture dollars. The Genesis Mission committed $5B+ across 15+ federal agencies to embed AI in national scientific research. Harvey (legal AI) hit $8B valuation; Cerebras (wafer-scale chips) at $8.1B.

([Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/54941/2026-08-07-ai-washington-report-august-2026-edition))

### EU AI Transparency Rules Now Active

As of August 2, the EU's continent-wide rules requiring AI systems to identify themselves to humans they interact with are in force — the first regulation of its kind at continental scale.
