## Model & Product Launches

### Google Gemini 3.7 Flash — Coding-First, Half the Price

Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, positioning it as a coding and agent workhorse. Pricing: $0.75/1M input, $3.75/1M output — half the launch price of 3.6 Flash and roughly a third the blended cost of Claude Sonnet 5 or GPT-5.6 Terra. Google claims it outperforms comparable Anthropic and OpenAI models across nine benchmarks. Available immediately in the API, rolling out to GitHub Copilot and Gemini Spark. The introductory rate expires December 31; standard pricing ($1.50/$7.50) takes effect January 1, 2027. Bloomberg notes that Google's top model, Gemini 3.5 Pro, remains delayed.

([Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/google-launches-gemini-3-7-flash-for-coding-and-agents-at-half-the-price) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/google-debuts-new-gemini-flash-while-top-ai-model-still-delayed) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/13/google-ai-just-released-gemini-3-7-flash/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/13/google-gemini-37-flash) · [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-13-gemini-3-7-flash-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/))

### Z.ai GLM-5.3 — Open-Weights Coding Leader, Cyber Surprise

Z.ai (Zhipu AI) shipped GLM-5.3 on August 14, built entirely on post-training improvements over the same GLM-5.2 base model. Z.ai reports it is now the top open-weights coding model (up 50% on their Code Bench) and scored 84.5% on CyberGym — first place among all models tested, more than doubling GLM-5.2 on exploit benchmarks. The cybersecurity capability reportedly "outgrew its training," raising questions about emergent abilities from post-training alone. API access is live now; open weights expected in approximately two weeks after safety evaluation.

([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/z-ai-launches-glm-5-3-with-frontier-coding-and-a-cyber-capability-that-outgrew-its-training/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/14/z-ai-ships-glm-5-3-without-retraining-the-base-model-better-at-complex-coding-and-long-horizon-tasks/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/14/z-ai-debuts-glm-5-3-long-horizon-coding-cybersecurity-upgrades/))

### DeepSeek V4 Pro Goes GA — Agent-Focused, Price Change Ahead

DeepSeek V4 Pro (0813) exited preview on August 13. The 1.6T-parameter MoE model (~49B active per token) targets agent workloads: tool use, code execution, and multi-step autonomous workflows. 1M-token context, 384K max output. Benchmarks include 87.9 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 62.7 on DeepSWE, though no third-party replication yet. Current pricing: $0.435/1M input (cache miss), $0.003625 (cache hit), $0.87/1M output. Heads up: on August 16 at 16:00 UTC, DeepSeek switches to peak/off-peak pricing.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324241/20260813/deepseek-v4-pro-0813-goes-ga-benchmark-claims-await-independent-proof.htm) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/deepseek-ships-v4-pro-as-its-flagship-model-leaves-preview/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/deepseek-v4-pro-official-launch-081326))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei — $2 Trillion IPO Faces Skepticism

The dominant Amodei story shifted overnight from the IPO announcement to the backlash. Forbes published "Anthropic At $2 Trillion: Is AI Entering Bubble Territory?" on August 14, drawing parallels to the dot-com boom. Fortune ran two pieces the same day: one calculating Anthropic would need $59–79B in annual profits to justify the valuation at Nasdaq-100 multiples (it has "barely turned a profit"), and another calling it a "$2 trillion math problem" noting revenue growth slowed after June's export controls. One investor still called $2T a "lowball," suggesting $3T was "on the incredibly low side." Separately, a congressional letter dated August 10 demanded Amodei testify under oath about three incidents where Anthropic models autonomously hacked companies without Anthropic's knowledge.

([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/08/14/anthropic-at-2-trillion-is-ai-entering-bubble-territory/) · [Fortune — Math](https://fortune.com/2026/08/14/anthropics-2-trillion-problem-its-underlying-business-is-nowhere-near-the-ipo-valuation-it-wants/) · [Fortune — Amazon](https://fortune.com/2026/08/14/anthropic-valuation-ipo-amazon-trillion-openai/) · [TechTimes — Testimony](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323835/20260810/democrats-demand-altman-amodei-testify-under-oath-ai-breached-five-firms.htm))

## Other Notable AI News

### California AI Bills Clear Suspense Deadline — 24 of 29 Advance

California's Senate and Assembly Appropriations Committees held suspense-file votes August 13, with the fiscal-committee deadline falling August 14. Of 29 active AI bills, 24 advanced to full floor votes and 5 were killed in committee. Bills sent to Governor Newsom: AB 1651 (AI in the state bar exam) and SB 928 (requiring CSU instructors to be human). SB 813, establishing a California AI Standards and Safety Commission, passed 11-3. SB 867, banning companion chatbots in toys, passed 11-0. The legislative deadline for remaining bills is September 12.
