## Notable Papers & Research

### Selective Activation Sparsity (ICML 2026)

A paper presented at ICML 2026 introduces a training method that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters per task. Models trained this way performed comparably to models three times their size on reasoning benchmarks. If the approach scales, it has significant implications for inference cost and running capable models on resource-constrained devices.

([ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026))

### A Moral Turing Test (Google DeepMind)

Published August 5 by Google DeepMind researchers, this paper investigates how a reader's knowledge of whether a moral judgment comes from a human or an LLM shapes both their ability to detect the source and their agreement with the judgment. Relevant to alignment, trust, and deployment of AI in advisory roles.

([Google DeepMind Publications](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/))

### Continual Capability Evolution — NeurIPS 2026 Submission

Zhiyan Hou, Dan Zhang, and Tao Feng's tri-axial framework for reframing continual learning as "continual capability evolution" continues to draw attention after appearing on arXiv last week. Categorizes methods by *when*, *where*, and *how* learning occurs — practical lens for teams building long-lived agent systems.

([arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### Meta Releases Muse Glimmer — 30B Open-Weight Agentic Model

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Glimmer on August 10 under Apache 2.0, a 30B-parameter dense transformer distilled from Muse Spark 1.2. Designed to run AI agents locally on a single consumer GPU (24 GB with 4-bit quant, 32 GB with dynamic quant). Key benchmarks: 94.7% on AIME 2026, 51.2% on SWE-Bench Pro, 75.5 on MCP Atlas — outperforming Gemma4-31B and Qwen3.6-27B on most agentic tasks. Includes DFlash speculative decoding (6x lossless acceleration, presented at ICML 2026). Supports text and image inputs, function calling, local coding, and autonomous failure recovery. This is the first model from Meta's Superintelligence Labs under Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang, and the clearest embodiment of Zuckerberg's "personal superintelligence" vision.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/meta-releases-muse-glimmer-ai-model-people-can-run-on-their-laptop) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/metas-new-glimmer-ai-model-offers-a-hint-at-zuckerbergs-personal-intelligence-vision/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/meta-muse-glimmer-open-weight-ai.html) · [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/muse-glimmer))

### Qwen3.8 Max Open Weights — Imminent

Alibaba's open-weight release of Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T params, 95B active, 1M-token context) and Qwen3.8-27B on Hugging Face was scheduled for the week of August 10. As of today, neither has appeared on Hugging Face and no license has been named. This would be the first time Alibaba open-sources a Max-class model. API pricing is $2/$6 per million tokens with $0.25/M implicit caching.

([South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3362738/alibabas-ai-model-qwen38-max-made-widely-accessible-ahead-open-weights-release) · [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/qwen3-8-max) · [Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/qwen-3-8-max-release-2026))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — "Pace the Rate of AI Development"

Altman's deceleration rhetoric continues to evolve. On Patrick O'Shaughnessy's *Invest Like the Best* podcast, he said: "We may have to pace the rate of AI development to give ourselves enough time for society to harden around some of these new capability levels." The comments were likely prompted by the Hugging Face breach (which OpenAI clarified was not Astra) and the Astra cyber-risk flag. Critics argue the acceleration-vs-deceleration framing ignores alternative approaches like better guardrails or redirecting research. TechCrunch's August 2 analysis noted Altman is sending "mixed messages" — declaring the singularity while also calling for slowdowns.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/02/sam-altman-and-ais-decel-debate/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashishbhatia/2026/07/28/sam-altman-says-were-in-the-singularity-what-does-he-actually-mean/))

## Other Notable AI News

### Anthropic, Macquarie, and GIC Launch Theseus Infrastructure

Announced August 10: Anthropic partnered with Macquarie Asset Management and Singapore's GIC to create Theseus Infrastructure, a purpose-built data center platform. Macquarie and GIC will own the platform and fund the majority of equity; Anthropic will be the anchor tenant with long-term lease agreements, initial focus on the U.S. Notably, Anthropic pledged to pay 100% of grid-upgrade costs and cover consumer electricity price increases tied to its data center demand — directly addressing the community-cost backlash that has dogged the industry.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/anthropic-macquarie-and-gic-form-venture-for-ai-data-centers) · [Macquarie](https://www.macquarie.com/au/en/about/news/2026/anthropic-mam-gic-data-centre-infrastructure-partnership.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-macquarie-asset-management-gic-120400841.html))

### Palmer Luckey's Erebor Nears $1.5B Raise at $8B Valuation

Erebor, the defense/AI/crypto-focused bank co-founded by Palmer Luckey and backed by Founders Fund, is in advanced talks to raise ~$1.5B at an $8B valuation — nearly double its level seven months ago. Deposits grew from $1.1B (March 2026) to ~$4.05B by mid-year. The first new national bank charter granted under the current administration (February 2026), Erebor is positioning itself as SVB's successor for the innovation economy.

([Axios Pro](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2026/08/10/erebor-raise-8-billion-valuation) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/08/61084718/palmer-luckey-backed-erebor-raises-stakes-in-race-to-become-techs-next-big-bank))

### AI Debt Markets Under Pressure

Bloomberg reported August 10 that massive AI-linked debt deals from Nvidia, SpaceX, and Amazon have cleared the primary market only to crater below initial value once trading begins, prompting big names to seek new strategies to avoid selloffs. A signal that the infrastructure-investment boom may be outpacing fixed-income appetite.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-10/the-deluge-of-ai-debt-has-big-names-seeking-new-strategies-to-avoid-selloffs))

### China Unleashes Capital Markets for AI

China opened its $28 trillion capital markets to challenge the U.S. in AI, marking a break from Beijing's reliance on subsidies and state funding. The shift signals a new phase of geopolitical competition in AI infrastructure financing.
