## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Fable 5 — Free Access Window Now Closed

The third and final extension of Anthropic's free Fable 5 promotional access ended at 11:59 PM PT on July 19. Starting today, all Fable 5 usage requires prepaid credits at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, on top of existing plan costs. Anthropic has said Fable 5 will return to subscription plans "when it has enough compute," but no timeline has been given. The promotion had allowed up to 50% of weekly usage limits at no additional cost on Pro, Max, Team, and eligible Enterprise plans.

([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320528/20260715/claude-fable-5-free-window-ends-sunday-gpt-56-sol-closes-benchmark-gap.htm) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/07/13/claude-fable-5-extends-to-july-19-7-days-7-power-moves/))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Third Missed Deadline, August Now Rumored

Three days after its reported July 17 launch target, Google still has not published an official model card, benchmarks, or pricing for Gemini 3.5 Pro. The model is not listed in Google's public API. Reports indicate Google scrapped the near-ready model and ordered a ground-up pre-training restart after engineers found structural failures in recursive tool-calling and SVG generation. Prediction markets now show 73% probability for an August 7 delivery. Some reports suggest Google may skip 3.5 Pro entirely and focus on Gemini 4.0 Flash — which would make Google the only major frontier lab without a 2026 flagship in general production.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320736/20260716/rebuilt-gemini-35-pro-misses-third-deadline-google-eyes-stopgap-release.htm) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/16/gemini-3-5-pro-delays/) · [Geeky Gadgets](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/gemini-3-5-pro-delayed-again/))

## Notable Papers & Research

### 170,927 AI Papers Reveal H1 2026 Research Shifts

A meta-analysis of all 170,927 AI papers published in H1 2026 reveals major research shifts: agent architectures and tool use have overtaken pure scaling as the dominant theme, while inference-time compute and efficiency papers have surged. The analysis confirms the industry narrative that 2026 marks the transition from "bigger models" to "smarter inference."

([Medium](https://medium.com/@aipapers/170-927-ai-papers-reveal-the-biggest-research-shifts-of-the-first-half-of-2026-f26f3b792824))

### ResearcherBench: Can AI Be a Research Partner? (GAIR-NLP)

A new benchmark evaluating whether AI systems can act as genuine scientific research partners rather than just paper summarizers. Contains 65 expert-curated questions across 34 AI research subjects, with responses from 11 AI systems and a dual evaluation framework (rubric + factual assessment).

([arXiv 2507.16280](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.16280) · [GitHub](https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/ResearcherBench))

### Life After Benchmark Saturation (CORE-Bench)

A case study examining what happens when AI benchmarks become saturated — a growing problem as frontier models top out on standard evaluations. Proposes frameworks for creating next-generation benchmarks that remain meaningful as models improve.

([arXiv 2606.26158](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26158))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei — Tempers His Own Biotech Predictions

In a notable shift, Amodei walked back his bold 2024 "Machines of Loving Grace" prediction that AI could compress a decade of biotech progress into one year. Speaking at a recent event alongside the launch of Claude Science, he said: "I don't think that today we can make progress at a rate of 10 years per year." He now estimates the visible biotech transformation may be a decade away. Separately, FEC filings this week confirmed his $1M donation to Public First, an AI regulation super PAC.

([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-ai-biotech-impact/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/dario-amodei-anthropic-employees-give-millions-to-regulate-ai))

### Demis Hassabis — Washington Lobbying Tour for AI Watchdog

Hassabis continues his push for a FINRA-style AI standards body, meeting with Washington officials this past week. His July 14 manifesto, "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age," proposes a US-led public-private body that would test frontier models up to 30 days before release. He wants it operational "before year-end." Both Altman and Musk called the proposal "thoughtful." Meanwhile, DeepMind launched a bioresilience program with Isomorphic Labs to help governments detect and respond to biological threats, building on AlphaFold's protein-mapping capabilities.

([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind) · [Axios - Biosecurity](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/16/google-deepmind-biosecurity-safety))
