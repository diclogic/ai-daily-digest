## Notable Papers

### "QMFOL: Benchmarking LLM Reasoning via Quantifiable Monadic First-Order Logic"
Xinyi Zheng et al. introduce an automated benchmark generator that tests LLMs on formal logical reasoning through first-order logic test cases, providing a more rigorous evaluation of reasoning capabilities than natural-language benchmarks. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### "Robust Shielding for Safe Reinforcement Learning"
Proposes verified safety shields that can be composed with RL policies to guarantee constraint satisfaction without sacrificing task performance, bridging the gap between formal verification and practical RL deployment. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

### "Beyond Accuracy: Measuring Logical Compliance of Predictive Models"
Guillaume Olivier Delplanque et al. present a framework for evaluating whether ML model predictions are logically consistent with known domain constraints, not just statistically accurate — relevant for safety-critical deployments. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Other Notable AI News

### Pew: Only 16% of Americans See AI as Net Positive
A Pew Research survey (released June 17) found only 16% of Americans expect AI to benefit society over the next 20 years, while 40% predict a negative impact. 67% doubt the government will regulate AI effectively. Notably, under-30s are the most pessimistic cohort. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/only-16-percent-of-americans-think-ai-will-have-a-positive-impact-on-society-a-new-study-shows/))

### Reuters: 10% of People Now Use AI Chatbots for News
The Reuters Institute's 2026 Digital News Report, based on ~100K survey responses across 45 markets, found weekly AI chatbot usage for news has risen to 10% (from 7% in 2025). South Korea and Turkey lead at 14%; the US trails at 6%. Only 1% cite AI as their *main* news source. ([Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary) · [GIJN](https://gijn.org/stories/2026-reuters-institute-digital-news-report/))

### VivaTech 2026 Wraps in Paris
The 10th edition of VivaTech (June 17–20) closes today with Jensen Huang's GTC Paris keynote covering AI factories, agentic AI, and physical AI as Europe's next industrial play. The conference theme — "Impact, Not Illusion" — underscored the shift from AI hype to deployment. ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318178/20260610/nvidia-gtc-paris-keynote-headlines-vivatech-june-17-20ai-factories-sovereign-ai-robotics-europe.htm) · [ActuIA](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/vivatech-2026-kickoff-of-the-10th-edition-with-ai-as-the-red-thread/))
