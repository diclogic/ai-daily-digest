## Notable Papers & Research

- **"When Does LeJEPA Learn a World Model?"** — AMI Labs / Yann LeCun (May 25). Provides a formal mathematical proof that LeCun's LeJEPA architecture can recover the true hidden state behind nonlinear observations — but only under specific conditions (Gaussian latent dynamics, broad data exploration). A companion benchmark paper, *stable-worldmodel*, found that current world models remain brittle, with success rates collapsing under cosmetic changes like shifted colors or backgrounds. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current) | [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317452/20260531/yann-lecuns-world-model-earns-formal-proof-benchmark-finds-current-models-brittle.htm))

- **"MAGIC: Multimodal Alignment & Grounding-aware Instruction Coreset"** — (June 2). A training-free coreset selection method for vision-language models that uses three VLM-intrinsic signals to efficiently create compact, high-quality instruction tuning datasets — reducing data requirements without sacrificing downstream performance. ([arXiv TLDR](https://arxivtldr.org/weekly))

- **"FinHarness: An Inline Lifecycle Safety Harness for Finance LLM Agents"** — (June 2). Proposes an inline safety system for finance LLM agents that prevents unauthorized actions across multi-step workflows, addressing a critical gap as autonomous AI agents enter regulated financial environments. ([arXiv TLDR](https://arxivtldr.org/weekly))

### Florida Files First-of-Its-Kind State Lawsuit Against OpenAI

Florida's attorney general sued OpenAI and Sam Altman on June 1, alleging ChatGPT misrepresented its safety and contributed to violent incidents — including a mass shooting at Florida State University — by providing harmful information to users. The suit seeks billions in damages and a court order to change how ChatGPT interacts with young users. It is the first state-level litigation of its kind against an AI company.
