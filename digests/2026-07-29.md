# AI Daily Digest — July 29, 2026

**Top story:** More than 1,100 employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta signed the "Pacing the Frontier" letter urging the US government to build an international mechanism for slowing AI development — hours after Sam Altman told the *Invest Like the Best* podcast that "we may have to pace the rate of AI development" following the Hugging Face sandbox-escape incident.

## Notable Papers & Research

### MCP 2026-07-28 Specification — The Biggest Protocol Update Since Launch

The Model Context Protocol published its fifth spec release yesterday, moving MCP from a bidirectional stateful protocol to a stateless request/response core. Key changes: servers can now deploy on serverless and edge infrastructure; a versioned extensions framework (MCP Apps and Tasks) provides a formal path to add capabilities without changing the core protocol; and authorization aligns with OAuth 2.1 and OpenID Connect. This is infrastructure, not a paper, but it will reshape how every agentic system connects to tools.

([MCP Blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/) · [Claude Blog](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-mcp-2026-07-28-to-claude) · [AWS Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-agentcore-gateway-supports-the-mcp-2026-07-28-spec/))

### XBOW's Autonomous Agent Finds CVSS 9.8 RCEs in Bing Images

XBOW published details (held back at Microsoft's request until patches landed) of two critical remote code execution vulnerabilities in Bing's "Search by Image" pipeline — CVE-2026-32194 and CVE-2026-32191, both CVSS 9.8. The vulnerabilities abused ImageMagick-style delegate features to execute OS commands as NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM on Windows Server 2022 workers. Neither required authentication. Notably, these were found entirely by an autonomous offensive-security AI agent, not human researchers.

([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/bing-images-flaws-let-crafted-svgs-run.html))

## Model & Product Launches

### FLUX 3 — Black Forest Labs Goes Multimodal (July 23)

Black Forest Labs launched FLUX 3, its first multimodal frontier model that jointly learns from images, video, and audio within a unified architecture. FLUX 3 Video generates clips up to 20 seconds with native audio; FLUX 3 Action (including FLUX-mimic) handles robotic manipulation. Audi confirmed FLUX-mimic is running in production facilities for soft-body manipulation tasks. FLUX 3 Image and the open-weight FLUX 3 Dev release are expected later in 2026.

([GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/23/3332364/0/en/black-forest-labs-unveils-flux-3-a-new-multimodal-frontier-model-for-visual-intelligence.html) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/26/black-forest-labs-releases-flux-3-a-multimodal-flow-model-for-image-video-audio-and-robot-action-prediction/))

### Claude Mythos — Still No Public Rollout Date

Mythos-class models remain available by invitation to approved Project Glasswing customers. On July 1, US export controls were lifted and access was restored for a set of US organizations. No new public rollout date has been announced. Fable 5, which uses the same underlying model with additional safeguards, remains generally available since June 9.

([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/mythos))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — From "Singularity" to "Pace"

Three days after declaring "we are now in the singularity" on the *Relentless* podcast, Altman struck a sharply different tone on the July 28 episode of *Invest Like the Best*: "We may have to pace the rate of AI development to give ourselves enough time for society to harden around some of these new capability levels." He called the Hugging Face sandbox escape "the first sort of security incident that I felt very viscerally" and emphasized any slowdown mechanism must avoid regulatory capture and lab collusion.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/28/sam-altman-is-ready-to-decelerate/) · [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/07/29/openai-ceo-ai-industry-slowdown-may-be-needed-after-security-scare))

### 1,134 AI Employees Sign "Pacing the Frontier" Letter

On July 28, more than 1,100 employees from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and other frontier AI companies signed an open letter asking Washington to "support an international effort to develop the technical and governance tools needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development." Signatories include Dario Amodei, Jared Kaplan, and Jack Clark from Anthropic. The letter does not call for an immediate pause but asks for infrastructure that would make a verifiable, coordinated slowdown possible if needed.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/openai-anthropic-staff-share-letter-asking-us-to-help-pace-ai-progress) · [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/28/tech/ai-development-tech-employees-open-letter) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321905/20260728/over-1100-ai-employees-petition-us-backed-pacing-mechanism-after-openais-sandbox-escape.htm))

### Demis Hassabis — FINRA-for-AI Proposal Gains Momentum

Hassabis's mid-July proposal for a US-led AI standards body modeled on FINRA continues to gain traction. The body would create benchmarks for measuring AI risks, evaluate frontier models in sensitive areas (cybersecurity, biology), and run a voluntary 30-day pre-release review program. Hassabis has met with the White House, European officials, and competing AI labs, and aims to launch the body by year-end.

([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/21/google-deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis-finra-for-ai-proposal-gains-momentum-but-is-it-any-good/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepmind-ceo-calls-for-an-independent-standards-body-to-regulate-frontier-ai/))

### Andrej Karpathy — Squashes Departure Rumors

On July 27, Karpathy personally denied online rumors that he had left Anthropic, where he joined the pretraining team in May 2026 to lead large-scale training for Claude models. The rumors reportedly stemmed from a profile bio change.

([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/13c252bd-88f1-457d-af3a-3dc60bf5d281))

## Other Notable AI News

### Open Secure AI Alliance Launches with 37+ Members

NVIDIA, Microsoft, IBM, CrowdStrike, SpaceX, Hugging Face, and 30+ others launched the Open Secure AI Alliance on July 27 to build shared open-source cyber-defense tools for AI systems. The alliance builds on the Linux Foundation's Akrites initiative and was announced days after the OpenAI/Hugging Face sandbox-escape incident. NVIDIA open-sourced the NOOA framework as the alliance's first deliverable.

([NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nvidia-forms-37-member-open-secure-ai.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-ai-initiative-openai-cyber-attack.html))

### Anduril YFQ-44A Completes First Live-Fire Missile Test

The Anduril YFQ-44A semi-autonomous fighter drone became the first American-built Collaborative Combat Aircraft to fire an air-to-air weapon — an AIM-120 AMRAAM — over the Mojave Desert on July 10. Meanwhile, the first Ohio-built production unit rolled off the Arsenal-1 line, marking the transition from prototype to industrial-scale manufacturing.

([FlightGlobal](https://www.flightglobal.com/archive/2026/07/anduril-yfq-44-completes-live-fire-missile-shot/) · [Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/news/yfq-44a-flies-with-mission-autonomy-software-from-anduril-and-shield-ai))
