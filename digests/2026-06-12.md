## Notable Papers & Research

- **"Traxia: A Framework for Verifiable, Agent-Native Scientific Publishing"** — Published June 9. Proposes infrastructure where autonomous AI research agents can publish verifiable papers, build reputational identities, and peer-review each other's work within a shared provenance framework alongside human researchers. An important step toward formalizing the role of AI agents in the scientific process. ([arXiv:2606.08256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08256))

- **"Coupling Language Models with Physics-based Simulation for Synthesis of Inorganic Materials"** — Accepted at ICML 2026. Bridges LLMs with physics-based simulation for inorganic materials synthesis, demonstrating that language models can propose viable synthesis routes when grounded in physical constraints. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

- **"EnergyMamba: An Uncertainty-Aware Graph-Enhanced Selective State Space Model for Energy Consumption Prediction"** — Accepted at ICML 2026. Introduces a selective state-space architecture enhanced with graph attention and uncertainty quantification for energy forecasting, outperforming transformers on standard benchmarks. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current))

- **"Probe Before You Edit: Probing-Guided Molecular Optimization for LLM Agents in Structure-Based Drug Design"** — Uses probing to guide LLM agents in molecular editing for drug design, achieving more targeted modifications than unguided generation approaches. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei — Workers' Rights, UBI, and Self-Imposed AI Tax

Amodei's June 11 essay and the $350M commitment represent his most concrete economic proposals yet. He predicted AI could deliver 5-10% annual GDP growth while causing 10-20% unemployment and displacing half of entry-level white-collar jobs in coding, finance, and law. His graduated policy prescription: wage insurance and pro-employment incentives first, escalating to UBI funded by higher capital gains taxes or direct AI company taxes if displacement becomes "severe and permanent." He also called for mandatory government power to block deployment of unsafe models with third-party audits above a compute threshold. ([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/11/2026/anthropics-amodei-calls-for-workers-rights-over-ai) | [ABC](https://abc11.com/post/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-calls-stronger-regulation-ai-exclusive/19275685/))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump Signs AI Executive Order (June 2)

The "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" EO establishes a voluntary framework for frontier model developers to give the government early access (up to 30 days) before releasing to partners. Creates an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse, directs federal system hardening, and expands cybersecurity specialist hiring. Notably, it expressly prohibits mandatory licensing or pre-clearance for AI model development or distribution. ([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/trump-executive-order-ai.html))
