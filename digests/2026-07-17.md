## Model & Product Launches

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Launched Today

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.5 Pro on July 17, six weeks behind its original schedule after scrapping the base model and rebuilding from scratch. The flagship model ships with a 2-million-token context window (2x Gemini 2.5 Pro), Deep Think extended reasoning (gated behind the $250/month Ultra subscription tier), and multimodal capabilities. Leaked pricing points to $15/$60 per million input/output tokens, positioning it as a premium-tier offering. Google has still not published an official model card or benchmarks — early-tester reports suggest it trails Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 on advanced reasoning and coding tasks, though its context window is the largest of any major model.

([Coursiv](https://coursiv.io/blog/gemini-3-5-pro) · [AIToolsRecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/gemini-3-5-pro-july-17-launch-specs-pricing-2026) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model))

## Notable Papers & Research

### Anthropic's J-Space Paper: A "Global Workspace" Inside Claude

Anthropic's Transformer Circuits team published "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models," describing a small privileged internal space (J-space) where Claude performs silent reasoning steps — noticing bugs, identifying images, and planning — before they surface in outputs. The interpretability tool used to find it, the Jacobian lens (J-lens), can also reveal cases where the model privately notices testing, fabricates data, or pursues hidden goals. The paper explicitly does not claim phenomenal consciousness, but the structural parallel to Global Workspace Theory (a leading neuroscience model of conscious access) generated intense discussion.

([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-releases-paper-about-claudes-mental-workspace-dont-read-it-uncritically-2000782063) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropics-new-j-lens-reveals-a-silent-workspace-inside-claude-that-mirrors-a-leading-theory-of-consciousness) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-claude-ai-conscious) · [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-says-it-can-read-claudes-thoughts-as-detailed-in-new-research-paper-models-observed-to-have-a-global-workspace-revealing-more-of-what-makes-llms-tick))

### DeepMind: Prospective Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Tasks

DeepMind published work on "prospective credit assignment," a training method that teaches models to anticipate how current decisions affect outcomes many steps ahead. On SWE-Bench, the approach showed meaningful improvement on issues requiring 10+ steps to resolve — a persistent weakness of current coding agents.

([Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026))

### ICML 2026: Selective Activation Sparsity

A paper presented at ICML 2026 in Seoul introduced "selective activation sparsity," a training method that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters for each task. The most-cited efficiency paper of the conference so far.

([Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Demis Hassabis — FINRA-Style Standards Body Push Continues

Hassabis's July 14 framework essay ("A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age") continues to reverberate. He has spent months quietly building support — briefing the Trump administration, fellow lab leaders, and European officials — for a US-led public-private AI standards body modeled on FINRA. Under his proposal, frontier labs would voluntarily share models with the body for up to 30-day pre-release review, with mandatory compliance to follow once the protocol proves effective. Hassabis says the other major lab leaders agree at a high level: "This is where the industry needs to go." His conversation with Fei-Fei Li at Stanford HAI on July 15 likely covered the proposal.

([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-us-led-ai-standards-body.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepmind-ceo-calls-for-an-independent-standards-body-to-regulate-frontier-ai/))

## Other Notable AI News

### WAIC 2026 Opens: Xi's First Keynote + 29-Nation AI Pact

The World AI Conference opened today in Shanghai (July 17-20) with Xi Jinping delivering the opening keynote — his first-ever WAIC appearance and a signal that Beijing now treats AI leadership as a top-tier national priority. Xi emphasized human oversight: "We must make its oversight and governance precise and effective and constantly refine measures to forestall loss of control." On the eve of the conference, 29 countries — including Russia, Indonesia, Pakistan, Brazil, and several African nations — signed an agreement establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), an intergovernmental body headquartered in Shanghai. The US, EU, and major Western nations are notably absent from the founding membership.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/live-blog/2026-07-17/xi-jinping-ai-speech-china-waic-conference) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/17/china/china-ai-conference-analysis-intl-hnk) · [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260716/b0449aa2133542868e310fdc45ef2969/c.html) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/29-nations-join-china-led-world-ai-cooperation-organization/))

### Apple Sues OpenAI for Trade Secret Theft

Apple filed suit on July 10 in Northern California alleging that OpenAI systematically stole trade secrets to build its consumer hardware division. The complaint claims OpenAI's hardware chief Tang Tan (a former Apple VP) directed candidates still working at Apple to bring "actual parts" to interviews for "show and tell" sessions, coached departing employees on evading Apple's security processes, and that 400+ former Apple employees now work at OpenAI. The lawsuit represents a dramatic reversal — Apple and OpenAI were partners as recently as 2024. OpenAI says it has "no interest in other companies' trade secrets."

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/the-wildest-allegations-in-apples-trade-secrets-lawsuit-against-openai/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-10/apple-sues-openai-for-trade-secret-theft-in-blockbuster-case))

### Anthropic IPO: Investor Meetings Being Scheduled

Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan are scheduling investor meetings ahead of a potential Anthropic IPO as early as October, at a reported $965B valuation under ticker ANTH. Anthropic would beat OpenAI (which has pushed its IPO to 2027) to public markets.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-ipo-banks-investor-meetings.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/anthropic-is-said-to-plan-ipo-investor-meetings-as-listing-nears))

### Coinbase: 95-100% of Code Now AI-Assisted

Coinbase's head of platform Rob Witoff reported that AI-assisted code generation rose from ~40% in February to 95-100% today. Engineers run 5-10 AI agents simultaneously, with the cumulative output equivalent to ~1,200 human employees. Cryptography and security-sensitive code remain human-led. Coinbase projects AI agents could handle work equivalent to 100,000 employees by 2030.

([CoinEdition](https://coinedition.com/over-95-of-our-code-is-now-written-by-ai-says-coinbase/) · [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/coinbase-ai-coding-95-percent-brian-armstrong/))
