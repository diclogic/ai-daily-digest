## Notable Papers & Research

### StreamEval — the first benchmark for video models you can interrupt
*StreamEval Team* ([arXiv:2610.28402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28402), [code](https://github.com/StreamEval/StreamEval))
A benchmark for *interactive streaming* video generation: 300 scripted sessions of 15–300 seconds, each with timed user requests arriving mid-generation, scored on ten metrics across five tasks (digital-human dialogue, avatar performance, world generation, instruction editing, reference editing). **37 models** evaluated. The headline finding is the useful one: high visual quality and identity retention did **not** predict whether a model actually executed the incoming request or held state over the session, and instruction-following was weakest in avatar performance and world generation — the two tasks closest to what world-model vendors demo. Reported leaders: Vidu S2 Avatar, MemFlow (world generation), Vidu S2 Editing. Apache 2.0 harness with a public leaderboard. *Caveat:* a third-party summary attributes the work to Tsinghua, NVIDIA and CMU; the arXiv listing credits only "StreamEval Team," so treat affiliations as unconfirmed.

### Looking Ahead — training video models to stop taking the shortcut
*Ruopeng Gao, Yukun Huang, Jiarui Hu, Shuai Wang, Wenbo Hu, Ying Shan, Limin Wang (Nanjing University, Tencent ARC Lab, Zhejiang University)* ([arXiv:2610.29989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29989), submitted Oct 21; [code](https://github.com/TencentARC/Looking-Ahead))
The argument: temporal redundancy means teacher-forced training lets an autoregressive video model predict the next frame from local cues without ever learning the dynamics. Their fix, **Future Chunk Prediction (FCP)**, is an auxiliary objective that predicts a *later* chunk from the current chunk's intermediate features while hiding the intervening ground-truth frames. Author-reported gains on Physics-IQ physical plausibility: **+11.2% (I2V)** and **+9.4% (V2V)**, with **57.9% less compute** and **63.4% less data**. Weights released for 1.3B and 5B text/image-to-video models. All numbers are the authors' own and unverified — but the release of weights at two scales makes this checkable, which is more than most physics claims on this beat offer.

### Cache Engineering for Agents — stop re-prefilling, start masking
*Tianyu Fu, Ruoxi Wang, Zihan Min, Zhongqi Ren, Zhihao Li, Tengxuan Liu, Shengxu Mu, Hanling Zhang, Yu Wang* ([arXiv:2610.28577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28577), submitted Oct 20; [code](https://github.com/thu-nics/AKV))
Context engineering trims or summarizes an agent's history, which forces the retained context to be re-prefilled. The authors propose doing it at the KV level instead: exclude selected cache entries from future attention and reuse every retained KV state with no re-prefill, served by a co-designed system called **AKV**. Reported: a tool-response rolling-drop policy gains **+1.7 points** average benchmark score at **27.7% lower cost** than threshold-triggered summarization; AKV delivers up to **1.2× output throughput** and up to **1.5× better P95 latency**; recursively self-improved cache policies shave a further **7.1%**. Affiliations (Tsinghua, CUHK) come from a third-party summary, not the arXiv page.

### Microsoft ties the ConnectWise supply-chain compromise to a China state-aligned group running an agentic pentest framework
**October 21–22.** Microsoft Threat Intelligence attributed last month's **ConnectWise Cloud Backup** multi-tenant compromise to **Storm-0940**, a China state-aligned actor, and says it used **ARTEX** — a multi-agent, LLM-driven penetration-testing framework that runs reconnaissance through exfiltration with minimal human input — to automate attacks against hundreds of organizations globally. Reported scale: sign-in attempts against **2,250+ tenants** at peaks of **2,900 attempts per minute**, with privileged cloud access reached in **under three hours** from initial targeting in some cases. Microsoft did not say how many customers were actually compromised, and CyberScoop notes successful authentication is not proof of data access. ConnectWise temporarily shut services down and is restoring in phases.

**Why it matters:** this is the first major attribution naming a specific agentic framework as the automation layer of a state-aligned supply-chain intrusion — the concrete counterpart to the abstract agent-containment debate, and the exact scenario Hassabis called a warning shot the same week. ARTEX's own history is the uncomfortable part: an open-source AGPL-3.0 project (~2.0k stars) whose developer took it **private on October 8** after Zscaler documented its use against South Korean financial institutions, in a campaign that exfiltrated PII on roughly **67,000** people. Forks and pre-privatization mirrors remain on GitHub. ([CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/microsoft-attributes-connectwise-supply-chain-attack-china-storm-0940/), [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/microsoft-storm-0940-connectwise-supply-chain-attack), [Microsoft Threat Intelligence on X](https://x.com/MsftSecIntel/status/2112975078266716390), [Zscaler](https://www.zscaler.com/blogs/security-research/threat-actor-abuses-agentic-ai-pentesting-framework-target-south-korean))

**[StreamEval/StreamEval](https://github.com/StreamEval/StreamEval)** — Apache 2.0 harness and leaderboard for the interactive-streaming video benchmark (arXiv:2610.28402); the paper's own description is "the first-ever interaction streaming video generation benchmark." **Why it's trending:** it topped Hugging Face's October 22 Daily Papers page, and it is the instrument the world-model and digital-human vendors will be measured by — 37 models already scored, with the finding that visual quality doesn't predict instruction-following. **[thu-nics/AKV](https://github.com/thu-nics/AKV)** — Serving system for KV-level cache engineering in long-horizon agents, from arXiv:2610.28577. **Why it's trending:** it lands directly on the cost line every team running agents in production is staring at — a reported 27.7% cost reduction *with* a small accuracy gain over summarization, plus 1.2× throughput and 1.5× better P95 latency. Listed as a development to understand, **not** a recommendation — check provenance and keep anything like it isolated.
