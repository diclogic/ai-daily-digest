## Notable Papers & Research

- **"Predicting Model Behavior Before Release by Simulating Deployment"** — OpenAI (published June 16). Introduces Deployment Simulation: replay ~1.3M de-identified production conversations through a candidate model before release, then scan regenerated outputs for novel or increased-frequency misbehaviors. Across GPT-5 through GPT-5.4 deployments (Aug 2025–Mar 2026), the method achieved a median multiplicative error of 1.5x — far more realistic than traditional adversarial red-teaming. Widely read as pre-launch infrastructure for GPT-5.6. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation/) | [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/16/openai-deployment-simulation/))

- **"On the Reliability of Networks of AI Agents: Density Evolution, Stopping Sets, and Architecture Optimization"** — Ehsan Aghazadeh & Hossein Pishro-Nik. Applies network reliability theory (density evolution, stopping sets) to multi-agent AI systems, providing a formal framework for predicting when agent networks degrade and how to architect them for resilience. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/recent))

## Model & Product Launches

### G7 Evian: AI CEOs Pitch Global Governance to World Leaders

The biggest AI governance event of the week: a working lunch at the 52nd G7 summit in Evian, France, on June 17, titled "Ensuring Safe, Rapid, and Efficient AI Adoption." Around a dozen tech CEOs sat with G7 heads of state, including President Trump.

**Who attended:** Sam Altman (OpenAI), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind), Arthur Mensch (Mistral), Aidan Gomez (Cohere), Marc Benioff (Salesforce), Robin Rombach (Black Forest Labs), Victor Riparbelli (Synthesia), plus leaders from Sakana AI, Sarvam, and Meta. Altman sat between Trump and Egyptian President el-Sisi and spoke first.

**What was proposed:**
- Altman called for "an international forum for discussion that establishes globally accepted standards for testing, provides expert and impartial analysis of capabilities and risks, and serves as a venue for cooperation among nations."
- Amodei called for structured access to frontier models and trade of chips/critical components that excludes China, plus international cooperation on cyber and bioterrorism risks.
- Hassabis backed a U.S.-led coalition to shape rules around AI.
- Canadian PM Mark Carney endorsed the idea, comparing it to the Financial Stability Board created after the Great Recession.

No binding commitments emerged. The irony was not lost on observers: Amodei pitched global AI governance while his own company's frontier models remain banned by the U.S. government he wants to lead the coalition. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/g7-trump-ai-tech-leaders-openai-anthropic-google.html) | [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/17/2026/ai-ceos-talk-global-standards-at-g7) | [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-google-us-led-ai-coalition-g7-amodei-hassabis))

### Andrej Karpathy — Green Card Clarification

The initial reports that Karpathy was locked out of Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were misleading. Under U.S. export control law, lawful permanent residents (green card holders) are classified as "U.S. persons" and exempt from the foreign national ban. Karpathy holds an EB-1 extraordinary ability green card, so he retains access to Anthropic's restricted models and can continue his pre-training research work under team lead Nick Joseph. No public comment from Karpathy this week. ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/ai-expert-andrej-karpathy-anthropic-tech-regulation-1802715))

### GitHub Forced to Route Through AWS as AI Coding Agents Overwhelm Infrastructure

Microsoft confirmed on June 16 that it is routing GitHub traffic through Amazon Web Services after AI coding agents drove platform availability down to 88.4% in June — well below enterprise SLA thresholds. GitHub logged nine service-degrading incidents in May alone. The scale of AI-driven demand is staggering: 275M commits/week (on pace for 14B in 2026, up from 1B in all of 2025), and AI agent-opened pull requests surged from 4M in September 2025 to 17M+ by March 2026. Azure can't absorb the load fast enough, so Microsoft is routing Actions runners and Codespaces to AWS while architectural work continues. ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318481/20260616/githubs-ai-agent-crisis-forces-microsoft-tap-aws-outages-break-enterprise-slas.htm) | [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/microsoft-taps-aws-to-keep-github-running-amid-ai-surge))
