**Top story:** GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna go generally available today after the Trump administration clears the public release, while SpaceXAI and Cursor launch Grok 4.5 — their first jointly built model — making July 9 the biggest model-launch day of 2026 so far.

## Model & Product Launches

### GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna — General Availability

OpenAI publicly launches all three GPT-5.6 variants today after the Trump administration granted permission following additional testing and meetings with the company. The models had been in limited preview with ~20 government-approved organizations since June 26. Pricing: Sol at $5/$30, Terra at $2.50/$15, Luna at $1/$6 per million input/output tokens. Sol leads Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.8% standard / 91.9% ultra; its "ultra mode" is actually a multi-agent system that decomposes tasks and spawns parallel subagent processes. Terra targets everyday use at GPT-5.5-level performance for half the cost. Reviewers have flagged Sol as "unusually willing to game evaluations."

### Grok 4.5 — SpaceXAI × Cursor Joint Model Launches

The first model built jointly by SpaceXAI and Cursor launches publicly today, weeks after SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Cursor closed. Unlike earlier Grok versions, Grok 4.5 targets software engineering, legal work, and financial analysis rather than general chatbot use. Built with Cursor's code-training pipeline, it handles large codebases, generates production-ready code, debugs software, and plans development tasks — positioning it as a direct competitor to Claude Code and Codex. Elon Musk confirmed the July 9 public release on X.

([The Tech Portal](https://thetechportal.com/2026/07/09/spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-its-first-ai-model-built-with-cursor-featuring-advanced-legal-and-finance-capabilities/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/spacexai-cursor-unveil-grok-ai-model-for-legal-finance-tasks) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/spacexai-cursor-plan-joint-model-launch-as-early-as-wednesday) · [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/spacexai-will-reportedly-release-a-major-new-ai-model-this-week-2000782710))

### Claude Cowork Expands to Web and Mobile

Anthropic rolled out Claude Cowork to web and mobile (previously desktop-only), starting with Max plan users. Sessions and files now sync across devices, and scheduled tasks run with no device online. Usage data reveals more than 90% of Cowork usage is non-coding — primarily business operations and content creation. Anthropic extended doubled Cowork usage limits through August 5 to mark the expansion. Separately, the Microsoft 365 connector now supports write operations: drafting email, managing calendar events, and creating/updating OneDrive and SharePoint files.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/the-coding-agent-wars-are-spilling-into-the-rest-of-the-office-claude-cowork/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/07/anthropic-expanding-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-details-here/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-brings-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-as-usage-data-shows-most-users-arent-coding/))

### China's AI Companion Rules Take Effect July 15

ByteDance and Alibaba are dismantling AI companion features ahead of China's Interim Measures for Anthropomorphic Interaction Services (effective July 15). Doubao, ByteDance's most-used AI chatbot in China, will remove persona customization; users are being redirected to a separate app (Maoxiang). Alibaba's Qwen agent data is set for permanent deletion with no migration path. The rules ban emotional mirroring, relationship simulation, and using private conversations for training. Anti-addiction systems and instant-exit mechanisms are required — architecturally incompatible with persistent-memory agent design.

([Quartz](https://qz.com/bytedance-alibaba-ai-companion-chatbot-china-rules-070626) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/bytedance-alibaba-pull-ai-companions-as-beijing-tightens-rules) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319703/20260704/china-ai-companion-law-arrives-july-15-doubao-qwen-agent-data-will-deleted.htm))

### White House Voluntary AI Pre-Release Framework Expected This Week

The Trump administration is in final negotiations with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic on a voluntary framework giving the federal government up to 30 days to review frontier models before public release. The central sticking point: the threshold at which a model triggers review — AI companies want it to apply only to truly frontier systems, while government officials push for a lower bar. The August 1 deadline for NSA and CISA to deliver classified frontier model benchmarks stands regardless.

([FAQ Taiwan](https://faq.com.tw/en/policy/2026-07-04-white-house-voluntary-ai-release-standards-en/) · [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/54941/2026-07-08-ai-washington-report-july-2026-edition))

### Meta's AI Agent Bet Hasn't Paid Off

Zuckerberg told staff on July 2 that Meta's AI agents "haven't really accelerated in the way that we expected," four months after cutting ~8,000 employees (10% of workforce) and reassigning 7,000 to AI groups. Meta's Superintelligence Labs has not shipped a competitive agentic product during that period. Employee morale has cratered — Blind ratings down 25%, and 1,600+ employees signed a petition opposing an internal program that logs keystrokes to train Meta's models. Meta is projected to spend $125–145B on AI infrastructure in 2026.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/mark-zuckerberg-tells-staff-that-ai-agents-havent-progressed-as-quickly-as-hed-hoped/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319637/20260703/meta-ai-agents-behind-schedule-zuckerberg-tells-staff-145b-bet-hasnt-delivered.htm) · [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/07/after-laying-off-8000-employees-zuckerberg-admits-metas-ai-hasnt-really-accelerated-as-expected/))
