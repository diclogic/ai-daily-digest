## Notable Papers & Research

- **"World Models: A Comprehensive Survey of Architectures, Methodologies, Reasoning Paradigms, and Applications"** — Arif Hassan Zidan et al. (May 28). A massive 26-author survey organizing world models along four axes: architecture, methodology, reasoning strategy, and application domain. Covers state-space, transformer, diffusion, physics-informed, and language-augmented multimodal approaches. Timely given LeCun's AMI Labs bet on world models over LLMs. ([arXiv:2606.00133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00133))

- **"AXIOM: A Trust-First Neuro-Symbolic Execution Architecture for Verifiable Mathematical Reasoning"** (June 2026). Proposes a hybrid architecture that chains neural language models with symbolic proof engines, enabling verifiable step-by-step mathematical reasoning with formal correctness guarantees. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current))

- **"A Pre-Training Analogue of Grokking in Language Models: Tracing Delayed Grammatical Generalization"** (June 2026). Discovers that language models exhibit grokking-like behavior during pre-training — grammatical generalization emerges suddenly and long after the loss plateau, mirroring the delayed generalization phenomenon first observed in small-scale arithmetic tasks. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current))

- **"Automatically Differentiable Nonlinear Tensor Networks (ADNTNs) for Exponential Compression of Deep Neural Networks"** (June 2026). Introduces tensor network decompositions that can compress deep networks exponentially while remaining end-to-end differentiable, enabling training of compressed models directly rather than compress-then-finetune. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current))

## Model & Product Launches

### Dario Amodei's "Policy on the AI Exponential" (June 10)

Amodei published a lengthy essay calling for the U.S. government to have the power to block deployment of dangerous AI models — his strongest regulatory stance yet. Key proposals: (1) mandatory third-party audits for any model above a compute threshold, covering cybersecurity, biological weapons, loss of control, and automated AI research; (2) government authority to shut down models that fail audits; (3) wage insurance, retention tax incentives, and possibly UBI to address AI-driven labor disruption. He compared the needed framework to how cars, airplanes, and drugs are regulated. The timing — one day after launching Fable 5 — drew both praise for consistency and criticism for regulatory moat-building.
