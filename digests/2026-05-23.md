## Notable Papers

- **MOSS: Self-Evolution through Source-Level Rewriting in Autonomous Agent Systems** — Qianshu Cai et al. (submitted May 22). Introduces a system where autonomous agents rewrite their own source code to improve performance, achieving a mean grader score jump from 0.25 to 0.61 on OpenClaw in a single cycle without human intervention. Unlike prior self-evolving agents limited to prompt tuning, MOSS performs Turing-complete source-level adaptation. ([arXiv 2605.22794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22794))

- **POW3R: Policy-Aware Rubric Rewards** — Scale AI researchers. A framework that dynamically adjusts criterion-level weights for reinforcement learning with rubric-based rewards, improving alignment of RL training with nuanced evaluation criteria. ([Hugging Face Trending](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending))

- **MiniCPM-o 4.5** — Enables real-time full-duplex multimodal interaction through Omni-Flow, a unified streaming framework that aligns inputs and outputs temporally for simultaneous perception and response. Demonstrates a practical path to responsive multimodal AI agents. ([Hugging Face Trending](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Jack Clark — AI Will Co-Produce a Nobel Discovery Within 12 Months (May 21)

Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark told an Oxford audience that AI will collaborate on a Nobel Prize-winning scientific discovery within a year. He also predicted bipedal robots assisting tradespeople within two years, AI-only companies generating millions in revenue within 18 months, and AI systems designing their own successors by end of 2028. Despite his optimism, Clark maintained there remains a "non-zero chance of killing everyone on the planet" and said that risk "hasn't gone away." ([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/jack-clark-predicts-ai-nobel-prize-win-in-12-months), [TechCentral](https://www.techcentral.ie/anthropic-co-founder-predicts-that-ai-will-within-a-year/))

### Mustafa Suleyman — All White-Collar Work Automated in 18 Months (May 16)

Microsoft's AI chief predicted that most tasks involving "sitting down at a computer" will be fully automated within 12–18 months, naming accounting, legal, marketing, and project management as vulnerable fields. The prediction stands in stark contrast to actual enterprise AI adoption, which remains incremental despite the hype. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/why-microsoft-ai-chief-mustafa-suleyman-predicts-ai-automation-18-months/), [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/artificial-intelligence/microsofts-head-of-ai-says-white-collar-jobs-could-vanish-within-the-next-12-to-18-months-as-automation-bots-replace-you))

### Yann LeCun — Calls Job-Loss Predictions "Ridiculously Stupid"

LeCun continues to push back against AI doom narratives, calling the prediction that AI will erase 20% of jobs "ridiculously stupid" and warning the doom narrative is damaging teens' mental health and career decisions. He argued there is "nothing qualitatively different between the previous technological revolutions and this one." Now executive chairman of AMI Labs, LeCun is pursuing JEPA world models as a fundamental alternative to LLMs, backed by $1B+ in funding. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/ai-job-apocalypse-warnings-destructive-yann-lecun/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/04/ai-godfather-survival-guide-hype-doom))

### Dario Amodei — "The Adolescence of Technology"

Amodei's widely discussed essay frames the arrival of powerful AI as a turbulent "rite of passage" for humanity — a teenager handed the keys to a supercar. He identifies five categories of existential risk, from autonomous misalignment and biological misuse to authoritarian consolidation and economic disruption, while proposing Constitutional AI and interpretability research as solutions. At a financial services briefing alongside JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon, he said AI could "eliminate half of entry-level, white-collar knowledge work within years." ([darioamodei.com](https://www.darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/dario-amodei-jevons-paradox-will-ai-wipe-out-white-collar-jobs/))

## Other Notable AI News

### OpenAI Confidential IPO Filing Expected Today

OpenAI is expected to confidentially file its IPO prospectus as early as today (Friday, May 23), working with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. The company is targeting a September public debut at a valuation exceeding $850B. Internal projections suggest losses of $14B in 2026, with profitability not expected until ~2030. The filing follows the jury dismissal of Musk's $150B lawsuit, which removed a major legal overhang. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/openai-ipo-filing.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/22/openai-ipo-filing-1-trillion-may-finally-answer-these-big-questions/))

### House Oversight Committee Demands Altman Testimony on Conflicts

The House Oversight Committee sent Altman a letter demanding documents on OpenAI's governance practices and any conflicts tied to his outside investments, with a May 22 deadline. The WSJ reported Altman pressured OpenAI to invest in companies he personally holds stakes in, including Helion (nuclear fusion) and Stoke Space (aerospace). An internal audit committee investigation was never publicly disclosed. ([House Oversight](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Altman-OpenAI-Letter-050826.pdf), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/sam-altman-faces-gop-firestorm-100108319.html))

### Meta Cuts 8,000 Jobs, Reassigns 7,000 in AI Pivot

Meta's largest restructuring since the 2022–2023 "Year of Efficiency" campaign is under way. Approximately 8,000 employees are being laid off (10% of workforce), while 7,000 are being moved to newly created AI-focused teams including Applied AI Engineering and Agent Transformation Accelerator. Capital expenditures for 2026 are projected at $125–145B, more than double the 2025 outlay. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/20/nx-s1-5826917/meta-layoffs-ai-jobs), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/18/metas-layoffs-starting-this-week-underscore-zuckerbergs-ai-reality-.html))

### Jeff Bezos Reveals Project Prometheus — "Artificial General Engineer"

Bezos offered the first public description of his $38B startup, calling it an "artificial general engineer" building next-generation AI-powered design tools for physical objects — "a very, very modern version of CAD." The company, operating from SF/London/Zurich with ~120 employees poached from OpenAI, DeepMind, Meta, and xAI, is explicitly not a robotics company. ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/jeff-bezos-describes-his-38b-startup-prometheus-for-the-first-time-nothing-to-do-with-robotics/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/bezos-ai-bubble-concerns-amazon-prometheum-blue-origin.html))
