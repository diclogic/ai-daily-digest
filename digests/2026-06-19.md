# AI Daily Digest — June 19, 2026

**Top story:** The G7 summit in Évian-les-Bains, France became an unprecedented convergence of AI power, with Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, Demis Hassabis, and a dozen other tech CEOs joining heads of state to debate AI governance — even as Anthropic's most capable models remain suspended under a US export control order.

---

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — Governments Must Lead on AI Regulation
Speaking at the G7 on June 18, Altman called on national governments to take the lead in setting AI standards, saying decisions about the technology's future "should not be left to the companies developing it." He also urged mandatory evaluations for the most powerful AI systems and floated a sovereign wealth fund to give Americans ownership of the AI revolution. ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/brand-makers/openai-ceo-sam-altman-says-nations-should-not-cede-ai-oversight-to-technology-firms-101550.htm) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/g7-trump-ai-tech-leaders-openai-anthropic-google.html))

### Dario Amodei — "Resist the Temptation to Splinter" on AI
Amodei urged G7 leaders to maintain unity on AI policy and warned against fragmenting access among democratic allies. The call came amid difficult optics: the US government had suspended Anthropic's own Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models just days earlier. In a separate Fortune interview (June 18), he revealed he has just one direct report — his chief of staff — with everyone else reporting to his sister, co-founder Daniela Amodei. ([Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/anthropic-dario-amodei-urges-g7-183457656.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/18/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-one-direct-report-unconventional-management-structure-start-up-success/))

### Demis Hassabis — U.S.-Led AI Coalition
Hassabis joined Amodei in calling for a U.S.-led coalition to shape AI rules and standards at the G7 closed-door session. Separately, at a Stanford GSB event, he said humanity is in the "foothills of the singularity" and narrowed his AGI timeline to 2029–2030. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-amodei-google-hassabis-us-ai-coalition-g7.html) · [Stanford GSB](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/insights/demis-hassabis-thinks-were-foothills-singularity))

### Andrej Karpathy — Software 3.0 and the End of Vibe Coding
Karpathy (now leading pre-training research at Anthropic) published his Sequoia Ascent 2026 talk summary, defining **Software 3.0**: LLMs can automate anything humans can *verify*. He called "vibe coding" — his own coinage from February 2025 — already obsolete, replaced by **agentic engineering**. Key claim: by December 2025, he'd flipped from writing 80% of his own code to delegating 80% to agents. ([StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-andrej-karpathy-software-thesis-evolution-2026-06-17) · [Analytics Drift](https://analyticsdrift.com/andrej-karpathy-agentic-engineering-software-3/))

### Yann LeCun — $1B Bet Against LLMs
LeCun, now executive chairman of **AMI Labs** (Paris), continues to stake his post-Meta career on world-model reasoning over text prediction. At the AI Impact Summit, he said AI will be an "amplifier for human intelligence" but argued current LLMs are fundamentally limited to information retrieval, not genuine understanding. ([StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-yann-lecun-llm-position-evolution-2026-06-16) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/you-certainly-dont-tell-a-researcher-like-me-what-to-do-says-lecun-as-he-exits-meta-for-his-own-startup/))

### Ilya Sutskever — SSI at $32B, No Product
Safe Superintelligence Inc. has reached a $32B valuation on $6B in funding — the highest-valued AI lab with no commercially available product. Sutskever took over as CEO after co-founder Daniel Gross departed for Meta. The company remains focused exclusively on safely developing superintelligent AI. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/12/openai-co-founder-ilya-sutskevers-safe-superintelligence-reportedly-valued-at-32b/) · [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-ilya-sutskever-ssi-financial-breakdown-2026-06-06))

## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Mythos / Fable 5 — Still Suspended
Anthropic's flagship models remain disabled worldwide after a June 12 Commerce Department export control order citing an alleged Mythos 5 jailbreak. Anthropic disputes the characterization, calling the trigger a "routine cybersecurity workflow." The DC Circuit previously denied an emergency stay of the Pentagon's supply-chain risk designation (from March 2026) but granted expedited review. Oral arguments in May showed a divided panel. ([Time](https://time.com/article/2026/06/13/anthropic-fable-mythos-ban-US-security/) · [Anthropic Statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access))

### Claude Mythos Expands via Project Glasswing
Despite the Fable/Mythos consumer suspension, the restricted Mythos Preview continues expanding through Project Glasswing, which now includes ~150 organizations across 15+ countries. Japan's government and three megabanks (MUFG, SMBC, Mizuho) gained access for cybersecurity applications. ([Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/06/03/tech/japan-anthropic-mythos/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Still Waiting
Google's flagship model, announced at I/O on May 19, remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview. Polymarket concentrates odds on June 23 or June 30 for public release. Features a 2M-token context window and Deep Think reasoning. ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm))

### NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Content Safety
Released June 4 — a 4B-parameter multimodal guard model (fine-tuned from Gemma-3-4B) that enforces custom safety policies written in plain language. Supports 12 languages, text + image input, and ships with the training dataset alongside weights. ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-3-5-content-safety))

### GPT-Rosalind Update
OpenAI updated its life-sciences specialized model on June 3, adding Codex plugins for genomics and drug discovery. Outperforms GPT-5.5 on all tested biological domains while using 31% fewer tokens. Now available to qualified organizations globally. ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317754/20260604/gpt-rosalind-drug-discovery-update-openai-cuts-genomics-compute-expands-global-access.htm))

## Notable Papers

### "Continually Self-Improving AI"
Proposes a framework for AI systems that autonomously improve their own capabilities through iterative self-evaluation and refinement, without additional human-labeled data. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.18073))

### Automated Conjecture Resolution
A dual-agent system that pairs a reasoning agent searching for mathematical proofs with a second agent that formalizes them in Lean 4, ensuring every result is machine-verified. Part of a growing wave of AI-for-mathematics research. ([BuildThisNow](https://www.buildthisnow.com/blog/guide/mechanics/ai-research-june-2026))

### TurboQuant (ICLR 2026) & Tool-DC (ACL 2026)
Peer-reviewed methods for efficient model quantization and tool-augmented reasoning respectively, now safe to build production systems on. ([BuildThisNow](https://www.buildthisnow.com/blog/guide/mechanics/ai-research-june-2026))
