## Notable Papers & Research

### VikingMem: Memory Base Management System for Stateful LLM Applications (VLDB 2026)

ByteDance/Volcano Engine published the VikingMem paper (arXiv 2605.29640), accepted at VLDB 2026, describing a memory base management system for stateful LLM-based applications. The paper underpins the newly open-sourced OpenViking project (see Trending Open-Source below). It introduces a file-system paradigm for organizing agent context — memories, knowledge, and skills — with tiered loading (L0 abstract → L1 overview → L2 details) that cuts token expenditure while improving retrieval accuracy from 24–57% to 80–83% on their benchmarks.

([arXiv 2605.29640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29640) · [GitHub](https://github.com/volcengine/OpenViking))

## Model & Product Launches

### Anthropic Brings Claude Mythos 5 to Claude Security — $35M Open-Source Fund

Anthropic announced on August 21 that Claude Mythos 5 — the cyber-capable model previously restricted to ~150 vetted organizations via Project Glasswing — now powers vulnerability scans inside Claude Security for all Enterprise customers. The scan connects to a GitHub repository, traces data flows across files, and returns findings with CWE categories, confidence/severity ratings, and suggested patches. Crucially, Claude Security returns scan results rather than a prompt box, preventing the model from being steered into writing exploits. Alongside this, Anthropic launched the Defender Advantage Fund (0xDAF), committing $35 million in Claude credits to organizations securing open-source software, with initial pilot grants focused on patching live vulnerabilities, automating scanning/patching workflows, and hardening projects against whole classes of attack.

([Anthropic Blog](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-claude-mythos-5-to-more-defenders) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-deploys-claude-mythos-5-in-security-tools-35m-open-source-fund/) · [CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/mythos-5-claude-security-scanning/) · [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-makes-claude-mythos-5-available-for-security-scans))

### NVIDIA–Poolside $6B Licensing Deal

Bloomberg reported on August 20 that NVIDIA will pay $6 billion to license Poolside's "Model Factory" — the startup's platform for building generative AI models tailored to software development — and extend job offers to 109 employees. NVIDIA is also investing $1 billion at a $12 billion pre-money valuation. The deal is non-exclusive (Poolside can still sell to others), and the three co-founders remain. This follows NVIDIA's pattern of license-plus-invest deals with AI startups (similar structures were used with Groq and Enfabrica).

## Other Notable AI News

### Nevada Approves Up to 8,000 Robotaxis in Las Vegas

The Nevada Transportation Authority unanimously approved applications from Tesla, Waymo, Uber, and Zoox to deploy autonomous vehicles in Clark County. Tesla received the largest allocation (up to 5,000), Waymo got 1,000, Uber 1,000 (via Motional and Zoox partnerships), and Zoox received a separate two-year exemption for up to 2,500 shuttles per year. However, Tesla asked for 5,000 and initially received only 10 operational permits with a 45 mph speed cap and no airport trips — the 5,000 is a ceiling that will scale gradually. This is the largest single regulatory approval for robotaxis in the US to date.

([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/21/nevada-approves-permits-for-tesla-uber-and-waymos-robotaxi/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/19/tesla-las-vegas-5000-robotaxi-permits-sin-city-10-capped/) · [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/08/17/tesla-nevada-robotaxi-permit-10-vehicles-las-vegas/))

### Apple Music Will Label AI-Generated Songs

Apple emailed creators on August 20 that "Made With AI" labels will become visible to Apple Music listeners later this year. Labels and distributors must tag any track, composition, artwork, or music video where AI played a material role. Apple Music executive Oliver Schusser previously disclosed that over one-third of monthly music deliveries are now fully AI-generated, though listener engagement with AI content remains under 0.5%. The move follows the EU's AI transparency requirements and builds on mandatory disclosure rules Apple introduced in March 2026.

([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/20/apple-musics-ai-disclosure-labels-will-soon-be-mandatory-rather-than-optional) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-to-label-ai-generated-songs/) · [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-ai-labeling-system/))

### Brazil Invests $444M in AI, Splits Between US and Chinese Tech

Brazil announced a 2.3 billion reais ($444M) investment to build domestic AI capacity, deliberately splitting between US and Chinese partners. Over half ($251M) funds a supercomputing project in Rio de Janeiro with Huawei and iFlytek, targeting 7,200 petaflops — enough for a top-10 global ranking. The remainder goes to partnerships with US firms. President Lula's administration emphasized: "The strategy is not to depend on a single company, technology, or country."

([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/21/brazil-launches-ai-supercomputer-push-while-balancing-us-and-chinese-tech) · [China-Global South Project](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/08/21/brazil-ai-investment-huawei-nvidia-supercomputer-lula/))

### Google Launches Publisher Tools to Fight AI-Driven Traffic Losses

On August 20, Google introduced new Search Console tools letting publishers control how their content appears in AI search features, plus "Search profiles" — dedicated, shareable pages for publishers and creators. Google says people are twice as likely to click through to a "preferred source." The move comes as UK publisher Reach reported Google referral traffic down 55% year-over-year, driven by AI Overviews keeping users on Google.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/google-gives-publishers-a-new-way-to-fight-ai-driven-traffic-losses/) · [Marketplace](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2026/08/03/googles-ai-search-is-changing-who-gets-web-traffic))
