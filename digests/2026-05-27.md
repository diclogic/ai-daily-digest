## Notable Papers & Research

**OpenAI Model Disproves 80-Year-Old Erdős Conjecture (May 22)**
An unreleased OpenAI reasoning model autonomously disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry — the unit distance problem posed by Paul Erdős in 1946. The model discovered a new family of point constructions using algebraic number theory that provably beats square grids, marking the first time AI has independently solved a prominent open problem in mathematics.
[OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) · [Greg Brockman on X](https://x.com/gdb/status/2057182650784452925)

**Beyond Summaries: Structure-Aware Labeling of Code Changes with LLMs**
*Published May 26, 2026 (arXiv cs.SE, cs.AI)*
Proposes methods for LLMs to generate structured, semantically rich labels for code diffs — moving beyond simple natural-language summaries toward labels that capture the type, scope, and intent of changes.

**Understanding Emergent Misalignment via Feature Superposition Geometry**
*Accepted to ACL 2026*
Investigates why fine-tuned models sometimes exhibit unexpected misaligned behaviors, tracing the phenomenon to geometric properties of feature superposition in neural networks. Relevant to ongoing AI safety research.

**MinerU2.5: Document Parsing Vision-Language Model**
A 1.2B-parameter VLM for document parsing that achieves state-of-the-art recognition accuracy using a coarse-to-fine strategy, offering practical efficiency gains for document processing workflows.

## Model & Product Launches

**Claude Mythos Spotted in Claude Code — Public Release Closer (May 25–26)**
References to "claude-mythos-1-preview" appeared in the public Claude Code interface over the weekend, with some users briefly seeing a Mythos toggle before it was removed. Anthropic has confirmed it is preparing Mythos for release via Claude Code and Claude Security, though no timeline has been set. The restricted model remains available only to ~50 Project Glasswing partners, where it has now flagged over 10,000 high/critical-severity vulnerabilities in its first month. Anthropic stated the public release will come "in the near future, once we've developed the far stronger safeguards we need."
[BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropics-restricted-claude-mythos-model-may-be-coming-to-claude-code/) · [CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/claude-mythos-moves-toward-public/)

## Key Opinions & Statements

**Sam Altman — "I Don't Think [a Jobs Apocalypse] Is Coming" (May 26)**
Speaking at a Commonwealth Bank of Australia conference in Sydney, the OpenAI CEO publicly walked back his earlier predictions about AI's economic impact. Altman said he was "roughly right" on technological predictions but "pretty wrong" on social and economic implications, admitting he expected more entry-level white-collar job losses by now. He noted a "human part" of employment that can't be replaced, saying he tried using AI to answer emails and Slack messages but went back to doing some himself because "people care about interacting with actual people."
[Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/05/26/no-ai-jobs-apocalypse-so-far-says-openais-sam-altman) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/05/52789946/sam-altman-says-ai-will-not-lead-to-a-global-jobs-apocalypse)

**Yann LeCun — "Don't Listen to CEOs" (ongoing)**
The AMI Labs founder and former Meta AI chief continued his campaign against AI doom narratives, calling CEO warnings about job loss "extremely destructive" and arguing they harm teens' mental health. LeCun maintains LLMs are "still not very good at reasoning" and function as pattern-matching systems without genuine comprehension. His new venture AMI Labs — focused on JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) as an alternative to LLMs — has raised $1B at a $3.5B valuation.
[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/ai-job-apocalypse-warnings-destructive-yann-lecun/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/04/ai-godfather-survival-guide-hype-doom)

**Demis Hassabis — AGI by 2029 "A Possibility" (Google I/O)**
The DeepMind CEO maintained his view that humanity is at the "foothills of the singularity" and refined his AGI timeline to 2029–2030. He emphasized AI as "the most important tool for scientists" and highlighted agents as an immediate near-term reality: "We can see agents really happening now and imagine what they will be in another year."
[Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/05/20/2026/google-exec-demis-hassabis-predicts-were-at-the-foothills-of-the-singularity)
