## Notable Papers & Research

### ICML 2026 — Selective Activation Sparsity

A paper presented at ICML 2026 this week introduces "selective activation sparsity," a training method that teaches models to activate only the parameters most relevant to each task. Models trained with the technique matched the performance of models three times their size on standard benchmarks while using a fraction of the compute at inference time. The approach is architecture-agnostic and applies to both dense transformers and mixture-of-experts systems.

### ~21% of ICLR 2026 Reviews Estimated AI-Generated

Analysis from Pangram Labs estimates that roughly 21% of official reviews submitted to ICLR 2026 were fully AI-generated, raising fresh questions about the integrity of peer review in machine learning. AAAI-26 ran a parallel pilot study on AI-assisted peer review at scale, finding that reviewer-AI collaboration improved review quality when used as a drafting aid but degraded it when reviewers rubber-stamped AI output wholesale.

## Model & Product Launches

### EU AI Act — Transparency Obligations Take Effect (August 2)

Today marks the enforcement date for Article 50 of the EU AI Act, the most significant milestone since the regulation's prohibited-practices ban took effect in February. Four transparency obligations now apply to AI systems placed on the EU market:

1. **Direct interaction**: Chatbots and interactive AI must tell users they are dealing with AI, not a human.
2. **AI-generated content**: Outputs must carry machine-readable provenance marks.
3. **Deepfakes**: Synthetically generated or manipulated audio, image, and video must be clearly labeled.
4. **Emotion recognition & biometric categorization**: Users must be informed when these systems are in use.

Fines for non-compliance reach EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher. The rules apply to providers and deployers alike.

### MiniMax H3 — Omni-Modal Video Generation (July 31)

MiniMax launched H3, a native-2K, 24fps text-to-video and image-to-video model that generates 5–15 second clips with synchronized dialogue, sound effects, and ambient audio in a single pass. H3 accepts text, images, video, and audio as unified context and supports up to 9 reference images, 3 video clips, and 3 audio clips for multi-reference consistency. MiniMax says it costs less than one-third per second compared to mainstream competitors and plans to release the model weights as open-source in early August.

([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/01/minimax-releases-minimax-h3-an-omni-modal-video-model-that-generates-15-second-2k-clips-with-native-stereo-audio/) · [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3362540/video-ai-minimax-challenges-bytedance-low-price-open-weights-new-h3-model) · [MiniMax](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3))

### OpenAI GPT-5.6 Price Cuts (July 30)

OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80% (from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens) and Terra by 20% (from $2.50/$15 to $2/$12), just three weeks after the GPT-5.6 family's July 9 launch. ### Alex Karp — Defends Amodei, Torches Rest of AI Industry

At a Palantir company-wide meeting on August 1, CEO Alex Karp offered a nuanced take on Anthropic's Dario Amodei: "I've spent a lot of time with Dario — he is not the caricature that many people, most Americans, believe." ## Other Notable AI News

### Y Combinator Open-Sources QM — Multiplayer Agent Harness

On July 31, Y Combinator released QM under the MIT license: the multi-agent harness YC uses internally across accounting, legal, events, and engineering. QM is organizational-first infrastructure — each employee and team room gets isolated scoped workspaces with separate memory, files, and permissions. It's model-agnostic (switch between Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or Pi without redeployment), cloud-first with native Slack and web UI, and includes identity, Postgres state, keychains, crons, skills grants, and admin policy. It hit ~1.9K GitHub stars within hours of announcement.

([GitHub](https://github.com/yc-software/qm) · [Y Combinator on X](https://x.com/ycombinator/status/2083243960684908768) · [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/y-combinator-qm-open-source-multi-agent-harness-august-2026))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Still Missing After 67+ Days

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro remains unreleased, now more than two months past the "within a month" promise Sundar Pichai made at Google I/O on May 19. A training data update in late June reportedly made coding performance worse rather than better, and Google concluded the original model couldn't be patched. The company has shipped three Gemini Flash variants as stopgaps and confirmed it has begun pretraining Gemini 4. No new release date has been given.

([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/16/gemini-3-5-pro-delays/) · [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/au/gemini-3-5-pro-67-days-delay-2026/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320736/20260716/rebuilt-gemini-35-pro-misses-third-deadline-google-eyes-stopgap-release.htm))
