## Notable Papers & Research

- **"DINOv3: Self-Supervised Learning for Vision at Unprecedented Scale"** — Timothée Darcet, John Brandt, Julien Mairal, Andrea Vedaldi et al. (Meta AI). Scales self-supervised vision to 6.7B parameters trained on 1.7B curated images, achieving state-of-the-art on dense tasks including COCO object detection (66.1 mAP) and ADE20K segmentation (63.0 mIoU). Introduces Gram Anchoring, a regularization technique that preserves high-quality dense features over long training runs — solving a key limitation of earlier DINO versions. ([Meta AI Research](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/dinov3/) | [Blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/dinov3-self-supervised-vision-model/))

- **"The Deterministic Horizon: When Extended Reasoning Fails and Tool Delegation Becomes Necessary"** — Dongxin Guo (University of Hong Kong). Accepted as a main conference paper at ICML 2026. Proves that decoder-only LLMs hit a hard accuracy wall at 19–31 reasoning steps via an Attention Bottleneck Theorem, after which tool-augmented reasoning (86–94% accuracy) vastly outperforms chain-of-thought (24–42%). Continues to generate discussion this week as researchers debate implications for agentic architectures. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00376))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump Signs AI Cybersecurity Executive Order

On June 2, President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," establishing a voluntary framework for frontier model developers to provide early government access (up to 30 days pre-release) for cybersecurity benchmarking. The order also creates an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse and directs the AG to prioritize criminal enforcement against anyone using AI to unlawfully access systems. The framework is expressly voluntary — no preclearance requirement — with details due by August 1. The timing, just 10 days before the Mythos export ban, raises questions about whether the voluntary approach is sustainable.
