## Model & Product Launches

### Fable 5 Exits Subscriptions — Usage Credits Start Tomorrow

Today (July 7) is the last day Claude Fable 5 is included in Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans at no additional cost. From July 8, Fable 5 moves entirely to usage credits at standard API rates: $10/1M input, $50/1M output tokens. Users must enable credits at claude.ai > Settings > Usage or Fable 5 requests will simply not run. Spend limits are available to cap runaway agentic sessions. Anthropic describes this as a temporary capacity measure, not a permanent paywall — Opus 4.8, Sonnet, and Haiku remain inside normal plan limits.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319767/20260706/fable-5-subscription-ends-tomorrow-per-token-costs-who-gets-hit-hardest.htm) · [Codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/claude-fable-5-usage-credits-july-2026/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-isnt-permanently-leaving-subscriptions-anthropic-says/))

## Notable Papers & Conferences

### ICML 2026 — Main Conference Opens in Seoul (July 7–9)

The 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning is underway at COEX, Seoul, with a record 23,918 submissions, 6,352 accepted papers (26.6% acceptance rate), 168 Oral presentations (0.7%), and 536 Spotlights (2.2%). The research agenda is dominated by agentic AI and autonomous agent safety.

**Distinguished Paper Awards** (3 winners, 6 honorable mentions): Both winning research papers center on diffusion models — a rare double in the same direction. One winner is **"The Flexibility Trap: Rethinking the Value of Arbitrary Order in Diffusion Language Models"** by Zanlin Ni et al. (Tsinghua, team of Huang Gao), which identifies a critical failure mode where diffusion LLMs' ability to generate tokens in arbitrary order actually narrows reasoning performance during RL, and introduces JustGRPO, a framework constraining dLLMs to left-to-right order during RL exploration while preserving parallel decoding at inference.

**Test of Time Award** goes to **"Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning"** (DeepMind) — recognizing the foundational A3C paper's enduring influence on scalable RL.

([ICML](https://icml.cc/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319684/20260704/icml-2026-opens-monday-seoul-agentic-ai-tops-record-year-peer-review-strains.htm) · [ArXivIQ](https://arxiviq.substack.com/p/icml-2026-the-flexibility-trap-rethinking))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### UN Secretary-General Guterres — "We Cannot Vibe-Code the Truth"

At the opening of the first Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (July 6–7), UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres warned that AI systems are "no longer tools awaiting instruction" but are "writing code, acting online and making choices with less and less human oversight." He cautioned that "an experiment is being run on our own societies, without a plan and without consent" and called for a global governance system, emphasizing that "while vibe-coding can do wonders, we cannot vibe-code the truth." He singled out children's safety as a top priority, noting AI has reached children's learning and friendships "before anyone asked what it would do to them." The summit runs alongside the WSIS Forum 2026 and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit.

([UN SG Remarks](https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statements/2026-07-06/secretary-generals-remarks-the-opening-of-the-first-global-dialogue-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered) · [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/46030/world/world-must-not-let-ai-vibe-code-humanitys-future-un-chief/) · [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/world/un-chief-urges-global-oversight-of-ai-says-humanitys-future-cant-be-vibe-coded-1968858))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump AI Executive Order — July 2 Deadline Passed

The 30-day deadline from the June 2 executive order passed on July 2. Federal agencies were required to: prioritize AI-enabled cyber defense, release Binding Operational Directives for civilian IT, and form an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" with industry for vulnerability scanning and patch coordination. The 60-day deadline (August 1) requires a voluntary framework for pre-release federal access to frontier models — the mechanism now governing both GPT-5.6 and Mythos releases.

([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/assessing-trumps-executive-order-on-ai-oversight))

### Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-Pro Replaces V2 Series

Xiaomi deprecated the MiMo-V2 series on June 30 and is directing developers to MiMo-V2.5-Pro, a 1.02T-parameter MoE model (42B active) with a 1M-token context window. Pricing is aggressively low at $0.43/$0.87 per million tokens. Intelligence index of 42.2 and agentic index of 68.7 place it in the excellent tier for both reasoning and agentic tasks — at a fraction of US frontier model costs.

([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/xiaomi/mimo-v2.5-pro) · [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/mimo-v2-5-pro))

### UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance — Geneva

The first session of the UN's Global Dialogue on AI Governance wrapped up today in Geneva, bringing together all 193 UN member states alongside private sector, civil society, and academia. A second session follows in New York in May 2027. The summit coincides with WSIS Forum 2026 and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit (July 7–10), creating the densest week of international AI governance activity in history.

([UN](https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/en) · [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-dialogue-ai-governance-geneva-6-7-july))
