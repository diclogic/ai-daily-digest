## Notable Papers & Research

- **"VoxCPM2: Tokenizer-Free TTS for Multilingual Speech Generation, Creative Voice Design, and True-to-Life Cloning"** — OpenBMB. A 2B-parameter model trained on 2M+ hours of multilingual speech data that bypasses discrete audio tokenization entirely, generating 48kHz audio across 30 languages with real-time performance (RTF ~0.13 on RTX 4090). ### Fable 5 / Mythos 5 — Week 2: Amazon's Role Revealed, No Deal in Sight

The full picture of how Anthropic's models ended up offline is now clear:

**Amazon triggered the ban.** Amazon called administration officials on the evening of June 12 with a report showing they could jailbreak Fable 5 to write vulnerability "proofs of concept" — capability that security teams routinely use but that alarmed the White House. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick sent the export control directive hours later, and by 10 PM users lost access. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-amazon-white-house) | [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/14/how-a-warning-from-amazon-led-the-white-house-to-shut-down-anthropics-mythos-model/))

**"They screwed us."** Axios reports personality clashes between Amodei and White House AI czar David Sacks have hardened the dispute. Sacks said Amodei was "less than cooperative" when the administration first raised the jailbreak concern; Anthropic officials reportedly believe the administration overreacted under pressure from a competitor. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/15/anthropic-white-house-fable-mythos))

**Cybersecurity defense concerns grow.** A June 16 Axios analysis highlights the irony: the same model whose vulnerability-finding capabilities triggered the ban was being used by U.S. critical infrastructure operators for defensive security. Taking it offline may have weakened, not strengthened, national cyber defense. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/16/anthropic-fable-trump-white-house-cybersecurity))

**Status:** Both models remain offline globally. Anthropic's Tom Brown and Sarah Heck continue in-person meetings with Lutnick, Bessent, and National Cyber Director Cairncross. No committed timeline for restoration. The June 22 pricing transition (from free plan access to token-based credits at $10/$50 per M tokens) approaches with no product to price.
