## Model & Product Launches

### Kimi K3 — Early Benchmarks Arrive

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, the largest open-weight model ever released (2.8T total parameters, 50B active via 896-expert MoE, 16 active per token), went live via API and apps on July 16. The first independent benchmarks are now in: Artificial Analysis gives it an Elo of 1,547 — a 732-point jump from Kimi K2.6 and behind only Claude Fable 5 on the composite leaderboard. It ranks #1 on arena.ai's "Code - WebDev" category, scores 88.3 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 91.2% on BrowseComp. API pricing is $3/$15 per million input/output tokens — roughly Sonnet-tier. Full open weights are scheduled for July 27.

([Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-kimi-k3-28t-a50b-the-largest) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems) · [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/moonshotai/kimi-k3) · [BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/models/kimi-3))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Launched But Still Unverified

### NotebookLM → Gemini Notebook

Google rebranded NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook on July 16, folding it into the Gemini product family. The standalone product gains code execution capabilities and cross-app syncing with the Gemini app. Over 30 million people and 600,000+ organizations use it. Notebooks will soon be accessible directly from AI Mode in Google Search.

([Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-continues-its-renaming-streak-by-turning-notebooklm-to-gemini-notebook/))

### PrismML Bonsai 27B — 27B Parameters on a Phone

PrismML open-sourced Bonsai 27B on July 14: a 27.8B-parameter multimodal model compressed to 1-bit weights (3.9 GB) that runs on an iPhone, retaining 90% of full-precision Qwen3.6 27B performance. A 5.9 GB ternary variant retains 95%. Both support 262K-token context, vision, tool calling, and agentic loops. Apache 2.0 licensed.

([PrismML](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-27b) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Bonsai-27B-gguf) · [Kie.ai](https://kie.ai/blog/what-is-bonsai-27b))

## Other Notable AI News

### WAIC 2026 Day 2: Humanoid Robots Go to Work

Day 2 of the World AI Conference in Shanghai (July 17–20) showcased humanoid robots performing real commercial tasks:

- **AGIBOT** unveiled four new products: the A3 Ultra full-size humanoid, X2 Edu, G2 Max heavy-payload industrial robot, and OmniHand 3 Ultra-M dexterous manipulation system. Live demos included table tennis (real-time trajectory prediction), boxing (teleoperation with whole-body coordination), and end-to-end beverage service.
- **KEENON** demonstrated humanoids completing hotel laundry operations — loading washing machines, retrieving clean laundry, and folding garments.
- **SenseTime's SenseMart Go** featured humanoid robots independently running a retail store: picking products, organizing shelves, conducting inventory, and processing purchases.

The conference features 108 chips, 261 foundation models, 208 embodied-intelligence terminals, and 300+ product debuts across four Shanghai venues.

### Germany: AI Search Outputs Are Media Content

Germany's media regulators ruled on July 14 that Google's AI Overviews and Perplexity AI are content publishers, not neutral intermediaries — the first formal application of media law to AI-generated search outputs anywhere in the world. The ruling means these services cannot claim the EU's standard platform liability exemption. Google is expected to appeal. The decision could set a precedent for how AI-generated content is regulated across Europe.

([BroadbandTVNews](https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2026/07/14/german-media-regulator-applies-media-law-to-ai-search-services-in-landmark-ruling/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320790/20260716/germany-strips-ai-search-its-eu-liability-shield-worlds-first-media-ruling.htm) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/landmark-german-ruling-declares-googles-ai-overviews-are-googles-own-words-and-makes-it-liable-for-false-answers/))
