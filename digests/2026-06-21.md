## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei & Demis Hassabis — Joint Call for US-Led AI Coalition at G7

At a closed-door session during the G7 summit in Evian-les-Bains, France (June 17), Amodei and Hassabis jointly called for a US-led coalition to shape international AI rules and standards. Amodei proposed structured access to frontier models, coordinated chip trade that excludes China, and cooperation on cyber and bioterrorism risks from AI. Around a dozen tech executives — including Sam Altman — joined heads of state; Canadian PM Mark Carney agreed the US could lead such a coalition. The irony was not lost on observers: Amodei was asking the same government that had shut down his flagship products five days earlier to lead global AI governance. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-amodei-google-hassabis-us-ai-coalition-g7.html) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-google-us-led-ai-coalition-g7-amodei-hassabis))

### Dario Amodei — "Policy on the AI Exponential"

Amodei published a sweeping policy essay calling for binding, enforceable AI regulation — a significant shift from Anthropic's prior transparency-only stance. The core proposal: FAA-style oversight where frontier models above a compute threshold (10^25 FLOPs) undergo mandatory third-party testing in four domains (cybersecurity, bioweapons, loss of control, automated R&D), with government power to block deployment. Anthropic simultaneously pledged $350M for AI labor-displacement programs — a $200M Economic Futures Research Fund and a $150M national fellowship program. ([darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/post/policy-on-the-ai-exponential) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-ceo-calls-for-faa-style-regulation-of-powerful-ai-models-what-enterprises-should-know) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318217/20260611/ai-regulation-push-amodei-demands-power-blocking-unsafe-models-anthropic-pledges-350-million.htm))

### Demis Hassabis — "Foothills of the Singularity"

In a conversation at Stanford GSB (late May), Hassabis said humanity is standing "in the foothills of the singularity" and expects AGI by 2029-2030, "plus or minus a year." He described AI as a "species-level transition" advancing ~10x faster than the Industrial Revolution, with "little margin for error" over the next decade. ([Stanford GSB](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/insights/demis-hassabis-thinks-were-foothills-singularity) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/26/deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis) · [Stanford Daily](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/05/29/deepmind-ceo-warns-about-ai/))

### Yann LeCun — AI Bubble Warning

Beyond his "xAI is a failure" comments (covered yesterday), LeCun warned at VivaTech that all frontier AI labs "are losing money" and running services more expensively than they can charge. His own venture AMI Labs ($1.03B raised, $3.5B valuation) is betting the entire LLM approach is wrong, building world models grounded in physical reasoning instead. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/yann-lecun-xai-failure-ai-bubble-explosion))

## Notable Papers

### "World Models: A Comprehensive Survey of Architectures, Methodologies, Reasoning Paradigms, and Applications"
A timely survey organizing the world-models landscape — relevant given LeCun's ongoing thesis that world models, not LLMs, are the path to AGI. Introduces a multi-axis taxonomy covering architecture, methodological family (SSMs, transformers, diffusion, physics-informed, language-augmented), and reasoning strategy. ([arXiv:2606.00133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00133))

### "AXIOM: A Trust-First Neuro-Symbolic Execution Architecture for Verifiable Mathematical Reasoning"
Alessio Bruno. Uses LLMs strictly as canonicalizers — rewriting informal math into schemas consumed by a deterministic computer-algebra-system pipeline. Reports 94.36% correctness (2,592/2,747) at 100% trust (zero confident-wrong answers) across MATH benchmark categories, with 3,100+ routing rules. ([arXiv:2606.00671](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00671))

### "Artificial Intelligence for Mathematical Reasoning: An Integrated Survey of Language Models, Neuro-symbolic Systems, and Verified Discovery"
Comprehensive survey integrating the three main approaches to AI math reasoning — pure LLMs, neuro-symbolic hybrids, and formal verification — into a single framework. ([arXiv:2606.08728](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08728))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump Signs AI Executive Order and National Security Directive

Two major policy actions from earlier this month, now generating industry-wide impact:

1. **Executive Order (June 2):** "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" establishes a voluntary framework where AI companies submit frontier models for government testing up to 30 days before public release. Creates an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse and directs the AG to prioritize AI-enabled crime enforcement. Explicitly states no mandatory licensing or pre-clearance for AI model release. ([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order))
2. **National Security Presidential Memorandum (June 5):** Establishes an AI framework for defense and intelligence procurement guided by four pillars: Adoption, Adaptation, Assurance, and Accountability. ([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-historic-directive-on-ai-in-the-national-security-enterprise/))

### Anthropic IPO Process Advances

Anthropic confidentially filed a draft S-1 with the SEC on June 1, targeting an October 2026 Nasdaq listing. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley are leading the offering, expected to raise $60B+. The company's annualized revenue run rate has crossed $47B, and its post-money valuation stands at $965B — surpassing OpenAI's $852B for the first time. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec))

### Qualcomm in Talks to Acquire Tenstorrent

Qualcomm is in early-stage talks to acquire Jim Keller's Tenstorrent for $8-10B, signaling a major push into AI chip competition. Tenstorrent designs AI chips using the open RISC-V standard, offering a potential alternative to NVIDIA's dominance.
