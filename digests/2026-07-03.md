## Notable Papers

- **"Agent Safety Alignment via Reinforcement Learning"** — Introduces a tri-modal taxonomy for user prompts and tool responses, with a custom sandbox environment that simulates real-world tool execution and fine-grained reward shaping to train safer AI agents. ([arXiv:2507.08270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08270))

- **"ResearcherBench: Evaluating Deep AI Research Systems on the Frontiers of Scientific Inquiry"** — New benchmark measuring whether AI systems can go beyond narrow Q&A to formulate hypotheses and design experiments on open-ended scientific research tasks. Gaining attention as labs race to build autonomous research agents. ([arXiv:2507.16280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16280))

- **"Measuring the Gap Between Human and LLM Research Ideas"** — Systematically compares the novelty and feasibility of research ideas generated by LLMs vs. human researchers, providing empirical grounding for the "AI scientist" hype cycle. ([arXiv — cs.CL](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent))

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI: 5% Government Stake Proposal (July 2)
Sam Altman proposed that OpenAI cede a 5% equity stake — worth ~$42.6B at the company's $852B valuation — to the U.S. government, modeled on Alaska's Permanent Fund. Under his vision, every leading U.S. AI lab (Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI) would contribute the same share into a public investment vehicle paying annual dividends to citizens. The FT characterized the discussions as "conceptual and early-stage"; implementing it may require an act of Congress. Senator Bernie Sanders has introduced a separate bill along similar lines. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/openai-proposes-giving-the-us-government-a-5-stake-ft-says), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/openai-proposes-us-government-own-5percent-stake-to-address-political-blowback.html), [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/america-first-sam-altman-proposes-us-led-international-forum-for-ai-and-5-stake-for-trump-admin-2000780668))

### Google: Gemini 3.5 Pro Delayed to July
Google's next flagship — with a 2M-token context window and Deep Think reasoning mode — has slipped from its original June target into July as Google refines coding performance and long-task reliability from early enterprise previews. Pricing is estimated at ~$15/$60 per 1M input/output tokens, roughly 10× Gemini 3.5 Flash. The White House voluntary-standards negotiations with AI companies, confirmed by Reuters on July 2, are reportedly a factor in the timing of Google's advanced coding model releases. ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/is-google-delaying-gemini-35-pro-launch-to-july-for-further-testing), [Bind AI](https://blog.getbind.co/gemini-3-5-pro-slips-to-july-and-four-senior-google-researchers-just-left-for-anthropic/))

### GPT-5.6 Sol: Cerebras Partnership
OpenAI confirmed GPT-5.6 Sol will launch on Cerebras hardware at up to 750 tokens/sec when it moves to general availability "in coming weeks." The model remains in limited preview with ~20 vetted organizations under the government-coordinated rollout. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov))

### Claude Mythos Watch

Fable 5, the general-use sibling with robust safety classifiers, is included for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans at up to 50% of weekly usage limits through July 7. ## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman (OpenAI CEO)
Beyond the government-stake proposal, Altman published a Financial Times op-ed calling for an "American-led international forum" for AI safety — a body he compared to the International Atomic Energy Agency. He's been meeting with Trump, Commerce Secretary Lutnick, and Treasury Secretary Bessent. The timing follows the G7 summit (June 17) where Trump met leading AI executives. Altman is making an aggressive political play as OpenAI's enterprise market share continues to slide (from ~50% in 2023 to 27% in 2026). ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/02/sam-altman-new-world-order-ai-openai-google-anthropic/), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/altman-us-led-global-ai-safety-forum/))

### Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO)
Court documents released July 2 reveal the full email exchange between Amodei and Pentagon Undersecretary Emil Michael that led to the Anthropic-DoD split earlier this year. Amodei held firm on two red lines: no integration of Claude into autonomous weapons systems and no use in domestic surveillance. Michael warned there was "one more chance to align" before the Pentagon designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk. The emails also raise conflict-of-interest concerns — Michael holds stock in Anthropic competitor xAI. ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/read-the-tense-emails-between-the-pentagon-former-uber-exec-and-anthropic-dario-amodei-2000780849), [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12669))

### Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind CEO)
No new public statements since the Semafor interview. Hassabis continues to project confidence about Google's talent position and maintains his AGI timeline of "2030, plus or minus a year," with 2029 now seen as a possibility. ([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/23/2026/deepmind-chief-demis-hassabis-says-googles-still-winning-ai-talent))

### Yann LeCun (AMI Labs Executive Chair)
Speaking at VivaTech in Paris, LeCun told the BBC that ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini "are not a path towards human level or human-like intelligence" and that AI still doesn't understand the physical world as well as a rat. ([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/), [ResultSense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-07-02-us-voluntary-ai-model-standards/))
