## Key Opinions & Statements

### Demis Hassabis — Proposes FINRA-Style AI Standards Body

In the day's biggest policy story, Hassabis published a proposal on July 14 calling for a U.S.-led, industry-funded AI standards body modeled on FINRA (the self-regulatory organization that polices Wall Street under SEC oversight). Key elements:

- Frontier labs would voluntarily share models with the body **up to 30 days before release** for safety testing that probes cyber, biological, and deception capabilities.
- Once the testing regime proves "effective and robust," formalization "could quickly follow" — meaning mandatory pre-deployment approval.
- The board would have a **majority of independent experts** (including Turing Award winners), alongside industry, government, and open-source representatives.
- Rules would apply to **all frontier-class models, regardless of origin or whether open or closed**, with qualifying benchmarks regularly updated.
- Hassabis said he wants the body running within **"months," ideally before year-end**, and that his talks with the Trump administration have drawn "positive signals."
- He spent months quietly building support, briefing the administration, fellow lab leaders, and European officials before going public. Other major lab leaders reportedly agree at a high level that "this is where the industry needs to go."

Hassabis is also scheduled for a conversation with Fei-Fei Li at Stanford HAI today on "Using AI to Accelerate Scientific Discovery," where the standards-body proposal will likely come up.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-us-led-ai-standards-body.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepmind-ceo-calls-for-an-independent-standards-body-to-regulate-frontier-ai/) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/07/14/2026/deepminds-hassabis-calls-for-ai-government-oversight) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/demis-hassabis-frontier-ai-standards-body-finra))

## Other Notable AI News

### China's AI Companion Ban Takes Effect Today

China's Interim Measures for AI Anthropomorphic Interactive Services — the first binding regulation globally targeting AI virtual companions — take effect today, July 15. ByteDance's Doubao is shutting down its user-created AI persona feature today, giving users until October to export chat histories before permanent deletion. Tencent's Yuanbao went first on June 30, Alibaba's Qwen pulled user-created bots on July 10, and Doubao is the final major platform to comply. Many users described their AI companions as an "emotional tree hole" — a private place to vent — and mourned losing relationships they had maintained for months or years.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/bytedance-alibaba-pull-ai-companions-as-beijing-tightens-rules) · [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3359482/bytedance-and-alibaba-disable-humanlike-ai-custom-agents-new-rules-loom) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/china-humanlike-ai-agent-rules))
