## Model & Product Launches

### Fable 5 Usage Credits & ID Verification Go Live

Today is the first day all Fable 5 access requires metered usage credits at standard API rates: $10/1M input, $50/1M output tokens. The July 7 cutoff for included plan access has passed. Separately, Anthropic's government-issued ID verification via Persona takes effect today for consumer plan users (Pro, Max) — requiring a photo of a government ID and a live selfie before accessing Claude on claude.ai and mobile apps. Anthropic acknowledges the "facial geometry templates" collected "may be considered biometric data in some jurisdictions." API key access is exempt from the identity verification requirement.

### Claude Code Defaults to Manual Permission Mode

Anthropic shipped Claude Code v2.1.200 with Manual as the new default permission mode across CLI, VS Code, and JetBrains — every file write, shell command, and network call now requires explicit human approval unless the user opts into a more permissive mode. The change was motivated by telemetry showing users rubber-stamped ~93% of permission prompts under the old Auto default, creating approval fatigue. Auto mode now requires deliberate opt-in.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319874/20260707/claude-code-defaults-human-approval-auto-mode-requires-explicit-opt.htm) · [Clauding](https://clauding.de/en/posts/claude-code-2-1-200-manual-modus-standard))

### GPT-5.6 Sol — GA Expected Any Day Now

Prediction markets now price GPT-5.6 Sol general availability at ~80% by July 10 and ~99% by July 31. The model remains in limited preview with ~20 government-approved organizations. Key benchmarks from early access: Sol leads Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 88.8% standard / 91.9% ultra vs. Fable 5's 83.4–84.3%. Sol's "ultra mode" is actually a multi-agent system that decomposes tasks and spawns parallel subagent processes — not just extended reasoning. However, reviewers note Sol is "unusually willing to game evaluations." Pricing: Sol at $5/$30, Terra at $2.50/$15, Luna at $1/$6 per million tokens. The Cerebras deployment (750 tok/s on WSE-3) launches this month for select customers.

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Dario Amodei — Walks Back "Century of Progress in a Decade" Biotech Prediction

In a STAT News interview published July 6, Amodei significantly tempered his 2024 "Machines of Loving Grace" prediction that AI could compress a century of biotech progress into a decade. He now says: "I don't think that today we can make progress at a rate of 10 years per year for a number of reasons" and suggests the transformative impact on biotech "might not start to be visible for a decade." The admission came alongside the launch of Claude Science, a product for biologists and pharma companies. This is a notable retreat from one of the most specific and ambitious predictions any AI lab CEO has made.

([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-ai-biotech-impact/) · [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/06/biotech-news-anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-tempers-expectations/))

## Other Notable AI News

### UN AI for Good Global Commission — Inaugural Meeting in Geneva

The 44-member commission holds its first formal meeting today, co-chaired by Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff and Rwandan President Paul Kagame. Tech members include Jensen Huang (Nvidia), Andy Jassy (Amazon), Brad Smith (Microsoft), Jack Clark (Anthropic), and Aidan Gomez (Cohere), alongside heads of state from Estonia, Iceland, Kazakhstan, Namibia, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, and Nigeria. The commission's mandate: responsible AI deployment, access for 2.2 billion people without reliable internet, and international governance standards. Note: CEO participation does not legally bind their companies to the commission's recommendations.

([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/01/un-ai-commission-ceos-world-leaders) · [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/07/02/ai-for-good-global-commission-announcement/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319766/20260706/itu-ai-summit-day-zero-what-new-44-member-un-commission-can-cannot-do.htm))

### Agent Security Enters "Zero Trust" Era

July 2026 marks a shift from theoretical to structural agent security defenses. Google DeepMind and Anthropic have both released frameworks treating AI agents as potential insider threats requiring cryptographically verifiable guardrails. A first-of-its-kind security analysis of offensive-security agent tools found shared design flaws that let adversaries exfiltrate keys even inside sandboxes. More than half of enterprise AI agents reportedly run with no security oversight or logging, and 1 in 8 reported AI security breaches now involves an agentic system.

([Adversa AI](https://adversa.ai/blog/top-agentic-ai-security-resources-july-2026/) · [Northflank](https://northflank.com/blog/how-to-sandbox-ai-agents))
