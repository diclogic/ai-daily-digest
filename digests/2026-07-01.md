# AI Daily Digest — July 1, 2026

**Top story:** Claude Fable 5 is back. The Department of Commerce lifted export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on June 30, and Anthropic begins restoring global access today — ending a 19-day suspension that turned a jailbreak report into the first real test of government-gated AI model access.

---

## Model & Product Launches

### Fable 5 Returns — Export Controls Lifted, Access Restoring Today

The Department of Commerce has lifted the export controls imposed on Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 12. Anthropic is restoring access starting today (July 1) across Claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and the API. Cloud provider access via AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry will follow as quickly as possible.

The suspension was triggered when Amazon researchers demonstrated a jailbreak that prompted Fable 5 to identify and exploit software vulnerabilities. Anthropic's internal review found the technique was "relatively simple, could be achieved with other models, and did not demonstrate a flaw in Fable 5's safety systems." Anthropic implemented a new safeguard that blocks the specific jailbreak 93% of the time, with the remaining 7% of cases producing only previously discovered or already-patched flaws. The company also established a new industry jailbreak framework with Amazon, Microsoft, and Google for consistently assessing and fixing jailbreaks across models.

For Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans, Fable 5 will be included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits through July 7, after which it moves to usage credits. API pricing: $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, with 90% prompt caching discount.

([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) · [Anthropic/X](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2072106151890809341) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/1/us-lifts-restrictions-on-powerful-ai-models-fable-mythos-anthropic-says) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/trump-administration-lifts-claude-mythos-5-fable-5-export-restrictions-after-anthropic-works-government))

### Mythos 5 — Broader Access Now, Still Restricted to ~100 Organizations

With the export control lift, Mythos 5 access expands but remains restricted. The model — Anthropic's most capable, particularly in cybersecurity — is available to roughly 100 vetted US critical-infrastructure organizations through Project Glasswing, plus their foreign-national staff and US government agencies. This represents the first working instance of a government clearance process for frontier AI models: model access as a licensed privilege granted case by case.

([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319213/20260628/claude-fable-5-still-offline-us-clears-mythos-5-critical-infrastructure.htm) · [CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/anthropic-claude-mythos-5/))

### GPT-5.6 Sol on Cerebras — 750 tok/s Coming This Month

OpenAI confirmed GPT-5.6 Sol will launch on Cerebras hardware in July at up to 750 tokens per second — a significant speed milestone for frontier-grade inference. Access will initially be limited to select enterprise customers as capacity scales. The broader GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna) remains in limited preview with ~20 government-approved partners. Sam Altman's internal guidance still points to mid-July (~July 10–17) general availability. Pricing: $5/$30 (Sol), $2.50/$15 (Terra), $1/$6 (Luna) per million input/output tokens.

([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) · [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/openai-to-launch-gpt-5-6-sol-on-cerebras-hardware-in-july) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319172/20260626/openai-cerebras-bet-spawns-jalapeno-chip-gpt-56-faces-government-gate.htm))

### Gemini 3.5 Pro — Still Targeting July, No Specific Date

Google's flagship model remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview. Multiple reports say it is "cleared for July launch," but Google has not confirmed a specific date. The company continues refining coding performance, token efficiency, and long-task handling based on enterprise tester feedback. The 2-million-token context window will be the largest in any production frontier model.

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Andrej Karpathy — "Growing Gap in Understanding of AI Capability"

Karpathy's most recent public post (late June) argues that there is a "growing gap in understanding of AI capability" driven by two factors: recency (people forming views from older, weaker models like the free ChatGPT tier) and tier of use (the gap between free-tier chatbot experiences and frontier models running agentic workflows in tools like Codex). He warns that people who tried ChatGPT's free tier "last year" and extrapolated are now severely underestimating what current frontier models can do. Separately, his expanded 10-rule CLAUDE.md file continues spreading through the developer community, with reported error rate reductions from 41% to 11% in agentic coding loops.

([X/@karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319214/20260628/karpathy-claudemd-grows-ten-rules-new-self-check-protocol-ai-coding-loops.htm))

### Dario Amodei — Vindicated but Quiet

Amodei's "Policy on the AI Exponential" essay — which called for mandatory third-party testing and government power to block unsafe models, published one day after Fable 5's launch — was widely criticized as regulatory overreach that invited the very government action that suspended his own models. With export controls now lifted, Amodei's position looks partially vindicated: the government did test the process, found the jailbreak concern overblown, and reversed course. No new public statements from Amodei.

### Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, Ilya Sutskever, Yann LeCun — No New Statements

All four remain quiet. Altman is focused on the GPT-5.6 general availability push. Hassabis is working to ship Gemini 3.5 Pro. Sutskever's SSI remains at $32B valuation with ~20 researchers and no public output. LeCun's April critique of Amodei's job displacement predictions ("He knows absolutely nothing about the effects of technological revolutions on the labor market") continues circulating on Hacker News but there are no new statements.

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump AI Executive Order — July 2 Deadline Tomorrow

The June 2 Executive Order's first 30-day deadline arrives tomorrow (July 2). Federal agencies must: prioritize AI-enabled cyber defense for national security systems, release binding operational directives for civilian IT, and form an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" with industry for coordinated vulnerability scanning and remediation. The 60-day deadline (August 1) requires a voluntary framework for pre-release federal access to frontier models — the same mechanism now governing GPT-5.6, Fable 5, and Mythos 5 releases.

([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/president-trump-signs-executive-order-establishing-ai-cybersecurity-and-frontier-model-framework))

### SpaceX–Reflection AI Compute Deal — $150M/Month Starting Today

SpaceX's compute lease agreement with Reflection AI, the open-source AI startup co-founded by former DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou (co-creator of AlphaGo), begins today at $150 million per month. Total payments could reach ~$6.3 billion if the contract runs through 2029. This is separate from SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere (Cursor), which remains on track for Q3 close.

### Tech Markets Rebound — Nasdaq Up 1.52%

After ending every session since June 18 in the red and falling more than 6% from its June 2 all-time high, the Nasdaq rallied 1.52% on July 1 — its first green day in nearly two weeks. The S&P 500 gained 0.52% and the Dow added 0.27%. Analysts noted the rally was selective, driven by tech and growth stocks, possibly on optimism from Fable 5's return and the approaching GPT-5.6 general availability.
