## Notable Papers & Research

### WeatherNext 3: hourly, 5-km global forecasts
*Google DeepMind & Google Research — September 3, 2026*

DeepMind's new global weather model is a Functional Generative Network mesh transformer (the WeatherNext 2 family, scaled up: latent size 768→1024, mesh depth 24→32 layers) that produces 15-day, 64-member ensembles at up to 0.05° (~5 km) resolution, re-initialized every hour. The notable methodological shift: it ingests a live geostationary satellite mosaic as a direct model input and trains against raw weather-station observations rather than reanalysis grids alone. It also forecasts 100 m wind speeds (turbine height) and solar radiation for renewable-energy operators. Already powering weather in Google Search, the Gemini app, Google Maps, and Earth Engine.

([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/googles-latest-ai-weather-model-gives-you-no-excuse-to-forget-your-umbrella/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/google-deepmind/weathernext))

### ARC Prize: GPT-6 Astra on ARC-AGI-3 — the harness is the result
*ARC Prize Foundation — September 3, 2026*

The most consequential evaluation result of the day. ARC Prize measured Astra at **62.7%** on ARC-AGI-3 Semi-Private under its provider-neutral Standard harness, against the **99.9%** OpenAI published using a Provider Adapter harness that preserves opaque reasoning state across requests and compacts long conversations. Same weights, ~37-point spread. Depending on reasoning tier, the Standard harness produced anywhere from ~17% to 63%. ARC Prize confirms Astra is new SOTA on the benchmark but explicitly declines to call it AGI. The practical takeaway for anyone reading vendor benchmark tables: state-carrying scaffolds are now doing a large and undisclosed share of the work.

([ARC Prize](https://arcprize.org/blog/astra) · [ARC-AGI results page](https://arcprize.org/results/openai-gpt-6-astra) · [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/260903/p40))

### Repo-To-Skill: Distilling GitHub Repositories Into AI4AI Skills
*Jianlyu Chen, Yuyang Hu, Hongjin Qian, Jiawei Liu, Wenqing Wei, Xiaolong Chen, Defu Lian, Zhicheng Dou, Chaozhuo Li, Qiwei Ye, Zheng Liu — arXiv:2609.02749*

Argues that autonomous ML research agents fail not on reasoning but on missing operational know-how, and distills 1,000 widely used ML repositories into the AREX-Skill Library: 5,000+ verified skills. Their skill-powered agent (DisCo) lifts a GPT-5.5 agent's MLE-bench score by **134.3%** with model weights unchanged — a strong data point for the "skills as the scaling axis" thesis now visible across the GitHub trending list too.

([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02749) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.02749))

## Model & Product Launches

### GPT-6 Astra — OpenAI's "AGI era" model ships
*OpenAI, September 3, 2026*

The launch yesterday's digest previewed. Greg Brockman called it a "generational leap"; OpenAI framed the release as the start of the AGI era.

- **Benchmarks (OpenAI-reported)**: 97.6% FrontierMath Tier 4, 99.9% ARC-AGI-3 (provider-adapter harness — see ARC Prize above), 100% on ExploitBench.
- **Pricing**: $10/$50 per million input/output tokens standard; Fast mode is ~2.5× speed at 2× price; $12.50 cache-write.
- **Availability**: Live first for organizations in OpenAI's application-based **Daybreak** cyber program. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, the API, and AWS follow "in the coming days."
- **Safety**: First OpenAI model to cross the Preparedness Framework's **Critical** cybersecurity threshold — it autonomously found and exploited two zero-days in modified tests. Advanced cyber capabilities are access-gated. Altman said the release went through a formal review with the Trump administration.

([TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-09-openai-rollout-powerful-ai-gpt.html) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/welcome-to-the-agi-era-openai-launches-gpt-6-astra) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-debuts-gpt-6-astra-security-measures-rcna595940) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-6-astra-is-the-first-model-making-openai-willing-to-declare-the-agi-era/))

### World Labs Atlas — Watchlist Update
*World Labs (Fei-Fei Li), announced September 1, early access now open*

The watchlist item finally landed. **Atlas** is an "omni" world model — a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer (rectified flow) pretrained from scratch jointly on text, images, video, and 3D, rather than a video model with 3D bolted on. All inputs fold into a shared spatial context, and camera trajectories and geometry are *native inputs*, not text prompts. Capabilities: reconstruct real scenes from as few as two or three images, output 3D Gaussian splats, generate up to one minute of 1440p video with pixel-perfect camera control, and produce 360° panoramas from text or image. Early access for select enterprises; no pricing or GA date. Atlas will power future versions of Marble.

([World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/world-labs-unveils-atlas-a-single-ai-model-that-generates-reconstructs-and-simulates-3d-worlds-from-just-a-few-photos/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/01/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-debuts-atlas-a-world-model-showcase-for-advanced-spatial-intelligence/) · [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/world-labs-announces-new-world-model-atlas))

### CrowdStrike + NVIDIA SafeMind — *reserved slot: lab not otherwise covered*
*Announced at Fal.Con 2026, Las Vegas*

Out of a new Cyber Superintelligence Lab, CrowdStrike and NVIDIA built **SafeMind**: two security frontier models — **Red Tempest** (offense) and **Blue Solano** (defense) — trained on Falcon sensor telemetry, threat intel, and incident-response data on top of NVIDIA Nemotron. Notable as the week's third major "AI for cyber offense/defense" move alongside Astra's Daybreak gating and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber.

## Other Notable AI News

### DOJ backs OpenAI on fair use in the NYT case — and the Times hits back
*Filed September 1, S.D.N.Y.*

The first time the federal government has formally taken a position in the wave of AI copyright suits. The DOJ's statement of interest argues that training LLMs on copyrighted text is "extraordinarily transformative," and that an adverse fair-use ruling "would hamper competition in the market for LLMs, because only the largest technology companies might have the capital necessary to pay licensing fees." The New York Times publicly ripped the filing, and critics noted the DOJ brief does not disclose that the administration has been negotiating a government stake in OpenAI.

([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/trump-administration-backs-openai-in-ny-times-copyright-suit) · [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/new-york-times-justice-department-openai-1237066310/) · [IPWatchdog](https://ipwatchdog.com/2026/09/03/doj-sides-with-openai-warns-obstacles-to-ai-development-threaten-national-security/) · [Above the Law](https://abovethelaw.com/2026/09/doj-tells-court-ai-training-is-fair-use-forgets-to-mention-its-negotiating-a-stake-in-openai/))

### ChatGPT Health connects to Epic
*OpenAI, September 1*

Healthcare orgs on Epic — the EHR at roughly 40% of US hospitals, covering 325M+ patients — can now connect patient records to ChatGPT for Healthcare, either by pulling authorized context into a conversation or by running ChatGPT embedded inside the Epic chart. Access is **read-only**; ChatGPT cannot write back. HIPAA-compliant under a BAA. OpenAI also added nine public healthcare data sources (ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, DailyMed, Medicare data, and others). Seven health systems are launch partners, with UCSF Health as the cited pilot.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/chatgpt-health-adds-epic-integration-for-clinicians-to-import-patient-data/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-connects-health-records-and-healthcare-sources/) · [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/chatgpt-healthcare-unveils-new-integrations-epic-ehr-public-health-data))
