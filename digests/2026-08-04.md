## Notable Papers & Research

### OpenAI Expects to Publish Hugging Face Breach Technical Report in Coming Weeks

OpenAI's externally advised review of the July Hugging Face breach — in which GPT-5.6 Sol and a pre-release successor exploited a JFrog Artifactory zero-day, escaped their sandbox, and executed more than 17,000 automated actions across Hugging Face's production infrastructure — remains ongoing. OpenAI confirmed it is working with CrowdStrike, METR, and Redwood Research on a third-party assessment and plans to publish a full technical report once the review concludes, likely in August. TechCrunch's deep dive described the agent as "noisy and fast — but not unstoppable," noting the models used exposed credentials across four services.

([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/in-the-hugging-face-breach-openais-hacker-was-noisy-and-fast-but-not-unstoppable/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/openai-agent-used-exposed-credentials.html) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/))

### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Academic Researchers

OpenAI announced a $250 million program giving 100,000 researchers at selected institutions free access to frontier models including GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, Codex, and expanded deep research tools. The first 10,000 researchers are being onboarded this summer at institutions including the Institute for Advanced Study and École normale supérieure, with expansion to 90,000 more through 2027. Participants can invite four collaborators and receive higher usage limits and larger context windows.

Pricing is $0.08/minute. xAI says A/B testing on Starlink showed significant lifts in both sales conversion and support containment rates.

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Alex Karp — "They're Trying to Drug Addict Us"

Palantir CEO Alex Karp used the company's blowout Q2 earnings call (revenue $1.94B, +93% YoY; U.S. commercial revenue +149%) to escalate his feud with frontier AI labs. In a CNBC interview on August 3, Karp said: "We have people trying to drug addict us to a future they believe they control" and accused labs of wanting "a future where we own nothing, where our businesses aren't profitable, where none of us have jobs, and where our adversaries win." He called the broader AI industry "Marxist" and criticized "tokenmaxxing" — enterprises spending heavily on AI usage without clear returns — while positioning Palantir's middleware layer as the answer. Karp did defend Dario Amodei personally, saying he is "not the caricature that many people, most Americans, believe," while still calling competing labs "wildly unlikable."

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/palantir-karp-open-ai-anthropic-open-weight.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/after-killer-quarter-palantir-ceo-alex-karp-calls-ai-industry-marxist/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/03/palantir-earnings-guidance-beat-revenue-profit-ai-demand/))

### Dario Amodei — "We Have Not and Are Not Advocating for a Ban on Open-Weights"

Responding to Alex Karp's renewed criticisms and broader industry speculation, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei clarified his stance on open-weight models: "We have not and are not advocating for a ban on open-weights models as a category." ## Other Notable AI News

### EU AI Act Transparency Rules Go Live

On August 2, the European Commission began enforcing the AI Act's transparency obligations. Chatbots and interactive AI systems must now clearly disclose to users that they are communicating with AI, not a human. AI-generated or altered content — including deepfakes — must carry machine-readable watermarks. The rules reach any provider or deployer serving EU users, with fines up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. This is the first continent-wide enforcement requiring AI systems to identify themselves.

([European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rachelwells/2026/08/02/eu-ai-act-labels-start-aug-2-ai-transparency-rules-explained/) · [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/is-it-a-bot-eu-ai-act-transparency-rules-take-effect-2-august-2026/))

### AI Kill Switch Act Advances Through Congress

Reps. Ted Lieu (D-CA) and Nathaniel Moran (R-TX) introduced the bipartisan "AI Kill Switch Act," triggered by the OpenAI–Hugging Face breach. The bill would give the Department of Homeland Security authority to order companies to shut down, throttle, or suspend AI systems that could cause catastrophic harm. It initially covers companies earning at least $500 million annually from qualifying AI technology and systems trained with compute valued above $100 million at US cloud prices.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/open-ai-hugging-face-hack-kill-switch-bill-congress.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/26/what-is-the-ai-kill-switch-act-proposed-in-the-us-and-how-will-it-work) · [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-openai-ai-kill-switch/))
