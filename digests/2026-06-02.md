## Notable Papers & Research

**ICML 2026 Acceptances Announced**
The 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (Seoul, July 6–11) accepted 6,352 papers out of 23,918 submissions (26.6% acceptance rate), with 168 oral presentations (top 0.7%) and 536 spotlight posters (2.2%). Notable accepted work includes:

- **"Adaptive Reasoning in LLMs: From Post-Training to Test-Time Learning"** — Selected as an ICML 2026 tutorial by Akhil Arora and Nouha Dziri. Covers the emerging paradigm of models that improve their reasoning at inference time. [icml.cc](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026)
- **"Distilling LLM Feedback for Lean Theorem Proving"** by Gaetan Narozniak et al. — Accepted at ICML 2026. Uses LLM-generated feedback to improve automated theorem proving in the Lean proof assistant.
- **"COMPASS: Cognitive MCTS-Guided Process Alignment for Safe Search Agents"** by Wenkai Shen et al. — Applies Monte Carlo Tree Search to align AI search agents with safety constraints during multi-step reasoning.
- **"SLAT: Segment-Level Adaptive Trimming for Efficient CoT Reasoning"** by Jian Yao et al. — Proposes a method to reduce the computational cost of chain-of-thought reasoning by adaptively trimming unnecessary segments. [paperdigest.org](https://www.paperdigest.org/2026/05/icml-2026-papers-highlights/)

## Model & Product Launches

### Microsoft Build 2026 — The Agent Era Arrives (June 2–3, San Francisco)

Satya Nadella opened the keynote declaring that AI has moved beyond passive assistants into **autonomous agents that own entire workflows**. Key announcements:

- **Project Polaris** — Microsoft's homegrown mixture-of-experts coding model will replace GPT-4 Turbo as the default in GitHub Copilot starting August 2026. It outperforms GPT-4 Turbo on HumanEval and MBPP, with particular gains in Rust and Haskell. [chatforest.com](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/microsoft-build-2026-recap-windows-agent-platform-project-polaris-copilot-workspace/)
- **Windows Agent Framework** open-sourced, plus Azure Agent Mesh and a new Windows Agent Store for distributing autonomous agents through the OS.
- **Computer-Using Agents (CUAs)** now support OpenAI's CUA and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 for navigating websites and desktop apps visually, with enterprise governance including key vault credentials and session replay auditing.
- **Foundry Local GA** — Microsoft's local AI inference runtime is now production-ready on Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon), and Linux x64, enabling fully offline AI with no per-token costs. [devblogs.microsoft.com](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/foundry-local-ga/)
- First Windows PCs powered by **Nvidia RTX Spark** chips officially launched.

### Gemini 3.5 Flash — Now Generally Available

Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash reached full GA, offering frontier-level performance at 280 tok/s with 1M-token context. It beats the previous Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic tasks. Pricing: $1.50/$9.00 per million input/output tokens. **Gemini 3.5 Pro** is expected to follow in the coming weeks. [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/)

### xAI Grok V9 Medium Training Complete

xAI has completed training on Grok V9 Medium — a 1.5 trillion parameter model, 3x the scale of the current v8 small. Public release targeted for mid-June. Meanwhile, Grok 4.3 is rolling out to SuperGrok and Premium+ subscribers with an improved architecture. [x.ai](https://x.ai/news)

### Claude Mythos Watch

Claude Mythos Preview remains in limited access through **Project Glasswing**, an invitation-only program for ~40 vetted critical-infrastructure operators. On the 2026 USA Mathematical Olympiad, Mythos scored 97.6% (vs. Opus 4.6's 42.3%). The UK AI Security Institute reported a 73% success rate on expert-level hacking tasks no AI could complete before April 2025. Anthropic says Mythos-class models will come to all customers **"in the coming weeks"** — a timeline first stated in late May. [fortune.com](https://fortune.com/2026/05/29/anthropic-raises-65-billion-at-record-965-billion-valuation-promises-mythos-ai-model-in-wide-release-in-coming-weeks-releases-claude-opus-4-8/) · [bleepingcomputer.com](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-confirms-claude-mythos-class-models-will-roll-out-to-the-public/)
