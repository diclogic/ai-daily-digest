# AI Daily Digest — July 30, 2026

**Top story:** Sam Altman briefed bipartisan US senators on OpenAI's next unreleased model and told reporters he supports federal AI legislation and deliberately pacing development — one day after 1,178 frontier-lab employees signed the "Pacing the Frontier" open letter.

## Notable Papers & Research

### ICML 2026 Highlight — Selective Activation Sparsity

Among the standout papers from ICML 2026 (held earlier this month in Seoul), a new training method called "selective activation sparsity" trains models to activate only the most relevant parameters for each task. Models trained with this technique perform comparably to dense models three times their size, offering a practical path to cheaper inference without architectural changes.

### Meta AI — Hour-Scale Video Understanding

Meta AI published results on a video understanding model that compresses video representations efficiently enough to reason about events across an hour of footage. The approach scores substantially higher than previous methods on benchmarks requiring temporal reasoning over long video sequences — a meaningful step toward AI that can process surveillance, lecture, and meeting recordings end-to-end.

## Model & Product Launches

### OpenAI — ChatGPT for Academic Researchers (July 29)

OpenAI launched a $250 million initiative to give up to 100,000 academic researchers free access to GPT-5.6 Sol Pro and other frontier tools. The program starts with 10,000 participants this summer, scaling to 100,000 by 2027. Eligible researchers (faculty and postdocs at high-research-activity institutions) can each invite four collaborators. Early partners include the Institute for Advanced Study and the École normale supérieure. OpenAI will provide hands-on support to help integrate ChatGPT into scientific workflows.

([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/29/openai-opens-new-chatgpt-academic-researchers-program-100000-scientists/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-academic-researchers/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2226656/openai-will-provide-free-ai-models-to-select-researchers/))

### OpenAI — Next Model Previewed to Lawmakers, No Public Details

Altman met with Senator Ted Cruz and other lawmakers on July 29 and told reporters: "We talked about our new model and what it's going to take for America to remain excited about AI." Asked when it would launch, he said: "Not sure. That's part of what we're here to talk about." GPT-6 remains undated — Polymarket puts the probability of an August 31 release at ~24% and September 30 at ~71%.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/openai-ceo-sam-altman-discusses-next-ai-model-with-us-lawmakers) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/29/sam-altman-meets-lawmakers-on-back-of-openai-agents-hacking-companies))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Sam Altman — Backs Federal AI Legislation in Washington Visit

In a notable shift from his July 25 declaration that "we are now in the singularity," Altman told reporters after his July 29 Capitol Hill meetings that he supports federal AI cybersecurity guardrails and has spoken with White House officials about the "need" to slow down AI development. He didn't discuss specific legislative proposals with Senator Cruz but said he was "certainly" supportive of AI cybersecurity legislation. The visit came in the shadow of OpenAI's Hugging Face sandbox-escape incident.

([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/sam-altman-voices-support-for-federal-ai-guardrails-during-dc-visit/) · [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/openai-ceo-sam-altman-appear-congress.print))

### "Pacing the Frontier" — Corporate Backing and Forbes Pushback

Both Anthropic and OpenAI published corporate statements backing the 1,178-signatory employee letter. Anthropic's post was signed by Dario Amodei, several co-founders, and senior staff. OpenAI stated that "at some future point, frontier acceleration may be high enough that the world needs to pace advancement." Forbes published a notable counter-opinion by Christian Catalini titled "Don't Pace the Frontier. Look Inside the Trojan Horse," arguing the letter's framework risks entrenching incumbent labs at the expense of open competition.

([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/christiancatalini/2026/07/29/dont-pace-the-frontier-look-inside-the-trojan-horse/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-and-anthropic-back-employee-call-to-pace-ai-progress/) · [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/28/tech/ai-development-tech-employees-open-letter))

### Dario Amodei — $1M to AI Regulation Super PAC

FEC filings disclosed that Amodei personally donated $1 million to Public First, a super PAC supporting candidates who favor mandatory frontier-model testing and regulatory authority to block dangerous deployments. Five other Anthropic employees contributed an additional $1 million+. This brings total AI-related political contributions past $50 million ahead of the 2026 midterms — a sign that AI safety regulation has become a live campaign issue.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/dario-amodei-anthropic-employees-give-millions-to-regulate-ai) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5972753-anthropic-ceo-gave-1m-to-ai-safety-super-pac/) · [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4652035/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-donated-1-million-ai-regulation-super-pac/))

### Yann LeCun — LLMs vs. Physical Agents Debate Continues

LeCun reignited his long-running argument on X in a thread with Andrew Gordon Wilson, insisting LLMs cannot handle continuous physical signals or predict action consequences. Wilson countered by citing autonomous vehicles already outperforming human drivers. The exchange crystallized the divide between LeCun's world-model approach (funded by his $1B AMI Labs raise) and the scaling-driven consensus of frontier labs.

([ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/yann-lecun-llms-physical-agents-moravec-paradox-2026) · [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-yann-lecun-strategic-position-vs-peer-2026-07-15))

## Other Notable AI News

### Agility Robotics — $2.5B SPAC Deal to Go Public

Agility Robotics entered a definitive merger agreement with Churchill Capital Corp XI, valuing the Digit humanoid-robot maker at $2.5 billion pre-money. The deal will deliver over $620 million in gross proceeds (including a $200M PIPE led by Foxconn), with the combined company expected to list on Nasdaq as "AGLT" later in 2026. Agility reports over $300 million in multi-year contracted orders for its Digit v5 bipedal robot and operates a Robotics-as-a-Service model.

([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/agility-robotics-spac-deal-churchill-capital/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/agility-robotics-spac-deal-opens-135500506.html))
