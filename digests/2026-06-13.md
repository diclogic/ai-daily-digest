## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 — Pulled by US Export Control (June 12-13)

At 5:21 PM ET on June 12 — just three days after Fable 5's general availability launch — Anthropic received a US government directive and shut down all access to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 worldwide. The order originated from Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick in a letter dated June 1 to Dario Amodei, mandating that any export, re-export, or domestic transfer of these models to non-US persons requires prior governmental approval. Because Anthropic cannot reliably identify which users are foreign nationals in real time, the practical result is a hard shutoff for everyone — US and international users alike. Anthropic disputes the rationale, calling the demonstrated jailbreak technique "narrow" and "non-universal," noting it surfaces "previously known, minor vulnerabilities" discoverable by other public models. Anthropic says it is working to restore access. This is the first time a frontier model has been pulled mid-deployment by export control action. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/anthropic-says-us-limits-foreign-access-to-fable-5-mythos-5) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-disables-access-to-fable-5-and-mythos-5-to-comply-with-government-directive.html) | [Quartz](https://qz.com/anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-export-control-directive-061226))
