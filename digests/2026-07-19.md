## Model & Product Launches

Today (July 19) is the final day of Anthropic's free Fable 5 promotional access on Pro, Max, Team, and eligible Enterprise plans. ## Notable Papers & Research

### ResearcherBench: Evaluating Deep AI Research Systems (GAIR-NLP)

A new benchmark from GAIR-NLP evaluates whether AI systems can act as genuine research partners on frontier scientific questions. Contains 65 expert-curated questions across 34 AI research subjects with responses from 11 AI systems. Introduces a dual evaluation framework (rubric + factual assessment) — shifting the bar from "can AI summarize papers?" to "can AI provide meaningful scientific insights?"

([arXiv 2507.16280](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.16280) · [GitHub](https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/ResearcherBench))

### DeepMind: Prospective Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Tasks

Google DeepMind published work on "prospective credit assignment" — a method teaching models to anticipate how current decisions affect outcomes many steps ahead. On SWE-Bench, the approach shows meaningful improvement for issues requiring 10+ steps to resolve. This addresses one of the key bottlenecks for reliable AI agents: compounding errors in long task chains.

([Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026))

### ICML 2026: Selective Activation Sparsity

Multiple papers at ICML 2026 (Seoul, July 6–11) advanced model efficiency through activation sparsity — training models to use only the most relevant parameters per task. Training-free methods now achieve 50% sparsity in MLP blocks. Key insight: more training data, ReLU activation, and smaller width-depth ratios all promote sparsity naturally.

([arXiv 2509.00454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00454) · [ICLR 2026](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=I9M5OoOBnI))

## Key Opinions & Statements

### Demis Hassabis — Lobbying Washington for AI Watchdog

Hassabis met with Washington officials this week to push his proposal for a FINRA-style AI standards body, published July 14 as "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age." The proposal: frontier labs voluntarily submit models up to 30 days before release for safety testing by a majority-independent board of Turing Award winners and other experts. If testing proves robust, mandatory compliance "could quickly follow." Timeline: operational "before year-end." Both Altman and Musk called the proposal "thoughtful."

([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/deepmind-ceo-to-lobby-washington-on-plan-for-group-to-vet-ai-models) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepmind-ceo-calls-for-an-independent-standards-body-to-regulate-frontier-ai/))

### Dario Amodei — $1M to AI Regulation PAC

FEC filings from this week show Amodei donated $1M to Public First, a super PAC backing candidates who favor mandatory frontier model testing. Five other Anthropic employees contributed $1.26M more. This follows Anthropic's $20M pledge to Public First Action in February. Total AI-related political contributions have crossed $50M ahead of the 2026 midterms.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/dario-amodei-anthropic-employees-give-millions-to-regulate-ai) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5972753-anthropic-ceo-gave-1m-to-ai-safety-super-pac/) · [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4652035/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-donated-1-million-ai-regulation-super-pac/))

### Yann LeCun — "The 'G' in AGI Is Nonsense"

Speaking at RAISE Summit in Paris (July 8–9), LeCun explained why he left Meta to build "world models" at his startup AMI ($1.03B raised at $3.5B pre-money). He also pushed "Tapestry" — a plan for an open foundation model that many countries could train together without sharing data, with only parameters (never raw data) sent to a central server.

### China Launches WAICO — A Rival AI Governance Bloc

Twenty-nine countries signed an agreement on July 16 establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), an intergovernmental body headquartered in Shanghai. Members include Russia, Brazil, Pakistan, Indonesia, Cuba, Venezuela, Belarus, Serbia, and Kazakhstan. No major Western democracy signed. UN Secretary-General Guterres attended the ceremony. The world now has two competing institutional frameworks for AI governance.
