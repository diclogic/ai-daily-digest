## Model & Product Launches

### Claude Mythos Expands to 150 Organizations via Project Glasswing

Anthropic scaled its restricted Claude Mythos model to approximately 150 new organizations across 15+ countries through Project Glasswing, its joint initiative to find and fix critical software vulnerabilities. New partners include NATO, the EU's cybersecurity agency ENISA, Okta, Samsung, SK Hynix, and SK Telecom — spanning power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware. Since its April launch, Mythos has surfaced over 23,000 potential vulnerabilities, with an estimated 6,000+ confirmed as high or critical severity. Anthropic also embedded engineers inside the NSA for offensive cyber operations. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/) | [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-expanding-mythos-access-to-150-new-organizations/))

### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT "Dreaming V3" Memory

OpenAI's most significant memory upgrade since ChatGPT's launch began rolling out to Plus and Pro users on June 4. Dreaming V3 consolidates memories during idle periods — analogous to how the human brain processes events during sleep — with an asynchronous background process that synthesizes memory across conversations. OpenAI reports factual recall jumping from 41.5% (2024) to 82.8% on internal evals, with a 5x reduction in compute cost that makes free-tier rollout practical. ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/06/05/openai-chatgpt-dreaming-v3-memory/) | [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/05/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-memory-with-new-dreaming-architecture-for-plus-and-pro-users/))

## Other Notable AI News

### Trump Signs AI Executive Order

President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" on June 2, establishing a voluntary framework for AI companies to submit frontier models for government testing up to 30 days before public release. The order also directs agencies to develop cyber-capability benchmarks and create an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse." Notably, the order expressly prohibits mandatory licensing or pre-clearance — down from an earlier draft's 90-day review window.
